## Explicit question answer states

The publication model SHALL separate review status from answer semantics. A migration review note may carry `reviewStatus: needs_review` without changing its physician-authored Markdown.

Question frontmatter and the emitted `sr` projection use these fields:

- `answerStatus: confirmed` means the accepted answer set is clinically confirmed.
- `answerStatus: unresolved` means the answer is still under review; it is visible but not scorable.
- `answerStatus: no_valid_answer` means the question is confirmed to have no acceptable option; it is visible but not scorable.
- `answerMode: single` accepts exactly one answer, `any` accepts any one member of the accepted set, `all` requires the complete set, and `none` disables scoring.
- `acceptedAnswers` is a normalized array of option letters. It SHALL be empty for `unresolved`, `no_valid_answer`, and `none`.

The current 51-note migration queue receives `reviewStatus: needs_review`. Only notes with unresolved or conflicting answer semantics receive the non-scorable answer fields. `2017-010` is represented as `answerStatus: confirmed`, `answerMode: any`, `acceptedAnswers: [B, D]`; `2018-360` is represented as `answerStatus: unresolved`, `answerMode: none`, `acceptedAnswers: []`.

The compiler may normalize legacy scalar `correctAnswer` and `Ans:` values during migration, but SHALL NOT infer whether a multi-letter answer means `any` or `all`. That mode must be explicit.
