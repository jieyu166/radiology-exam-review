#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which feature is a described characteristic of advanced adenomas?
(A) They are smaller than 10 mm
(B) They do not exhibit dysplasia
(C) They have a substantial villous component
(D) They have a flat morphology
??
**Ans: C**
【2021 官方詳解】
Advanced adenomas—that is, those at high risk for progression to cancer, are 10 mm or larger (any histologic subtype), have a substantial villous component (>25% villous, including tubulovillous and villous adenomas), and/or exhibit high-grade dysplasia
