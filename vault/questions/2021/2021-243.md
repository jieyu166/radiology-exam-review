#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which feature is a described characteristic of advanced adenomas?
(A) They are smaller than 10 mm
(B) They do not exhibit dysplasia
(C) They have a substantial villous component
(D) They have a flat morphology
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。Advanced adenoma（高惡變風險腺瘤）定義為 ≥10 mm（任何組織型）、或含明顯 villous 成分（>25%，含 tubulovillous 與 villous）、或具高度異型增生；C「有明顯 villous 成分」符合定義，故正確。A 錯：應為 ≥10 mm 而非 <10 mm；B 錯：advanced adenoma 可有異型增生，甚至以高度異型增生為定義要件之一；D 錯：並非以 flat 型態定義，flat（carpet）僅是其中一種形態。→ [[gi-villous-lesions]]
