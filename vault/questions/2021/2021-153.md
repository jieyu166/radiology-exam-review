#交換 #2021交換 #PED Which description about features of coarctation is incorrect?
(A) berry aneurysms are a recognized complication
(B) the isthmus of the aorta is the usual site
(C) a lateral chest X-ray may show dilated internal mammary arteries
(D) the post-ductal type presents in infancy
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
Infantile (pre-ductal) form: just distal to brachiocephalic artery proximal to dectus arteriosus, the blood supple to the descending aorta is via the PDA.
Adult (juxtaductal, postductal, middle aortic) form: distal to ligamentum arteriosum
Aortic isthmus = distal to the origin of the left subclavian artery at the site of the ductus arteriosus.
Plain film:
- Figure of 3 sign: contour abnormality of aorta
- Scalloped contouring of soft tissue posterior to sternum (=dilated tortuous internal mammary arteries) on lateral X ray
- Inferior rib notching: Roesler sign (usually the 4th to 8th ribs)
Complication:
Neonatal herat failure, SAH from ruptured berry aneurysm, aortic dissection, infective endocarditis, mycotic aneurysm
