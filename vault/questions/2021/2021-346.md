#交換 #2021交換 #CV Which of the following is **true** about transcatheter mitral valve replacement?
(A) The size of mitral orifice area of < 1 cm2 is a favorable anatomic character
(B) Rheumatic mitral disease is a contraindication of the procedure
(C) The mobile leaflet length of > 1 cm in a contraindication of the procedure
(D) Left ventricle outflow tract (LVOT) obstruction is a minor complication of the procedure and is defined as a LVOT gradient increase of 50 mmHg or more
??
**Ans: B**
答案 B。依 RadioGraphics 的 MitraClip Table 1，風濕性二尖瓣疾病與瓣葉鈣化屬禁忌，故 B 正確。A 錯：有利解剖為二尖瓣口面積 >4 cm²（非 <1 cm²）。C 錯：可動瓣葉長 >1 cm 是「有利」解剖，非禁忌。D 錯：LVOT 阻塞是 TMVR 最嚴重的「重大」併發症（非次要），定義為術後 LVOT 壓差上升 ≥10 mmHg（非 50 mmHg）。（⚠️ 本題含影像，官方詳解 PDF 第 362 頁，影像尚未匯入，判讀請對照原檔。）→ [[transcatheter-mitral-valve-replacement]]

**TEACHING POINTS**
- CT can help provide information on the morphology and function of the mitral valve that is complementary to findings at echocardiography. CT adds excellent incremental value to performing echocardiography alone in guiding device sizing and procedural planning.
- Owing to the difficulty in reconstructing the true complex shape of the mitral annulus, a simplified D-shaped annulus is more reproducible for device sizing.
- CT is used to estimate the size of the anticipated neo-LVOT, thus allowing prediction of potential obstruction. CT can simulate a virtual prosthesis, a 3D model that matches the shape and dimensions of a specific TMVR prosthesis.
- The risk of neo-LVOT obstruction is higher with greater flaring, protrusion, and skirt size of the device.
- LVOT obstruction is the most dreaded complication of TMVR, defined as a postprocedural LVOT gradient increase of 10 mm Hg or more.

**Table 1: Favorable Anatomy and Contraindications for Transcatheter Mitral Valve Repair with MitraClip**

Favorable anatomy:
- Pathologic condition at A2–P2 cusps
- Coaptation depth, <1 mm
- Coaptation length, >2 mm
- Flail gap, <10 mm
- Flail width, <15 mm
- Mitral orifice area, >4 cm²
- Mobile leaflet length, >1 cm

Contraindications:
- Rheumatic mitral disease
- Mitral leaflet calcifications
