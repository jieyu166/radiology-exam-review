#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which of the following statement about the Dandy-Walker spectrum is **WRONG**?
(A) Classic Dandy-Walker Malformation has cystic dilatation of the fourth ventricle, high-inserting venous confluence, cerebellar hypoplasia and large posterior fossa
(B) Hypoplastic Rotated Vermis has normal size of posterior fossa and "Keyhole" opening of fourth ventricle
(C) Mega Cisterna Magna has enlarged mega cisterna magna which doesn’t communicate with the fourth ventricle, results in hydrocephalus
(D) Blake Pouch Cyst is caused by lack of fenestration of the Blake pouch, resulting in absence of communication between the fourth ventricle and the subarachnoid space and leading to hydrocephalus
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。Mega cisterna magna（大枕大池）是良性變異，與第四腦室「自由相通」且「不」造成水腦；選項 C 稱其「不相通、導致水腦」為錯誤敘述。(A) 正確：典型 Dandy-Walker malformation 有第四腦室囊狀擴大、竇匯上抬、小腦（蚓部）發育不全與後顱窩擴大。(B) 正確：旋轉發育不全之蚓部型後顱窩大小正常、第四腦室呈鑰匙孔（keyhole）開口。(D) 正確：Blake pouch cyst 因 Blake pouch 開窗失敗，第四腦室與蜘蛛膜下腔不通而導致水腦。→ [[posterior-fossa-malformations]]
