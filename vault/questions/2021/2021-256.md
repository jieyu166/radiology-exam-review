#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Which one of the following statements concerning soft tissue masses of the hand and wrist is **false**?
(A) Lipomas are relatively uncommon in the hand. Typical locations are the thenar or hypothenar eminence
(B) Sonography can differentiate between a cyst and a solid mass, as well as identifying internal blood flow of soft tissue masses
(C) Epidermal inclusion cysts are the third most common soft tissue mass of the hand. Calcifications are extremely common found in epidermal inclusion cysts
(D) Aneurysms may be mistaken for a solid mass on MRI. Color Doppler, duplex evaluation, CT angiography, and conventional angiography are useful diagnostic aids
(E) MRI is the best imaging modality to characterize these masses
??
**Ans: C**
【2021 官方詳解】
Epidermal inclusion cysts (EICs) are the third most common soft tissue mass of the hand and often result when penetrating trauma deposits keratin-producing epithelial cells into the subdermal tissues. Consequently, they are found most commonly on the tactile surfaces of the digits. T1 and T2 signal on MRI can be variable, depending on their proteinaceous content. Calcifications are uncommon and are more suggestive of pilomatricoma (ie, a benign solitary hair follicle mass).
