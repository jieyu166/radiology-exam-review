#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Choose the correct option:
(A) The intrinsic high T2 signal of cartilaginous precursors most commonly seen in calcaneous and lateral malleoli in pediatric ankles
(B) The preossification centers of ankles most commonly seen in calcaneal apophysis, navicular cartilaginous precursors and lateral malleolus
(C) Existence of intense enhancement on post-contrast imaging of MR from preossification center
(D) Preossification center may have fluid like content and mimics a cyst
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。此題選「正確」敘述。D 正確：骨化前中心（preossification center）在 MRI 可呈邊界清楚、圓形、類囊液的高訊號，酷似囊腫或膿瘍，關鍵鑑別在於周邊無骨髓/軟組織水腫。A 錯：軟骨前身的正常（有時相當強）T2/STIR 高訊號好發於內踝與跟骨，而非「跟骨加外踝」；B 錯：骨化前中心好發於內踝、跟骨骨突、navicular 軟骨前身，而非外踝；C 錯：軟骨前身/骨化前中心並非典型呈強烈對比增強（官方原標「????」，非正解）。
