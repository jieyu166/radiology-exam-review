#交換 #2021交換 #NR Warthin tumor of salivary gland is associated with all of the following choices **EXCEPT**?
(A) The most common benign neoplasms of major salivary glands.
(B) The relationship between smoking and development of Warthin tumors has been proved.
(C) At US, Warthin tumors are oval, hypoechoic, well-defined tumors and often contain multiple anechoic areas.
(D) Warthin tumors may also appear in the form of a simple cyst simple cyst at US and thus require differentiation from other cystic neoplasms.
(E) 10-60% of cases, tumors may occur bilaterally or multifocally.
??
**Ans: A**
答案 A。本題問 Warthin tumor「除何者以外」皆相關，A 稱它是主要唾液腺最常見的良性腫瘤為錯——最常見的良性唾液腺腫瘤是多形性腺瘤（pleomorphic adenoma／mixed tumor），Warthin 居次，故 A 不適用於 Warthin。B 正確：Warthin 與吸菸有明確相關。C 正確：超音波下典型為卵圓形、低回音、邊界清楚，且常含多個無回音區。D 正確：也可呈單純囊腫樣，需與其他囊性腫瘤鑑別。E 正確：約 10–60% 可雙側或多發，為 Warthin 的特色。
