【2021 官方詳解】
C: hard mass
Diabetic mastopathy
- Benign tumor like breast masses in women
- Long-standing type 1 or type 2 insulin-dependent DM.
- Large, painless, hard breast mass ,clinically indistinguishable from breast cancer
- Pathology, lymphocytic mastitis and stromal fibrosis
- Multicentric or bilateral involvement is a relatively frequent phenomenon, often occurring in late stages of
the disease.
US:
Irregular hypoechoic masses with marked posterior acoustic shadowing.
Mammography:
ill-defined masses or asymmetric densities.
Such lesions are often masked by dense glandular tissue, making mammographic evaluation difficult.
