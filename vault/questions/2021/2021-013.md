#交換 #2021交換 #Breast Which of the following statement about diabetic mastopathy of the breast is **incorrect**?
(A) Diabetic mastopathy is an uncommon benign breast disease, characterized by lymphocytic mastitis and stromal fibrosis.
(B) It typically occurs in patients with long-standing type 1 diabetes.
(C) Ultrasound showed a hypoechoic soft lesion.
(D) MRI shows an ill-defined mass with contrast enhancement.
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。本題問糖尿病乳腺病變（diabetic mastopathy）敘述何者「錯誤」。（A）對：為少見的良性乳房疾病，病理特徵為淋巴球性乳腺炎（lymphocytic mastitis）合併間質纖維化（stromal fibrosis）。（B）大致對：典型見於長期胰島素依賴型糖尿病；嚴格而言 type 2 打胰島素者亦可發生，但仍屬經典關聯敘述。（C）錯：超音波典型為不規則低回音腫塊伴「顯著後方聲影」，反映病灶質地「堅硬（hard）」而非「柔軟（soft）」，故「hypoechoic soft lesion」為不正確描述。（D）對：MRI 多呈邊界不清的腫塊伴對比強化，且顯影表現多變。
