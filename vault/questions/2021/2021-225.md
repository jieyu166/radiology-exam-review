#交換 #2021交換 #CH Which one is Not the chest CT findings have been reported in more than 70% of RT-PCR test–proven COVID-19 cases?
(A) ground-glass opacities
(B) vascular enlargement
(C) peripheral location
(D) pleural effusion
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】Chest CT Abnormalities with High Incidence (>70%)
Several chest CT findings have been reported in more than 70% of RT-PCR test–proven COVID-19 cases,
including ground-glass opacities, vascular enlargement, bilateral abnormalities, lower lobe involvement,
and posterior predilection. In COVID-19–endemic regions, the observation of these chest CT findings
should raise the suspicion of possible COVID-19 diagnosis.
