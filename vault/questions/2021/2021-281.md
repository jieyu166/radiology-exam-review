#交換 #2021交換 #NR 關於顱內大血管之血管壁影像(vessel wall imaging)在臨床上的應用，以下 何者有錯？
(A) 對於造成慢性動脈狹窄病因的鑑別診斷有幫助。
(B) 對於急性大血管阻塞的病因，及血管內治療策略的擬定有幫助。
(C) 對於動脈斑塊是否容易破裂造成併發病可能有幫助。
(D) 偵測非狹窄的血管病灶有幫助。
??
**Ans: B**
答案 B。血管壁影像（VW-MRI）的臨床價值在於「慢性」血管病變的鑑別與評估，而非急性大血管阻塞（LVO）的病因判定或血管內取栓策略擬定，故 B 有錯。（A）對慢性動脈狹窄成因的鑑別有幫助：動脈粥狀硬化呈偏心性管壁增厚、不均勻強化，血管炎呈同心圓管壁增厚、均勻強化，剝離見 T1 壁內血腫／T2 內膜瓣，RCVS 則管壁幾乎不強化，可依強化型態區分，正確。（C）可評估斑塊活性與是否易破裂（如強化代表活性斑塊、T1 高訊號代表斑塊內出血），對併發症風險判讀有幫助，正確。（D）能偵測管腔造影正常但管壁確有病灶的非狹窄性病變（如症狀性非狹窄斑塊），正確。→ [[mri-vessel-wall-imaging]]

**RECOMMENDATIONS FOR CLINICAL PRACTICE**

**Intracranial VW-MR Imaging Is Likely a Useful Adjunct to Conventional Imaging**
1) To differentiate among causes of intracranial arterial narrowing such as intracranial atherosclerotic plaque, vasculitis, reversible cerebral vasoconstriction syndrome, and arterial dissection.
2) To identify symptomatic, nonstenotic disease of the intracranial arteries.

**Intracranial VW-MR Imaging Is Possibly a Useful Adjunct to Conventional Imaging**
1) To determine the location of atherosclerotic plaque relative to branch artery ostia.
2) To assess atherosclerotic plaque activity.
3) To assess vasculitis activity.
4) To select an intracranial target for biopsy in suspected CNS vasculitis.
5) To determine which aneurysm has ruptured in patients with acute subarachnoid hemorrhage and multiple aneurysms.

**Intracranial VW-MR Imaging Is Currently in the Domain of Research**
1) To predict future behavior of unruptured intracranial saccular aneurysms.
