#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which of the following matches is INCORRECT about craniosynostosis?
(A) Early fusion of sagittal suture - Scaphocephaly
(B) Early fusion of metopic suture - Trigonocephaly
(C) Early fusion of one coronal suture - Brachycephaly
(D) Early fusion of one lambdoid suture - Plagiocephaly
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。單側（one）冠狀縫早閉造成的是前部／額部斜頭（anterior／frontal plagiocephaly，伴同側 harlequin eye 徵象），而非短頭（brachycephaly）；brachycephaly 需雙側冠狀縫和／或雙側人字縫早閉才會形成，故 C 配對不正確。其餘配對正確：A 矢狀縫早閉→舟狀頭（scaphocephaly）；B 額縫早閉→三角頭（trigonocephaly）；D 單側人字縫早閉→後部斜頭（plagiocephaly）。題目問「不正確」者，故選 C。→ [[craniosynostosis-suture-fusion]]
