#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which of the following matches is INCORRECT about craniosynostosis?
(A) Early fusion of sagittal suture - Scaphocephaly
(B) Early fusion of metopic suture - Trigonocephaly
(C) Early fusion of one coronal suture - Brachycephaly
(D) Early fusion of one lambdoid suture - Plagiocephaly
??
**Ans: C**
【2021 官方詳解】
C🡪 anterior/frontal plagiocephaly
- brachycephaly: bicoronal and/or bilambdoid sutures
- scaphocephaly/dolichocephaly: sagittal suture
- plagiocephaly: unilateral coronal and lambdoid sutures
  - anterior/frontal plagiocephaly: unilateral coronal suture
  - posterior/occipital plagiocephaly: unilateral lambdoid suture
- trigonocephaly: metopic suture
- pachycephaly: lambdoid suture
- oxycephaly/turricephaly: sagittal, coronal and lambdoid sutures (tower like skull)
- cloverleaf skull/Kleeblattschadel: intrauterine sagittal, coronal, lambdoid sutures (most severe)
- harlequin eye: ipsilateral coronal suture
- progressive postnatal pansynostosis: rare form of craniosynostosis which involves late (postnatal) fusion of all cranial sutures
