#交換 #2021交換 #Physics 有關含碘對比劑的安全事項，下列敘述何者正確?
(A) 有 hyperthyroidism 的病人若暴露於含碘對比劑，有引起thyrotoxicosis 的風險，corticosteroid premedication 可能有幫助
(B) 含碘對比劑的施打對接受 radioactive iodine therapy 或 radioactive iodine scan 的病人可能造成影響，應該建議有適當的 washout period
(C) Intradermal skin testing 可正確預測發生 adverse reactions 所以對 minimize 不良反應是有幫助的
(D) 病人經 premedication 後就不可能有 contrast reaction 了
??
**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】
- In patients with acute thyroid storm, iodinated contrast medium exposure can potentiate thyrotoxicosis; in such patients, iodinated contrast medium should be avoided. Corticosteroid premedication in this setting is unlikely to be helpful.
- Intradermal skin testing with contrast media to predict the likelihood of adverse reactions has not been shown to be useful in minimizing reaction risk.
- Premedication does not prevent all contrast reactions. Allergic- like contrast reactions that occur despite premedication are called “breakthrough reactions”. Physiologic reactions are not mitigated by premedication and are not considered “breakthrough reactions,” even if they occur following premedication.
