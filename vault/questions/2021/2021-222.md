#交換 #2021交換 #CH Currently, which is **wrong** about lung cancer screening?
(A) Using low-dose computed tomography (LDCT) for lung cancer screening is recommended for heavy smokers in North America, Europe and some countries in Asia
(B) Strategy to screen for lung cancer in the never-smoking population needs to be developed
(C) Environmental risk factors, but not genetic factors are reported to play a role in lung cancer in the never-smoking population
(D) The cost of LDCT screening and insurance coverage are important factors in lung cancer screening and mortality
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。針對從不吸菸者的肺癌（LCINS），遺傳因子與環境因子同樣扮演重要角色，且其在遺傳與分子變化上與吸菸者的肺癌明顯不同（如 EGFR 突變、ALK 重排在非吸菸者及亞裔更常見）。(C) 稱「環境因子有影響、但基因因子不參與」與事實相反，故為錯誤選項。(A) LDCT 篩檢已用於北美、歐洲及部分亞洲的重度吸菸者、(B) 針對從不吸菸族群仍需另行發展篩檢策略、(D) LDCT 篩檢成本與保險給付會影響篩檢普及與死亡率，皆為正確敘述。
