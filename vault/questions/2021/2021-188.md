#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Regarding splenic hemangioma: Which one is not the common imaging features on MRI?
(A) Hyperintense on T1-weighted images
(B) Hyperintense on T2-weighted images
(C) Can show nodular centripetal enhancement
(D) Can show uniform enhancement at delayed imaging
(E) Can show homogeneous enhancement
??
**Ans: A**
答案 A。脾臟血管瘤在 T1WI 多呈等訊號至低訊號（iso-to-hypointense to 脾實質），並非高訊號，故「T1 高訊號」不是其常見特徵、為本題正解。B 正確：T2WI 呈高訊號。C 正確：可呈結節狀向心性（centripetal）強化。D 正確：延遲期呈均勻強化。E 正確：較小的病灶可於即時對比後即呈均質強化並持續至延遲期。→ [[splenic-tumors]]
