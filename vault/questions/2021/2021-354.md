#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which of the following about hemorrhagic ovarian cysts is **WRONG**?
(A) A hemorrhagic ovarian cyst often involves a corpus luteum cyst rather than a follicular cyst
(B) Rupture may be associated with sexual intercourse, trauma, and pregnancy
(C) CT Hounsfield units (HU) is not applicable to differentiate hemorrhagic ovarian cyst rupture with hemoperitoneum
(D) The MR imaging can be variable, depending on the age of the hemorrhage, with hyperintensity on both T1- and T2-weighted images or hyperintensity on T1-weighted images and hypointensity on T2-weighted images
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。CT 的 Hounsfield unit 確實可用於偵測與鑑別出血性卵巢囊腫破裂與腹腔積血——囊內出血呈高衰減（未凝血約 30–45 HU、凝血塊 45–70 HU、水樣液體 0–15 HU），破裂後 hemoperitoneum 呈高衰減腹水並可見 hematocrit level／sentinel clot，故選項 C「CT HU 不適用於鑑別」為錯誤敘述（正解）。其餘皆正確：出血性囊腫多源自黃體（corpus luteum）而非濾泡囊腫（A）；破裂可與性交、外傷、懷孕相關（B）；MR 表現隨出血時期而異，可 T1、T2 皆呈高訊號，或 T1 高、T2 低訊號（D）。→ [[ovarian-mass-benign-vs-malignant]]
