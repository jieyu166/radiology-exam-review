#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which statement is correct?
(A) Loss of the normal swallow tail appearance of susceptibility signal pattern in the substantia nigra on axial imaging is perhaps the most promising diagnostic sign for Parkinson disease
(B) Mesial temporal lobe and hippocampi remain relatively normal in size, helping to distinguish Lewy body disease from Alzheimer disease
(C) On T1-weighted images in patients of Wilson disease presenting with neurologic manifestations, these areas show hypointensity. In contrast, patients with severe hepatic dysfunction show areas of T1 hyperintensity, especially in the globus pallidus, similar to that seen in acquired (non-Wilsonian) hepatocerebral degeneration attributed to manganese deposition
(D) All of above
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。A：帕金森氏症於軸位 SWI／T2* 見黑質正常「燕尾（swallow tail）」高訊號消失，是極具前景的診斷徵象（對 PD／路易體失智之診斷準確度可達九成以上），敘述正確；B：路易體失智（DLB）之內側顳葉與海馬相對保留、體積正常，可藉此與典型內側顳葉萎縮的阿茲海默症區分，敘述正確；C：威爾森氏症神經型於 T1WI 多呈低訊號，而嚴重肝功能不良者反於 T1WI（尤以蒼白球）呈高訊號，與後天性（非威爾森型）肝腦退化之錳（manganese）沉積表現相似，敘述正確；三段敘述皆正確，故選 D（All of above）。→ [[hepatic-encephalopathy-manganese-deposition]]
