**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
Parkinson disease: Loss of the normal swallow tail appearance of susceptibility signal pattern in the substantia nigra on axial imaging is perhaps the most promising diagnostic sign
Wilson disease:
- Most common: Abnormal T2 hyperintensity in the putamina
- T2 signal abnormality predominantly affect the deep gray nuclei.
- Face of the giant panda sign (Involvement of midbrain tegmentum)
- Miniature panda sign (Axial T2 MR at pons)
- On T1-weighted hypointensity
- *** Patients with severe hepatic dysfunction show areas of T1 hyperintensity, especially in the globus pallidus, similar to that seen in acquired (non-Wilsonian) hepatocerebral degeneration attributed to manganese deposition .
- Diffusion restriction may be seen early in the course of the disease 7.
