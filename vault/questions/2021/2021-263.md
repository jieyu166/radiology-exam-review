#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Choose the correct option:
(A) In psoriatic arthritis, since inflammation happens in enthesis and not in synovium, existence of effusion in MRI helps to exclude the diagnosis of psoriatic arthritis
(B) Boutonniere deformity happens more commonly in rheumatoid arthritis, while Hitchhiker deformity more commonly seen in patients with psoriatic arthritis
(C) Mouse ear sign represents erosion in bare areas of joint and suggests diagnosis of rheumatoid arthritis
(D) In late-stage psoriatic arthritis, bone destruction and erosions with new bone formation may result in pencil-in-cup deformity
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
(A) Effusion in the joint or tendon sheath, which occurs in both RA and PsA, is commonly associated with joint capsular synovitis or tenosynovitis
(B) Boutonnière deformity (buttonhole deformity) and swan neck deformity are most frequent type of deformity in RA and may manifest at early stages. Other deformities such as mallet finger and hitchhiker deformity are more likely to be found in advanced stages of RA.
(C) The combination of bone erosion and proliferation at enthesis gives a characteristic appearance of mouse ears on the distal surface of the interphalangeal joint, with frequent involvement of the DIP joints. The term bony corona was previously used to describe severe bone proliferation, which affects the entire circumference of the bone in PsA.
