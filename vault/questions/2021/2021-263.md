#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Choose the correct option:
(A) In psoriatic arthritis, since inflammation happens in enthesis and not in synovium, existence of effusion in MRI helps to exclude the diagnosis of psoriatic arthritis
(B) Boutonniere deformity happens more commonly in rheumatoid arthritis, while Hitchhiker deformity more commonly seen in patients with psoriatic arthritis
(C) Mouse ear sign represents erosion in bare areas of joint and suggests diagnosis of rheumatoid arthritis
(D) In late-stage psoriatic arthritis, bone destruction and erosions with new bone formation may result in pencil-in-cup deformity
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。此題選「正確」敘述。D 正確：晚期乾癬性關節炎的骨破壞與侵蝕合併新骨形成，可在 DIP 造成典型的鉛筆套杯（pencil-in-cup）變形。A 錯：關節或腱鞘積液在 RA 與 PsA 皆可見，不能用來排除 PsA；B 錯：鈕扣狀（Boutonnière）與天鵝頸變形是 RA 最常見且早期即可出現的變形，hitchhiker 等屬進展期，非 PsA 專屬；C 錯：老鼠耳（mouse-ear）徵象是 PsA 附著點骨侵蝕合併骨增生的表現、好發 DIP，並非 RA 的裸區侵蝕。
