#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which one has **wrong** MR signals corresponding to the schematic liver nodular lesions?
(A) Siderotic regenerative nodule: T1 dark, T2 dark, not enhance
(B) High-grade dysplastic nodule: T1 bright, T2 intermediate to dark, usually not enhance, but possible hypointense in hepatobiliary phase
(C) Early hepatocellular carcinoma: T2 slightly bright, no arterial phase wash-in, but portal venous phase wash-out, hypointense in hepatobiliary phase
(D) Typical hepatocellular carcinoma: T2 bright, arterial phase wash-in, portal venous phase wash-out, hypointense in hepatobiliary phase
(E) Green hepatocellular carcinoma: T2 bright, arterial phase wash-in, portal venous phase wash-out, hypointense in hepatobiliary phase
??
**Ans: E**

## 詳解附圖（OCR）
肝臟結節性病灶在各 MRI 序列的訊號表現（示意圖表）：

| Lesion | Unenhanced T1 | Arterial | Portal venous | Delayed | Hepatobiliary | DWI | T2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RN | iso | iso | iso | iso | hyper | iso | iso |
| HGDN | iso | slightly hyper | iso | iso | hypo | iso | iso |
| Early HCC | iso | slightly hyper | hypo | hypo | hypo | hyper | slightly hyper |
| HCC classic | iso | hyper | hypo | hypo | hypo | hyper | hyper |
| HCC green | iso | hyper | hypo | hypo | hyper | iso | hyper |
| HCC hypovascular | iso | iso | iso | hypo | hypo | hyper | hyper |

圖例：iso = isointense、hyper = hyperintense、hypo = hypointense、slightly hyper = slightly hyperintense lesion。
