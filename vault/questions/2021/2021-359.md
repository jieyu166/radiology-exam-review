#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Which of the following statements about giant cell tumor (GCT) of bone is incorrect?
(A) The prevalence of GCT peaks in the middle-aged adult, less commonly in elderly and rarely in children
(B) Most lesions develop in long bones, with the majority of cases occurring about the knee
(C) The typical appearance of GCT on conventional radiographs shows a lytic lesion that has a well-defined but nonsclerotic margin, eccentric in location, and extends to the subchondral bone
(D) Secondary aneurysmal bone cyst (ABC) formation occurs in up to 50% of cases of GCT of bone, telangiectatic osteosarcoma should be included in the differential diagnosis
(E) The drug denosumab targets the RANK pathway and stops the osteoclastic activity of cells in GCT, thus can be used to treat GCT
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。本題問「何者錯誤」，D 敘述次發性動脈瘤樣骨囊腫（ABC）發生率「高達 50%」為錯——GCT 內確實可因次發 ABC 而出現 fluid-fluid levels，惟文獻報告發生率僅約 10–14%，並非 50%；其提及需與 telangiectatic osteosarcoma 鑑別則屬正確概念。A 正確：GCT 好發於骨骼成熟後的中壯年（約 20–55 歲為高峰），老年較少、兒童罕見。B 正確：多發生於長骨末端，且以膝關節周圍（遠端股骨、近端脛骨）最多。C 正確：典型平片為偏心性溶骨、界線清楚但「非硬化緣」、延伸至軟骨下。E 正確：denosumab 作用於 RANK/RANKL 途徑，抑制蝕骨細胞活性，可用於治療 GCT。⚠️ 疑似與 2021-016 為同一題（相似度 0.823），請醫師確認是否合併。
