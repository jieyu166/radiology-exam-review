#交換 #2021交換 #CH In terms of connective tissue disease associated with interstitial lung disease (CTD-ILD), which one of the followings is not correct?
(A) NSIP is the most common finding in cases of CTD-ILD
(B) Among CTD-ILD, UIP is most commonly found in cases of rheumatoid arthritis
(C) Lymphocytic interstitial pneumonitis is relatively more commonly found in patients with Sjogren syndrome, it may also be found in cases of rheumatoid arthritis or SLE, but relatively rare
(D) Organizing pneumonia is more commonly found in cases of systemic sclerosis than in those of polymyositis/dermatomyositis
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。本題問 CTD-ILD 的敘述何者「不正確」。(D) 稱 organizing pneumonia（OP）在 systemic sclerosis 比 polymyositis/dermatomyositis 更常見，此敘述錯誤——OP 其實以 PM/DM（尤其 antisynthetase syndrome，常呈 NSIP-OP overlap／fibrosing OP）較常見，而 SSc 以 NSIP 為主、OP 少見，方向相反，故 (D) 不正確。
(A) 正確：NSIP（尤 fibrotic NSIP）是 CTD-ILD 整體最常見的型態。
(B) 正確：在各 CTD 中，UIP 最常見於 rheumatoid arthritis（RA 是唯一以 UIP 為主導、最像 IPF 的 CTD）。
(C) 正確：lymphocytic interstitial pneumonitis（LIP）最具 Sjögren 代表性，亦可見於 RA 或 SLE，但相對少見。
