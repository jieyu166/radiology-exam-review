#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which of the following about gynecologic emergencies is **WRONG**?
(A) Ectopic pregnancies occur when the blastocyst implants outside the endometrium of the uterus
(B) Ninety-five percent of ectopic pregnancies are tubal, and most commonly occur in the ampulla of the fallopian tube
(C) Torsion of a normal ovary is a rare entity that predominantly occurs in perimenarchal girls or during pregnancy
(D) Contralateral uterine deviation due to a shortened adnexal ligament is a direct finding of ovarian torsion
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。卵巢扭轉時因附件血管蒂縮短，子宮會偏向「同側（ipsilateral）」扭轉側，而非選項 D 所述之對側（contralateral），故 D 為錯誤敘述（正解）。其餘皆正確：子宮外孕為胚囊著床於子宮內膜腔以外（A）；約 95% 為輸卵管妊娠且最常見於壺腹部（ampulla，B）；正常卵巢的扭轉罕見，好發於初經前女孩或孕期（C）。→ [[adnexal-torsion]]
