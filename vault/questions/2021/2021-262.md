#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Choose the correct option:
(A) The test result of rheumatoid factor reliably differentiates rheumatoid arthritis from other inflammatory arthritis
(B) In rheumatoid arthritis, inflammation begins at the enthesis and later spreads to adjacent synovium
(C) In psoriatic arthritis, inflammation begins in bare areas of joints and most commonly involves the distal phalangeal joints
(D) New bone formation happens in psoriatic arthritis and uncommonly happens in rheumatoid arthritis
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。此題選「正確」敘述。D 正確：關節旁新骨形成（periostitis／enthesophyte）是乾癬性關節炎的特徵，類風濕性關節炎則以純侵蝕加近關節骨質疏鬆為主，較少見新骨形成。A 錯：約 30% 類風濕性關節炎的類風濕因子為陰性，故 RF 無法可靠區分 RA 與其他發炎性關節炎；B 錯：RA 發炎的原發部位是滑膜，附著點（enthesis）才是 PsA 的起始處；C 錯：始於關節裸區（bare area）侵蝕、以近端關節為主的是 RA，而非以 DIP／附著點為主的 PsA。
