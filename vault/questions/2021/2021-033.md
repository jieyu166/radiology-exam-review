#交換 #2021交換 #CV Which of the following statements is **false** about anomalous origin of coronary artery?
(A) The ALCAPA syndrome (anomalous origin of the left coronary artery from the pulmonary artery) is among the differential diagnoses for massive cardiomegaly in the newborn
(B) Artery courses downward (hammock sign) below the crista supraventricularis in interarterial course
(C) Prepulmonic coronary arteries are particularly common in tetralogy of Fallot (D)Transseptal course does not have an oblong or slitlike orifice
??
**Ans: B**
答案 B。hammock sign（冠狀動脈向下走行、位於室上嵴 crista supraventricularis 下方、肺動脈瓣下 RVOT 層面）屬於 transseptal（subpulmonic）變異，而非 interarterial course，因此「in interarterial course」是張冠李戴的錯誤敘述，為本題所選。其餘皆正確：(A) ALCAPA（左冠狀動脈起源自肺動脈，Bland-White-Garland）因整個冠狀系統擴大扭曲，是新生兒期巨大心臟的鑑別診斷之一；(C) 橫越右心室流出道的 prepulmonic 冠狀動脈好發於法洛四聯症，術前須標明以免傷及跨越 RVOT 之血管；(D) transseptal 為良性走行，不具裂縫狀（slit-like）或橢圓形開口——裂縫狀開口是惡性 interarterial/intramural 走行的高風險形態。→ [[acaos]]
