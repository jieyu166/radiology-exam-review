#交換 #2021交換 #CH What is the upper limit diameter for a normal pulmonary arterial segment on CT or MR?
(A) 20mm
(B) 29mm
(C) 30mm
(D) 39mm
??
**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】
Enlarged pulmonary trunk (measured at pulmonary artery bifurcation on an axial slice vertical to its long axis) >29 mm diameter is often used as a general predictive cut-off.
Ref: radiopaedia
