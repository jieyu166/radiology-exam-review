(A) The goal of imaging is to localize the underlying cause, determine the extent of anatomic abnormality, and plan appropriate treatment
(B) Ultrasound may show intraductal or intracystic, oval or lobulated mass. Lesions in the central breast are more common and often visible, while peripheral small papillomas are frequently missed
(C) Ductography can be performed if mammography and ultrasound are negative, and its sensitivity and specificity are both higher than MR
(D) On ductography, ductal wall irregularity, distortion, obstruction or irregular filling defect suggest carcinoma
??
**Ans: C**
【2021 官方詳解】
Breast ductography (a.k.a. galactography)
Precisely locating the mass within breast tissue and gives useful information for surgical approach and planning.
Ref:
Diagnostic Performance of MRI Versus Galactography in Women With Pathologic Nipple Discharge: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis
Conclusion:
higher diagnostic performance of MRI compared with galactography in the detection of any kind of lesion with pathologic nipple discharge.
High sensitivity and very high specificity for cancer by MRI could be confirmed in this clinical setting.
If mammography and ultrasound are negative, MRI should be preferred over galactography for further evaluation.
