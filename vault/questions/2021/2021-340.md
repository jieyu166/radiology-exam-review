#交換 #2021交換 #Breast Which description is “**WRONG**” for nipple discharge?
(A) The goal of imaging is to localize the underlying cause, determine the extent of anatomic abnormality, and plan appropriate treatment
(B) Ultrasound may show intraductal or intracystic, oval or lobulated mass. Lesions in the central breast are more common and often visible, while peripheral small papillomas are frequently missed
(C) Ductography can be performed if mammography and ultrasound are negative, and its sensitivity and specificity are both higher than MR
(D) On ductography, ductal wall irregularity, distortion, obstruction or irregular filling defect suggest carcinoma
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。本題問乳頭分泌物（nipple discharge）何者「錯誤」。（A）對：影像目標是定位底層病因、界定解剖異常範圍並規劃適當治療。（B）對：超音波可見管內或囊內的卵圓/分葉腫塊，中央（subareolar）病灶較常見且較易顯示，周邊小 papilloma 常被遺漏。（C）錯：當乳房攝影與超音波陰性時，進一步評估應「優先 MRI 而非 galactography」，且 MRI 對病灶偵測的敏感度（92%）與特異度（76%）皆「高於」galactography（69%、39%），本選項稱 ductography 兩者皆高於 MR 恰好相反。（D）對：乳管攝影上管壁不規則、扭曲、阻塞或不規則充盈缺損提示惡性。
