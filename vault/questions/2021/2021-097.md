#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which of the followings about renal cell carcinoma (RCC) is **wrong**?
(A) Clear cell RCC is associated with Von-Hippel-Lindau syndrome
(B) Chromophobe RCC is associated with Birt Hogg Dube syndrome
(C) Papillary RCC is more often associated with lymphangitic spread and peritoneal carcinomatosis
(D) Medullary RCC is an uncommon low-grade neoplasm with imaging appearance of a multilobulated enhanced mass
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。Renal medullary carcinoma 罕見但屬高度侵襲性腫瘤（好發具鐮刀型血球特徵者），並非「低惡性度、呈多分葉強化腫塊」，故 D 敘述錯誤、為正解。A clear cell RCC 與 Von-Hippel-Lindau 症候群相關、B chromophobe RCC 與 Birt-Hogg-Dubé 症候群相關、C papillary RCC 較常見淋巴管播散與腹膜癌病，皆為正確敘述。→ [[renal-cell-carcinoma]]
