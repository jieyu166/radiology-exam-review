#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which of the followings about renal cell carcinoma (RCC) is **wrong**?
(A) Clear cell RCC is associated with Von-Hippel-Lindau syndrome
(B) Chromophobe RCC is associated with Birt Hogg Dube syndrome
(C) Papillary RCC is more often associated with lymphangitic spread and peritoneal carcinomatosis
(D) Medullary RCC is an uncommon low-grade neoplasm with imaging appearance of a multilobulated enhanced mass
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
Hereditary renal cancer syndromes
- von Hippel Lindau syndrome: predominantly clear cell type
- tuberous sclerosis: predominantly clear cell type (also associated with angiomyolipoma)
- Birt-Hogg-Dubé syndrome: predominantly chromophobe type, oncocytoma, or a hybrid of both
- sickle cell trait or hemoglobin SC disease: renal medullary carcinoma
- hereditary renal oncocytoma: oncocytoma
Pathology
- clear cell renal carcinoma (conventional): 70-80%
- papillary renal cell carcinoma: 13-20%
  - can be multifocal and bilateral
  - most common form in dialysis-associated RCC
- chromophobe renal cell carcinoma: 5%
  - similar histologically to renal oncocytomas 4
  - best prognosis 5
- collecting duct renal cell carcinoma (Bellini duct): <1%
  - worst prognosis 5
- renal medullary carcinoma: rare, highly aggressive.
MRI
- T2: appearances depend on histology 6
  - clear cell RCC: T2 hyperintense
  - papillary RCC: T2 hypointense
- T1 C+ (Gd): often shows prompt arterial enhancement
Clear cell RCC: Pancreatic and renal metastases
Papillary RCC: Lymphangitic spread and peritoneal carcinomatosis
Sarcomatoid transformation in ccRCC: Increased the risk of lymphangitic spread, pleural and peritoneal metastases
