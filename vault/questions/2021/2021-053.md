#交換 #2021交換 #2023交換 #NR Which one of the following descriptions about lactate in [[MRS]] is **wrong**?
(A) Lactate is a marker of anaerobic metabolism.
(B) Double peak at 1.3 ppm on spectra obtained with a short echo time (35 msec),
(C) Inversion below the baseline on spectra obtained with intermediate echo times (135–144 msec)
(D) Lactate peak is overlaid with GABA peak
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。本題問「何者錯誤」，D 稱 lactate peak 與 GABA peak 重疊為錯——lactate 峰（1.3 ppm）實際是與相鄰的 lipid 峰（約 0.9–1.3 ppm）重疊，可利用 intermediate TE（135–144 ms）使 lactate doublet 反轉至基線下、而 lipid 不反轉，藉此區分二者。A 正確：lactate 為無氧代謝的標記。B 正確：於 1.3 ppm 呈雙峰（doublet，源自 J-coupling），短 TE 時可見。C 正確：intermediate TE 時 lactate 反轉至基線下方。⚠️ 本題含影像（官方詳解 PDF 第 302 頁），影像尚未匯入，判讀請對照原檔。
