#交換 #2021交換 #PED Which description about disease of neonatal chest is incorrect?
(A) Infantile respiratory distress syndrome, previously known as hyaline membrane disease, usually occurs in premature infants
(B) Cesarean section, prolonged labor and maternal aneshesis or diabetes are shown to be contributing factors of fetal lung fluid retention
(C) Meconium aspiration syndrome is related to perinal stress and commonly ocurrs in pre-term infant
(D) Pulmonary interstitial emphysema (PIE) and bronchopulmonary dysplasia (BPD) are possible complications of ventilatory therapy for iinfantile respiratory distress syndrme
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。胎便吸入症候群源於周產期窘迫／子宮內缺氧下吸入胎便，好發於足月或過期產兒，早產兒罕見，故 C 稱其「常發生於早產兒」為錯誤敘述。其餘正確：(A) 表面張力素缺乏病（即嬰兒呼吸窘迫症候群，舊稱透明膜病）因第二型肺泡細胞不成熟、surfactant 不足，好發早產兒；(B) 剖腹產、產程延長、母體麻醉或糖尿病會延遲胎兒肺液清除，是暫時性呼吸急促的促成因子；(D) 肺間質性肺氣腫（PIE）與支氣管肺發育不良（BPD）為表面張力素缺乏病接受呼吸器治療後的可能併發症。→ [[neonatal-chest-disease]]
