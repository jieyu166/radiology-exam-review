(A) Red marrow can be focal and mass-like
(B) Red marrow reconversion always progresses in a consistent, predictable pattern
(C) Red marrow in adults typically persists in the hands and feet
(D) Red marrow does not contain fat cells
(E) Red marrow is easily differentiated from neoplasm on MRI
??
**Ans: A**
【2021 官方詳解】
A.
Red bone marrow has a variety of normal appearances. include smooth contiguous involvement
of bones, focal mass-like lesions, and a fine-speckled appearance.
B.
red bone marrow reconverts from yellow marrow, it does not always progress in a predictable pattern
red marrow converts to mature yellow or fatty marrow, from peripheral to central.
C.
hands and feet tend to contain mature fatty marrow early in the maturation process, with red
marrow usually not persisting in these regions.
D.
Red bone marrow consists of hematopoietic cells containing water and fat cells
E.
differentiation is more problematic because of the inconsistent location and a sometimes masslike pattern of red marrow reconversion
