#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which one of the following is **false** regarding the adrenal dynamic CT exam?
(A) The most common protocol incorporate pre-contrast scan, initial enhanced phase (~70 second scan delay) and 30-minute delay
(B) 10-minute delay is utilized by some centers, but shorter delay may decrease sensitivity
(C) Reason for doing dynamic phase is to calculate washout percentage to evaluate for adrenal adenoma
(D) Adrenal washout calculators are readily available on multiple websites
??
**Ans: A**
答案 A。標準腎上腺 dynamic CT washout protocol 為非顯影期＋強化期（約 60–90 秒）＋15 分鐘延遲，而非「30 分鐘延遲」，故 A 敘述錯誤、即為本題要選者。B 正確：部分中心採 10 分鐘延遲，但較短延遲可能降低敏感度；C 正確：dynamic 期用於計算 washout 百分比以評估腎上腺腺瘤（absolute washout ≥60%／relative washout ≥40%）；D 正確：多個網站提供 washout 計算器。→ [[adrenal-incidentaloma-washout]]
