#交換 #2021交換 #US Which one is not the typical finding of uterine adenomyosis on US?
(A) tiny cysts and cystic striations
(B) increased vascularity
(C) distinct border
(D) heterogenous echotexture with nodules and striations
??
**Ans: C**
【2021 官方詳解】
C: ill-defined
US of adenomyosis:
"Venetian blind 百葉窗" or "rain shower" appearance (linear striations, parallel shadowing)
cirrhosis of the uterus (ill-defined)
"adeno":
- subendometrial echogenic linear striations and/or nodules (specific sign), extending from endometrium
and into inner myometrium
- hyperechoic islands
- tiny (1-5 mm) anechoic myometrial and subendometrial cysts (specific sign)
"myosis":
- focal or diffuse myometrial bulkiness, which may be asymmetric
typically of the fundal region and posterior wall
- Vascularity: increased
increased number of tortuous vessels penetrating myometrium
