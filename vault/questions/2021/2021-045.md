#交換 #2021交換 #2023交換 #MSK What are the typical MRI characteristics of neoplastic marrow involvement?
(A) Isointense to muscle on T1, lack of signal dropout on out-of-phase imaging.
(B) Hyperintense to muscle on T1, lack of signal dropout on out-of-phase imaging.
(C) Isointense to muscle on T1, signal dropout on out-of-phase imaging.
(D) Hyperintense to muscle on T1, signal dropout on out-of-phase imaging.
(E) Hypointense to muscle on T2, lack of signal dropout on out-of-phase imaging..
??
**Ans: A**
【2021 官方詳解】Neoplastic bone marrow involvement
isointense or hypointense to muscle on T1WI.
hyperintense to  skeletal muscle on fluid-sensitive sequences.
lacks normal signal dropout on out-of-phase imaging, due to the lack of remaining normal bone marrow
fat.
Red marrow:
hyperintense signal to muscle on T1WI
hyperintense to  skeletal muscle on fluid-sensitive sequences
signal dropout on out-of-phase imaging.
