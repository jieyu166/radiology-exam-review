#交換 #2021交換 #CV For a 43 y/o man, progressive dyspnea on exertion and jaw pain
(A) If CAC = 0, power of zero, low risk of MACE
(B) If CAC = 0, should consider coronary CTA
(C) Must check ASCVD 10-year risk and decide coronary CTA or not
(D) All correct
??
**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】When the pretest probability of coronary artery disease is low (eg, asymptomatic screening setting), a CAC
score of zero is associated with low risk of coronary artery disease and low risk of near-term coronary
events.
In older asymptomatic patients with risk factors, CAC=0 is associated with a moderate increased risk of
events.
Patient group with clinical signs and symptoms associated with an intermediate-to-high risk of coronary
disease, CAC=0 is often associated with myocardial ischemia on provocative testing (16% in their patients)
and with a high risk of near-term coronary events (nearly 9 times higher than previously reported for any
asymptomatic cohort). It is appropriate to question the added value of the CAC score in this moderately
high-risk patient group
Ref: How Low-Risk Is a Coronary Calcium Score of Zero? The Importance of Conditional Probability
https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.108.767665   Circulation. 2008;117:1627–1629
