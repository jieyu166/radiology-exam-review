#交換 #2021交換 #MSK About giant cell tumor of tendon sheath, which one is **WRONG**?
(A) These benign tumors of synovial origin typically occur in the digits over the dorsal aspect
(B) These tumors arise from the tendon sheath, joint capsule, fascia, or ligaments
(C) These tumors contain multinucleated giant cells and have intra- and extracellular hemosiderin deposition
(D) The low signals on T1WI and T2WI are due to the paramagnetic effect of hemosiderin deposition
??
**Ans: A**
答案 A。本題問「何者錯誤」。A 稱 GCTTS 好發於手指背側（dorsal aspect），為錯：GCTTS 雖約 85% 發生於手指，但以掌側（volar／屈側）為主、鄰近遠端指間關節（DIP）與近端指骨，故「背側」描述不正確。B 源自腱鞘、關節囊、筋膜或韌帶，正確；C 含多核巨細胞及細胞內外含鐵血黃素沉積，正確；D 含鐵血黃素的順磁效應致 T1WI、T2WI 皆呈低訊號，正確。→ [[giant-cell-tumor-tendon-sheath]]
