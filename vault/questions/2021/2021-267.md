#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which of the following description is **false** regarding pseudoprogression of tumor after radiation therapy?
(A) It rarely occurs within 3 months
(B) It shows increased size of enhancing lesion
(C) It is related to local tissue inflammatory reaction
(D) Imaging cannot reliably differentiate pseudoprogression from true progression
??
**Ans: A**
答案 A。假性進展（pseudoprogression）屬 early-delayed／subacute 期變化，多在放療後數週至約 3 個月內出現（膠質母細胞瘤同步放化療 Stupp protocol 後尤其常見），並非「罕見於 3 個月內」，故 A 敘述錯誤；B：表現為強化病灶增大，正確；C：與局部組織發炎反應（放射對寡突膠細胞髓鞘合成之暫時性損傷）相關，正確；D：常規影像無法可靠區分假性進展與真進展，需輔以灌注／MRS／PET 及時序追蹤，正確。題目問「錯誤」者，故選 A。→ [[radiation-induced-changes]]
