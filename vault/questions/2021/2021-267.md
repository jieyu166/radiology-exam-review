#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which of the following description is **false** regarding pseudoprogression of tumor after radiation therapy?
(A) It rarely occurs within 3 months
(B) It shows increased size of enhancing lesion
(C) It is related to local tissue inflammatory reaction
(D) Imaging cannot reliably differentiate pseudoprogression from true progression
??
**Ans: A**
【2021 官方詳解】
(A) Pseudoprogression often appears several weeks up to months (i.e., 3+) after radiation treatment.
The cause of pseudoprogression is the transient interruption of myelin synthesis secondary to
radiation injury to oligodendrocytes. Many studies suggest a transient course with spontaneous
recovery. In contrast, radiation necrosis may appear months to several years after radiation therapy. Radiation necrosis involves a space occupying necrotic lesion with mass effect and neurological dysfunction.
