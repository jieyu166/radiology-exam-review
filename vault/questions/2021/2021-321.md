**Ans: A**
【2021 官方詳解】US contrast agent
At a low MI      (< 0.2),  bubbles are in harmonic motion
At a higher MI (>0.3),   bubbles are disrupted  & emit a strong, highly nonlinear echo.
Mechanical index = peak negative pressure / √(center frequency of the US beam)
MI => proportional to an ultrasound beam's peak negative pressure (or peak rarefaction pressure).
=> inversely proportional to the frequency of the beam
peak negative pressure: an attenuation factor to account for in-tissue acoustic attenuation
