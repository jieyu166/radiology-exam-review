#交換 #2021交換 #ABD 有關Intraductal papillary neoplasm of the bile ducts, 以下敘述, 何者不正確 ?
(A) 又稱為 Biliary papillomatosis
(B) 可以分泌多量mucinous, 造成膽到阻塞
(C) 很少有惡性化的機會, 也很少復發, 預後很好
(D) MRCP, CT可以提供好的診斷
(E) 通常是多發性的
??
**Ans: C**
【2021 官方詳解】
Intraductal papillary neoplasm of the bile duct (IPNB)
preinvasive biliary tree neoplasm=> cholangiocarcinoma
biliary papillomatosis=> diffuse type IPNB
analogous to IPMN of pancreas
highest malignant potential, recurrence rate 20% to 60%, depending on the presence of an invasive component
