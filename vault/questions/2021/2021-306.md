#交換 #2021交換 #PED (C) + (D) ?? Which of the following statements is CORRECT?
(A) Pulmonary artery sling is a rare congenital anomaly in which the right pulmonary artery originates from the left pulmonary artery and encircles the left mainstem bronchus and distal trachea, causing compression
(B) Congenital tracheal stenosis consists of a simple local narrowing of the trachea, usually in the lower third
(C) Length of stenosis does not appear to be as critical as degree of stenosis in producing symptoms. Whereas resistance increases fourfolds relative to luminal radius decrease
(D) Magnetic resonance (MR) imaging is valuable for demonstrating the relationship of the airway to adjacent blood vessels
??
**Ans: B**
答案 B。局灶型（focal）先天性氣管狹窄即為氣管的單純局部狹窄，通常位於下三分之一，故 B 為正確敘述。(A) 錯誤：肺動脈吊索是「左」肺動脈異常起自「右」肺動脈（非右起自左），繞行於氣管與食道之間造成壓迫，A 把左右對調。(C) 描述有瑕疵：狹窄長度確實不如狹窄程度關鍵，惟氣道阻力與狹窄「長度」僅呈線性正比、卻隨管腔半徑縮小呈四次方增加，C 略去了「與長度呈線性」的前提。(D) 本身為正確概念：MR 可不打血管內對比劑即顯示氣道與鄰近血管的關係，CT 與 MR 皆能三維重建；本題設定選單一最正確者，故為 B。⚠️ 本卡標示疑與另題高度相似（相似度 0.831），選項判讀與是否合併請一併覆核。→ [[ring-sling-syndrome]]
