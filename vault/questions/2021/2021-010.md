(A) Rapid initial upslope in the ﬁrst 2 minutes with plateau or washout on delayed phase are more likely to be benign.

(C) DCIS generally enhances more rapidly than invasive cancers.

**Ans: A**
A. malignant. plateau 64%, washout 87%.
B. typically benign.
C. Kinetics are usually not useful in DCIS, especially low-grade. Many DCISs show no wash out and usually slow initial enhancement.
Non-mass like enhancement:
- Focal: <25% malignant
- Ductal: 60% malignant
- Linear: 31% malignant

Enhancing lesion in breast MRI
Focus and foci: <5mm, non-specific
Masses:
- Shape: round, oval, lobulated, irregular (32% malignant)
- Margin: smooth, irregular, speculated (80% malignant)
- T1 high: lymph node, fat necrosis, hamartoma
- T2FS high: cyst, lymph node, fibroadenoma, fat necrosis, colloid carcinoma
- Enhancement
  - Type I (persistent): benign (6%malignanct)
  - Type II (platea): malignant
  - Type III (washout): stroung malignancy
Non-mass like enhancement:
- Focal: <25% malignant
- Ductal: 60% malignant
- Linear: 31% malignant
- Segmental: 78% malignant
- Regional: 21% cancer
- Diffuse: typically benign
