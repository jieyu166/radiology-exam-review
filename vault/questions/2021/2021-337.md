#交換 #2021交換 #Breast 關於Mammography audit, 以下何者為非？
(A) 在Screening mammography, category 3亦屬於陽性判讀結果
(B) 在diagnostic mammography, category 3屬於陰性判讀結果
(C) Screening mammography的"recall" rate為(category,0,4,5之和)/(總檢 查數)
(D) PPV2為(真陽性之數目)/(建議切片之數目)。
??
**Ans: C**
【2021 官方詳解】
Recall rate was calculated by dividing the number of BI-RADS category 0 screening mammographic results by the total number of screening digital mammography examinations performed.
CDR (cancer detection rate) was calculated by dividing the total number of malignancies by the total number of screening digital mammography examinations performed.
PPV of positive screening result (PPV1) was calculated as the percentage of all positive screening examinations (BI-RADS categories 0, 4, and 5) that resulted in a diagnosis of cancer within 1 year
PPV of biopsy recommendation (PPV2) (真陽性之數目)/(建議切片之數目)。
the percentage of screening examinations with biopsy recommendations (BI-RADS categories 4 and 5) that resulted in a cancer diagnosis within 1 year
PPV of biopsied performed (PPV3) (真陽性之數目)/(所有切片之數目)。
the percentage of all known biopsies performed that resulted in a cancer diagnosis within 1 year
