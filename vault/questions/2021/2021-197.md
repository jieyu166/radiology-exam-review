#交換 #2021交換 #ABD At postpartum uterine imaging, it is challenging to differentiate dehiscence from normal appearance of uterine incision. Which of the followings is not CORRECT?
(A) CT is better than pelvic MRI in imaging diagnosis
(B) May have free fluid in peritoneal cavity
(C) May have large bladder flap hematoma
(D) May have pleural effusion
(E) May show bowel distention
??
**Ans: A**
答案 A。CT 對子宮切口 dehiscence 的敏感度差、影像徵象非特異；骨盆 MRI 因多切面與軟組織對比可顯示是否有完整漿膜層、進而區分 uterine rupture 與 dehiscence，其診斷率優於顯影 CT，故「CT 優於 pelvic MRI」與事實相反、為錯誤敘述、為本題正解。B、C、D、E 皆為可伴隨並支持 dehiscence 診斷的徵象：腹腔游離液、較大的 bladder flap hematoma（>5 cm 高度可疑）、肋膜積液（pleural effusion）與腸脹（bowel distention）。此鑑別的臨床意義在於 rupture 需手術修補、dehiscence 多可保守。→ [[postpartum-uterine-dehiscence-imaging]]
