#交換 #2021交換 #CH Which of the statement is **false**?
(A) In children younger than 5 years, thymus typically appears quadrilateral with convex borders
(B) When the body is exposed to stress, the thymus may grow, a phenomenon known as thymic rebound hyperplasia
(C) Cystic changes can be seen in a variety of thymus tumors
(D) In adults, thymoma is the most frequent primary tumor of the thymus; whereas, in children, lymphoma is the most common primary tumor of the thymus
??
**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】Thymic rebound hyperplasia
●
When the body is exposed to stress, the thymus may shrink to as little as
40% of its original volume. Once the body recovers, the thymus usually
grows back to its original size within 9 months; it can grow to be as much as
50% larger.
●
Rebound hyperplasia is commonly seen in children but also occurs in adults
●
Thymic rebound hyperplasia typically shows diffuse enlargement, a fine
mixture of fat and lymphoid tissue, a smooth contour, and normal vessels; in
contrast, thymic neoplasia is usually associated with a nodular contour and
frequently contains necrotic or calcified foci
