#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which of the following is **not** a classical radiological sign of normal pressure hydrocephalus (NPH)?
(A) Evans’ index > 0.3
(B) Acute callosal angle
(C) Cingulate sulcus sign
(D) “Knife-blade” configuration of gyri
??
**Ans: D**
想到Global cortical atrophy (GCA) scale 3
ABD都是NPH的特徵。
【2021 官方詳解】E. “Knife-blade” configuration of gyri is seen in frontotemporal atrophy (frontotemporal dementia, Pick’s
disease).
red = severe atrophy (knife-blade like)
Yellow = normal, spared.
Normal pressure hydrocephalus radiologic signs: Ventriculomegaly (Evan’s index>0.3, temporal horn lateral
ventricle >6mm, acute callosal angle); dilated Sylvian fissure, cingulate sulcus sign (posterior become
narrower than anterior cingulate sulcus).
