#交換 #2021交換 #US Which one of the following descriptions about Ultrasound findings of gallstones is not **true**?
(A) Posterior acoustic shadowing is one of the principal ultrasound imaging features of gallstones
(B) The wall-echo-shadow sign may be encountered when the gallstone is small
(C) Gallstones may also demonstrate twinkle artifact on Doppler imaging
(D) They tend to be hypovascular,
??
**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】B. The sign suggests either a large gallstone or multiple small gallstones filling the lumen of a contracted or
incompletely visualized gallbladder.
The wall-echo-shadow sign (also known as WES sign) is an ultrasonographic finding within the gallbladder
fossa:
-
a curvilinear hyperechogenic line representing the gallbladder wall a thin hypoechoic space
representing a small amount of bile
-
a curvilinear hyperechogenic line representing the near surface of gallstone(s)
-
and acoustic shadowing distal to the surface of the gallstone(s)
The sign suggests either a large gallstone or multiple small gallstones filling the lumen of a contracted or
incompletely visualized gallbladder. Recognizing this finding helps to avoid misinterpretation of a
stone-filled gallbladder as a loop of bowel.
