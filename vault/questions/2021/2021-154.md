#交換 #2021交換 #PED The following characteristic features of Sturge-Weber syndrome is correct?
(A) there is a “strawberry” nevus on the face
(B) the lesion is usually bilateral
(C) the intracerebral vascular lesion is usually well shown on arteriography
(D) calcification in the occipital cortex is visible on plain skull films
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
Sturge-Weber syndrome
Congenital facial cutaneous hemanioma (port wine stain or facial nevus flammeus), almost always involve CN V1.
*** Strawberry nervus -🡪 infantile hemangioma
Plain film: gyriform calcification of subcortical white matter
DXA: abscent of superficial cortical veins with abnormal and enlarged deep venous drainage (82%)
