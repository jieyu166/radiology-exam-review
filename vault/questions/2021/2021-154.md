#交換 #2021交換 #PED The following characteristic features of Sturge-Weber syndrome is correct?
(A) there is a “strawberry” nevus on the face
(B) the lesion is usually bilateral
(C) the intracerebral vascular lesion is usually well shown on arteriography
(D) calcification in the occipital cortex is visible on plain skull films
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。本題問何者「正確」。D 正確：Sturge-Weber 的腦回狀／tram-track 皮質下鈣化多位於頂枕（parieto-occipital）區，歷史上可於顱骨平片上顯示。A 錯：臉部為葡萄酒色斑（port-wine nevus／capillary malformation），而非草莓狀血管瘤（strawberry nevus，後者屬嬰兒型血管瘤）。B 錯：病灶約 85% 為單側（與色斑同側），並非通常雙側。C 錯：軟腦膜血管瘤為低流量病灶，動脈攝影常無法清楚顯示（DSA 反而顯示淺層皮質靜脈缺如、深部靜脈引流異常擴大）。
