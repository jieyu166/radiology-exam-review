#交換 #2021交換 #US Which of the description about the graft liver vessels is **wrong**?
(A) An elevated peak systolic velocity at hepatic artery anastomosis and tardus-pavus waveform at intra-hepatic artery suggesting hepatic artery stenosis
(B) A focal color aliasing at anastomosis, with >3-fold increase in velocity compared to pre-stenotic segment suggesting portal vein stenosis
(C) A biphasic or triphasic spetral Doppler pattern of hepatic veins suggesting patent venous outflow
(D) A resistence index of less than 0.45 and monophasic pattern of hepatic vein indicative of hepatic vein stenosis
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。本題要選「錯誤」敘述。
(D) 的概念方向雖對（肝靜脈呈 monophasic 且指數 <0.45 提示肝靜脈狹窄），但參數用錯了——正確應為「靜脈搏動指數（venous pulsatility index）<0.45」，而非題目所寫的「resistance index（RI）」；RI 用於評估動脈、不用於肝靜脈，故此敘述有誤。
其餘皆正確：(A) 吻合處收縮期峰值流速升高＋肝內動脈 tardus-parvus 波形提示肝動脈狹窄；(B) 吻合處局部 color aliasing＋流速較狹窄前段增加逾 3 倍提示門靜脈狹窄；(C) 肝靜脈維持 biphasic/triphasic 波形代表流出道通暢。
