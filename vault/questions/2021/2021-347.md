#交換 #2021交換 #CV About cardiac tamponade, which description about MR imaging findings is **wrong**?
(A) Diminished anteroposterior diameter (flattened heart sign)
(B) Collapse of RV free wall in early diastole
(C) Collapse of RA free wall in late diastole and early systole
(D) Inspiratory bounce of the intraventricular septum toward the right ventricle
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D（此題選「錯誤」敘述）。心包填塞的 MR 診斷核心是「鬆弛期心腔反轉」：B（右心室游離壁於早舒張期塌陷）與 C（右心房游離壁於晚舒張至早收縮期塌陷）皆正確；A（flattened heart sign，前後徑減小、心臟被壓扁）亦正確。D 為錯誤敘述：吸氣時室間隔的誇大擺動方向應為偏向「左心室（toward LV）」而非偏向右心室，且此徵象其實是心包束縛（constriction）的高特異表現，選項 D 方向寫反，故為錯的敘述。→ [[cardiac-tamponade]]
