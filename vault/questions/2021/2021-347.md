#交換 #2021交換 #CV About cardiac tamponade, which description about MR imaging findings is **wrong**?
(A) Diminished anteroposterior diameter (flattened heart sign)
(B) Collapse of RV free wall in early diastole
(C) Collapse of RA free wall in late diastole and early systole
(D) Inspiratory bounce of the intraventricular septum toward the right ventricle
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
Cardiac tamponade:
Diagnosed by findings of chamber inversion during the relaxation phase
(collapse of RV free wall in early diastole and RA free wall in late diastole and early systole)
Exaggerated flattening of ventricular septum toward LV during inspiration at real-time cine imaging
=> highly specific feature of pericardial constriction.
Ref: MRI of the Pericardium
https://doi.org/10.1148/rg.2019190081
