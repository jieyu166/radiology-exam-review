#交換 #2021交換 #Breast Which of the following statement about triangulation on mammogram is **true**?
(A) Regarding lesion found only on MLO view, lesions in medial breast will move up on ML.
(B) Regarding lesion found on MLO view, lesions in outer breast will move up on ML.
(C) Regarding lesion found on CC view, lesions in superior breast will move discordant to the direction which the top breast is rolled.
(D) For axillary tail lesions, exaggerated CC medial view would help.
??
**Ans: A**
答案 A。本題問乳房攝影三角定位（triangulation）何者「正確」，口訣「muffins rise, lead sinks」——由 MLO 換到 ML（true lateral）時，內側（medial）病灶上移、外側（lateral）病灶下移。（A）對：只見於 MLO 的病灶，若位於內側乳房，在 ML 上會「上移」（medial muffins rise）。（B）錯：外側（outer）病灶在 ML 上應「下移」（lateral lead sinks），並非上移。（C）錯：rolled CC view 中，上部病灶的移動方向與乳房上部被 roll 的方向「一致（concordant）」，而非相反。（D）錯：腋尾（axillary tail）病灶應以 exaggerated CC lateral（XCCL）而非 medial view 來顯示。→ [[mammography-positioning]]
