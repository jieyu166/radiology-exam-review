#交換 #2021交換 #2023交換 #NR Which is **not** one of signs of vocal cord paralysis at CT?
(A) Ipsilateral piriform sinus dilatation
(B) Medial rotation and thickening of the aryepiglottic fold
(C) Lateral displacement of ipsilateral arytenoids cartilage
(D) Ipsilateral laryngeal ventricle dilatation
??
**Ans: C**
ipsilateral arytenoids cartilage都被固定在前內側。
AD. 同側擴張
【2021 官方詳解】Most sensitive signs on axial CT of vocal cord paralysis (VCP):
ipsilateral piriform sinus dilatation                                      => (A)
medial rotation and thickening of the aryepiglottic fold => (B)
ipsilateral laryngeal ventricle dilatation                              => (D)
Another helpful sign of VCP:
Anteromedial displacement of the ipsilateral arytenoid cartilage with medial displacement of the posterior
vocal cord margin.
