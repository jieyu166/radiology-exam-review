#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Which one of the following tumors does not have matrix mineralization?
(A) Chondroblastoma
(B) Enchondroma
(C) Clear cell chondrosarcoma
(D) Giant cell tumor
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】Characteristic matrix mineralization patterns include:
●
fibrous – ground glass e.g. fibrous dysplasia
●
osteoblastic – cumulus cloud e.g. osteosarcoma, osteoid osteoma
●
chondroid – popcorn or arcs and rings e.g. chondrosarcoma, enchondroma
