#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Which one of the following tumors does not have matrix mineralization?
(A) Chondroblastoma
(B) Enchondroma
(C) Clear cell chondrosarcoma
(D) Giant cell tumor
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。GCT 無基質礦化、不產生礦化的腫瘤基質。A chondroblastoma、B enchondroma、C clear cell chondrosarcoma 皆屬軟骨源腫瘤，可見軟骨基質鈣化（ring-and-arc／popcorn），故三者皆有基質礦化。→ [[giant-cell-tumor]]
