#交換 #2021交換 #CH 以下關於COP(cryptogenic organizing pneumonia)之相關敘述，何者為非?
(A) ThehistologicpatternofCOPisorganizingpneumonia,formerlyreferred to as bronchiolitis obliterans (BO)
(B) Typically,theappearanceofthelungopacitiesvariesfromgroundglassto consolidation
(C) These opacities have a tendency to migrate, changing location and size
(D) reverse halo sign and the atoll sign are possible presentations
??
**Ans: A**
答案 A。COP 的組織型態為 organizing pneumonia，其舊稱應為 BOOP（bronchiolitis obliterans organizing pneumonia），後為避免與真正的小氣道阻塞性疾病混淆而捨棄此名。(A) 把舊稱寫成單純的「bronchiolitis obliterans（BO）」是錯的——BO（閉塞性／收縮性細支氣管炎）是另一種阻塞性小氣道病，與 COP 不同，故 (A) 為敘述錯誤者。(B) 肺部陰影由毛玻璃到實質化不等、(C) 病灶可移行（改變位置與大小）、(D) 可出現 reverse halo（atoll）sign，皆為 COP 正確且典型的表現。

## 詳解附圖（OCR）
**Cryptogenic Organizing Pneumonia**

COP is an IIP with characteristic clinical and radiologic features. The histologic pattern of COP is organizing pneumonia, formerly referred to as bronchiolitis obliterans organizing pneumonia (BOOP). The term *BOOP* has been omitted to avoid confusion with airway diseases such as constrictive bronchiolitis (3).
