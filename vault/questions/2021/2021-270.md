#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which of the following description is **false** regarding cerebral autosomal dominant arteriopathy with subcortical infarcts and leukoencephalopathy (CADASIL)?
(A) It is the most common single-gene disorder of cerebral small blood vessels
(B) It is related to NOTCH3 mutations
(C) MRI commonly show brain atrophy and diffuse white matter change
(D) MRI rarely show cerebral microbleeds
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。CADASIL 是 NOTCH3 基因（第 19 號染色體）突變造成的體染色體顯性小血管病，為最常見的單基因腦小血管病，故 A 正確；其致病基因確為 NOTCH3，B 正確；MRI 典型可見瀰漫白質高訊號與晚期腦萎縮，C 正確；D「MRI 少見腦微出血」與事實相反——在 GRE／SWI 上 CADASIL 的微出血相當常見（約 25–50%，隨年齡增加，最常群聚於視丘與深部灰質），故 D 為錯誤敘述。→ [[lacunar-infarction]]
