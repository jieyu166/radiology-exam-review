【2021 官方詳解】
- BRCA2 is tumor suppressor genes located on chromosomes 13
BRCA1 and BRCA2 are large tumor suppressor genes located on chromosomes 17 and 13, respectively.
Women with a BRCA mutation have a substantially greater risk of developing breast cancer compared with the general population, which has an average lifetime risk of 12.3%. According to recent estimates, 55%–65% of female BRCA1 mutation carriers and approximately 45% of female BRCA2 mutation carriers will develop breast cancer by age 70 years
