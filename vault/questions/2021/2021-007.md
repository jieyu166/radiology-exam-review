#交換 #2021交換 #Breast About the BRCA-associated breast cancers, which is **not** correct?
(A) BRCA1 is tumor suppressor genes located on chromosomes 17
(B) BRCA2 is tumor suppressor genes located on chromosomes 14
(C) Lifetime risk of carriers of BRCA1 gene is about 55%–65%
(D) Lifetime risk of carriers of BRCA2 gene is about 45%
??
**Ans: B**
答案 B。BRCA2 位於第 13 對染色體，而非第 14 對，故 B 為不正確敘述（記憶口訣：BRCA1→17、BRCA2→13）。其餘正確：(A) BRCA1 為位於第 17 對染色體的大型腫瘤抑制基因；(C) BRCA1 帶原者至 70 歲的終生乳癌風險約 55–65%；(D) BRCA2 帶原者約 45%；相較之下一般族群平均終生風險約 12.3%。→ [[breast-cancer-genetics]]
