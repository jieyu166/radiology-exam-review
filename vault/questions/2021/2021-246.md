#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which of the following is a described characteristic of sporadic duodenal villous lesions?
(A) They occur most commonly in the fourth portion of the duodenum
(B) They are commonly periampullary or ampullary when associated with FAP
(C) They most commonly manifest with obstruction
(D) They have imaging features that differ from those of colonic lesions
??
**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】In the duodenum, villous lesions can have an appearance similar to that of colonic villous lesions, but small
lesions are easily missed. Sporadic duodenal villous lesions are most common in the second portion of the
duodenum, and those associated with FAP are more commonly ampullary or periampullary. Small lesions
may appear as nonspecific intramural nodular filling defects. Larger lesions may have a soap-bubble or
cauliflower-like appearance. This appearance is that of multiple rounded radiolucent areas interspersed in a
fine lacework of radiopaque material owing to barium entering the clefts between frondlike projections, as
seen in the colon. Similarly, a frond- or lacelike mass in the duodenal lumen, with a feathery margin seen as
contrast material enters the interstices from different directions, has been described.
