#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Which ONE of the following image findings is NOT seen in Ollier disease on the radiograph?
(A) Lesions may contain foci of calcification
(B) Lesions may be intracortical and periosteal
(C) Lesions may protrude from the shaft of the tubular bone
(D) Lesions may extend from the growth plate to the epiphysis
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。⚠️ 此題問 Ollier 病 X 光「非」表現，各家文獻對此選項描述不一致，作答存疑。依概念判讀：Ollier 的內生軟骨瘤源自髓腔、主要位於幹骺端，隨生長可向骨幹遷移，但一般不會跨越生長板侵入骨骺（骨骺通常被保留），故「病灶由生長板延伸至骨骺」視為非典型表現。其餘皆屬 enchondromatosis 譜系可見：A 軟骨基質可有 rings-and-arcs／punctate 鈣化；B 病灶可呈皮質內或骨膜性軟骨瘤；C 病灶可自管狀骨骨幹膨出。

**Radiographic features**（Radiopaedia，enchondromatosis / Ollier）

Radiographs show multiple enchondromas. Larger lesions can show cartilage calcification in a typical rings and arcs pattern.

Vertical streaks of lucency also **present in the metaphysis of long bones, extending to the epiphysis**, which represents columns of cartilage.

For further discussion of appearances, please refer to the article on enchondromas.

**Pathology/Histology**

The macroscopic features of Ollier syndrome include islands of **benign resting cartilage within metaphyses and diaphyses**, which subsequently proliferate. Microscopically, calcifications, ranging from punctate to "popcorn" in appearance may develop within the cartilaginous matrix.
