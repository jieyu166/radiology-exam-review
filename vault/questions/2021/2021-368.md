(A) The most useful US findings for diagnosis of biliary atresia are the triangular cord sign, abnormal gallbladder morphology, and hypertrophy of the hepatic artery
(B) Congenital hepatic hemangiomas (CHHs) differ from infantile hepatic hemangiomas (IHHs) in that CHHs appear after birth, proliferate during the first few months of life, whereas IHHs tend to be fully formed at the time of birth

**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】B.
Infantile hepatic hemangiomas (IHHs) differ from congenital  hepatic hemangiomas (CHHs)
IHHs appear after birth, proliferate during the first few months of life, and are treated with propranolol
- Gradually involute over the next few years
- Unifocal (rare), multifocal (multiple lesions with intervening normal parenchyma), or diffuse (innumerable
lesions that replace the parenchyma)
CHHs tend to be fully formed at the time of birth and are not responsive to propranolol.
-  A solitary large mass
- Three patterns of growth
rapidly involuting, noninvoluting, and partially involuting
