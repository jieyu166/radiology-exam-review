#交換 #2021交換 #Breast Which benign breast lesion has high risk for the subsequent development of invasive breast cancer:
(A) Sclerosing adenosis
(B) Atypical hyperplasia
(C) Squamous metaplasia
(D) Papillary hyperplasia
??
**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】
Ref: https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/
- As a single feature, increased risk of cancer of 1.5 - 2x, as seen with proliferative fibrocystic changes (Cancer 1989;64:1977)
- atypical (ductal) hyperplasia: 4 - 5x risk of developing ductal carcinoma in situ within 5 years (Cancer Prev Res (Phila) 2014;7:211)
