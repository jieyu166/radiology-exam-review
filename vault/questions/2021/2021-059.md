#交換 #2020交換 #2021交換 #NR Which description about hypothalamic hamartoma is **WRONG**?
(A) The lesion can cause gelastic seizures
(B) The lesion usually shows strong enhancement
(C) Precocious puberty is a possible symptom
(D) On T2WI images, the lesion is usually iso- to hyper-intense to the cerebral cortex
??
**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】
Hypothalamic hamartoma
Clinical presentation: gelastic seizures, visual problems, early onset of puberty and behavioral problems
MR:
- T1: isointense to cerebral cortex
- T1 C+ (Gd): no contrast enhancement
- T2
  - iso- to hyperintense to the cerebral cortex
  - the higher the proportion of glial cells, the higher the T2 signal
