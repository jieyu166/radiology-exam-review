#交換 #2021交換 #2023交換 #Physics 在臨床上使用含釓（Gd）的磁振造影對比劑，其最主要目的為何?
(A) 縮短 T1弛緩時間 (shortening T1 relaxation time)
(B) 增長 T1弛緩時間 (prolong T1 relaxation time)
(C) 縮短 T2弛緩時間 (shortening T2 relaxation time)
(D) 增長 T2弛緩時間 (prolong T2 relaxation time
??
**Ans: A**
Gd contrast agent ==> shortening T1 relaxation time ==> T1WI Hyperintensity
- 但Gado也會有shortening T2 relaxation time的效果，T2訊號會降低。高濃度時較明顯(Radiopaedia)
【2021 官方詳解】
A. Gd contrast agent => shortening T1 relaxation time=> Hyperintensity in T1WI
The size of Mz, the z-component of tissue magnetization (M) before the RF-pulse.
The size of Mxy, the transverse components of M after the RF-pulse (when the signal is recorded).
T1 reflects the length of time it takes for regrowth of Mz back toward its initial maximum value (Mo). Tissues with short T1's recover more quickly than those with long T1's.
Their Mz values are larger, producing a stronger signal and brighter spot on the MR image inT1WI
T2 reflects the length of time it takes for the MR signal (Mxy) to decay in the transverse plane.
A short T2 means that the signal decays very rapidly.
So substances with short T2's have smaller signals and appear darker than substances with longer T2 values in T2WI
