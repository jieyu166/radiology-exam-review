#交換 #2021交換 #PED Which of the statements about the PRETEXT (PRE-Treatment EXTent of tumor) system is **wrong**?
(A) The PRETEXT groups are a powerful predictor of overall survival in children with hepatoblastoma and hepatocellular carcinoma
(B) The PRETEXT group (I, II, III or IV) is based on determining the number of contiguous tumor-free liver sections
(C) Tumor in caudate lobe only is classified as PRETEXT I
(D) The lungs are the most common site of distant metastases in children with hepatoblastoma and hepatocellular carcinoma
??
**Ans: C**
【2021 官方詳解】
C.
If tumor only involves the caudate lobe, it should be considered a PRETEXT II tumor by convention
Pediatric liver tumor staging (PRETEXT grouping system)
Used for any tumor lesion of the liver in children
The liver segments are grouped into sections:
left lateral section: segments 2 and 3
left medial section: segments 4a and 4b
right anterior section: segments 5 and 8
right posterior section: segments 6 and 7
(Only caudate lobe involvement => PRETEXT II)
PRETEXT I : one section is involved and three adjoining sections are free
PRETEXT II : one or two sections are involved, but two adjoining sections are free
PRETEXT III : tumor involves three liver sections and one liver section is free of tumor or tumor involves
two liver sections and two non-adjoining liver sections are free of tumor
PRETEXT IV: all four sections are involved, there is no liver section free of tumor
PRETEXT system divides patients into two risk groups:
high-risk group :
PRETEXT number IV
serum alpha fetoprotein <100 μg/L or very high AFP (>1 million ng/ml)
presence of additional PRETEXT criteria from groups E, H, M, N, P, and V
the rest of patients belong to the standard risk group
additional categories
caudate lobe involvement ( C)
extrahepatic abdominal disease (E)
tumor focality (F)
tumor rupture or intraperitoneal hemorrhage (H)
distant metastases (M)
lymph node metastases (N)
portal vein involvement (P)
involvement of the IVC and/or hepatic veins (V)
