#交換 #2021交換 #NR What is the most commonly observed brain MR abnormality pattern (excluding ischemic infarct) in patients with severe coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19)?
(A) non-confluent multifocal white matter hyperintense lesions on FLAIR/diffusion, with variable enhancement, associated with hemorrhagic lesions
(B) extensive and isolated white matter microhemorrhages
(C) extensive and confluent supratentorial white matter FLAIR hyperintensities
(D) unilateral FLAIR and/or diffusion hyperintensities located in mesial temporal lobe
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。依 Kremer 等（Radiology 2020）37 例重症 COVID-19 系列，扣除缺血性梗塞後，最常見的實質異常是內側（mesial）顳葉 FLAIR 和／或 擴散高訊號（約 43%），故選 D。(A) 非融合性多灶白質 FLAIR/DWI 病灶、可變強化併出血約 30%，居次；(B) 廣泛而孤立的白質微出血約 24%，再次之；(C) 廣泛融合性幕上白質 FLAIR 高訊號並非該研究描述的主要型態。→ [[covid-19-brain-mri]]
