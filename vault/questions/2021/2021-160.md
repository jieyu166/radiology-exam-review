#交換 #2021交換 #CV 有關pre procedure CT for Percutaneous aortic valve implantation 下列何者為 非?
(A) 因為要使影像清楚 scan 前最好使用 b blockeer 及NTG
(B) 必須針對 aortic valve, annulus, sinus 及 access artery 評估
(C) 因為要評估 aortic valve 所以要有一組 cardiac motion free 的影像最好 用 ECG gating
(D) 因為 systolic phase aortic valve 最大, 所以測量 valve 以 systolic phase 為 主
??
**Ans: A**
【2021 官方詳解】Standardisation of scanning protocols
A contrast enhanced ECG gated or triggered scan of the aortic root (include LVOT, snnulus, sinus of Valsalva,
sinotubular junction, ascending aorta, coronary ostia)
Contrstructed with 1mm or less slice thickeness
At least 64 slice or dual source scanner is required
At least including a systolic phase
At least from subclavian arteries to superficial femoral arteries
NTG and beta blockers are contraindicated in severe aortic stenosis and should not be administered prior to
scanning.
