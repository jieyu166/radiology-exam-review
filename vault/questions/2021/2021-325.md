#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which of the following statement about tuberculous peritonitis is incorrect?
(A) Risk factors for abdominal TB include cirrhosis, HIV infection, diabetes mellitus, malignancy, and use of peritoneal dialysis
(B) The most common forms include involvement of the peritoneum, intestine, and/or liver
(C) Intestinal TB most commonly demonstrates concentric mural thickening in the ileoceal region
(D) Lymphadenopathy usually demonstrates homogeneous solid centers over the mesentery and retroperitoneum
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。結核性淋巴結腫的典型表現為低衰減、中央乾酪化壞死（可逐漸囊變），淋巴結彼此糾結而周圍發炎變化輕微，並非如選項 D 所述之「均質實心中心（homogeneous solid centers）」，故 D 為錯誤敘述（正解）。其餘皆正確：腹腔結核的危險因子確含肝硬化、HIV 感染、糖尿病、惡性腫瘤與腹膜透析（A）；最常見的侵犯型態為腹膜、腸道與/或肝臟（B）；腸道結核以迴盲部環周壁增厚最為常見（C）。→ [[gi-infection-location-pattern]]
