#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which of the following descriptions of spontaneous intracranial hypotension is INCORRECT?
(A) Diffuse pachymeningeal enhancement is common in patients with SIH
(B) In patients with SIH, the angle between midbrain, pons increases
(C) Narrowing of midbrain-pons angle predicts poor response to blood patch treatment
(D) According to ICHD-3, SIH diagnosis requires measurement of low CSF pressure and/or evidence of CSF leak on neuroimaging (CT myelography or MR myelography)
??
**Ans: B**
答案 B。自發性顱內低壓（SIH）因腦幹下沉（sagging brain），中腦－橋腦夾角（pontomesencephalic angle）會「縮小」（<50° 提示顱內低壓）而非增大，故 B 敘述「夾角增大」為錯誤、為本題答案。（A）瀰漫性硬腦膜（pachymeningeal）平滑均勻強化為 SIH 常見表現，正確。（C）夾角縮小被認為可預測硬膜外血液貼片（blood patch）治療反應不佳；⚠️ 存疑：選項 C 的預後宣稱僅溯及 Brain 2017、Tier-1（AJR2013/AJNR2020）無法證實此因果，宜視為待覆核之較弱論述。（D）依 ICHD-3，SIH 診斷需有低 CSF 壓力測量值及／或影像上（CT／MR myelography）CSF 滲漏證據，正確。→ [[spontaneous-intracranial-hypotension]]
