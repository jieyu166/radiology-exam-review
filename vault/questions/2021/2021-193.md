#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which statement about Cryoablation for renal tumor is correct?
(A) “Rapid” cooling results in more “intracellular” ice crystal
(B) “Slow” thawing results in microvascular occlusion and cell hypoxia
(C) Critical temperature threshold for cell death is between −19.4°C and −40°C
(D) An ice ball margin should be at least 5mm larger than the target tumor
(E) All statements are correct
??
**Ans: E**
答案 E。冷凍消融的四項敘述皆正確、故選以上皆是。A 正確：快速冷卻會在細胞內形成較多冰晶，具直接細胞毒性。B 正確：緩慢解凍造成微血管阻塞與細胞缺氧，導致間接缺血傷害。C 正確：細胞死亡具時間與溫度依賴性，臨界致死溫度約介於 −19.4°C 至 −40°C。D 正確：冰球邊界應超出目標腫瘤至少 5 mm，以避免殘餘或未治療的腫瘤。→ [[tumor-ablation]]
