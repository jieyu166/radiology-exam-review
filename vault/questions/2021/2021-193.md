#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which statement about Cryoablation for renal tumor is correct?
(A) “Rapid” cooling results in more “intracellular” ice crystal
(B) “Slow” thawing results in microvascular occlusion and cell hypoxia
(C) Critical temperature threshold for cell death is between −19.4°C and −40°C
(D) An ice ball margin should be at least 5mm larger than the target tumor
(E) All statements are correct
??
**Ans: E**
【2021 官方詳解】Cryoablation makes use of rapid cooling to cause cell death. Two sequential and synergistic
mechanisms lead to cell death. Intra- and extracellular ice crystals are directly cytotoxic and
lead to cell dehydration and rupture. When the frozen tissue is thawed, there is microvascular
occlusion with cell hypoxia, resulting in indirect ischemic injury.
An initial freeze followed by thawing and additional freezing (double freeze-thaw cycle) results in a larger
amount of liquefaction necrosis and improves treatment efficacy.
Cell death is time- and temperature dependent, with the critical threshold for cell death being between
−19.4°C and −40°C.
the goal should be an ice ball margin of at least 5 mm to avoid residual or untreated tumor.
