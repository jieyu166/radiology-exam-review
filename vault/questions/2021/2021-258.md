#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Which one of the following skeletal sites is LESS common seen in patients with an Ewing sarcoma?
(A) Iliac bone
(B) Scapula
(C) Rib
(D) Clavicle
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
表示方式有兩種:
- lower limb: 45% (femur most common)
- pelvis: 20%
- upper limb: 13%
- spine and ribs: 13% (the sacrococcygeal region most common)
- skull/face: 2%
或者:
- long bones: 50-60%
  - femur: 25%
  - tibia: 11%
  - humerus: 10%
- flat bones: 40%
  - pelvis: 14%
  - scapula
  - ribs: 6%
