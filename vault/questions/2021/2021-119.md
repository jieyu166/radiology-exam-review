(A) Low density on CT
(B) Hypointense on T1-weighted images
(C) Extremely hyperintense on T2-weighted images, similar to water
(D) Usually only peripheral enhancement
??

matrix
OnCT,intramuscularmyxomasappearaswell-definedlow-densitylesionswithminimalto mild contrast
enhancement. On MRI, the lesions are well circumscribed with smooth or slightly lobulated margins,
