#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Which of the following statement is **wrong** for intramuscular myxoma?
(A) Low density on CT
(B) Hypointense on T1-weighted images
(C) Extremely hyperintense on T2-weighted images, similar to water
(D) Usually only peripheral enhancement
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
D. Heterogenous or avid enhancement due to the presence of abundant vascularity within the myxoid matrix
On CT, intramuscular myxomas appear as well-defined low-density lesions with minimal to mild contrast enhancement. On MRI, the lesions are well circumscribed with smooth or slightly lobulated margins, homogeneously hypointense on T1-weighted images, and extremely hyperintense on T2weighted images, similar to water . On contrast-enhanced images, lesions show variable heterogeneous, sometimes avid, contrast enhancement due to the presence of abundant vascularity within the myxoid matrix
