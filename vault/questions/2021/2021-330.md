#交換 #2021交換 #CV Which of the following statements comparing CMR with other noninvasive imaging technologies is correct?
(A) Clinical CMR can image the coronary arteries with superior spatial resolution compared with 64-detector MDCT
(B) Clinical CMR cine functional imaging has higher temporal resolution than M-mode echocardiography
(C) CMR is better than transthoracic echocardiography in assessing valvular structure
(D) Flow measurements by CMR are more reliable than those from transthoracic echocardiography in assessing calcific aortic valvular stenosis
(E) Flow measurements by CMR are more reliable than those from transthoracic echocardiography in assessing aortic valvular insufficiency
??
**Ans: E**
答案 E。相位對比（phase-contrast）CMR 可直接量測主動脈瓣的逆流量與逆流分率，對主動脈瓣「逆流」的定量比經胸超音波（TTE）更可靠，故 E 正確。A 錯：臨床 CMR 對冠狀動脈的空間解析度不及 64 切 MDCT，冠狀動脈評估整體遜於心臟 CT。B 錯：時間解析度以 M-mode 超音波最高，CMR cine 功能造影並未超過。C 錯：評估瓣膜「結構」上 TTE 仍相當好，非 CMR 強項。D 錯：主動脈瓣「狹窄」的高速噴流易 aliasing，CMR 流量量測於狹窄反而較不可靠，逆流才是其強項。→ [[coronary-mra-technique]]
