#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Another name for the Myenteric plexus is the ___.
(A) Submucosal plexus
(B) Brachial plexus
(C) Auerbach’s plexus
(D) Lumbar plexus
??
**Ans: C**
【2021 官方詳解】
Auerbach plexus, also known by the name of myenteric plexus, is a group of ganglia that run throughout the entire gastrointestinal tract and innervate its multiple layers of smooth muscle
