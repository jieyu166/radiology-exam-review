#交換 #2021交換 #ABD At barium examinations, villous lesions have which of the following characteristics?
(A) A narrow base
(B) Numerous polypoid protrusions into the bowel lumen
(C) An annular configuration causing luminal narrowing and obstruction
(D) Poor coating or covering with barium
??
**Ans: B**
答案 B。鋇劑攝影下 villous lesion 呈寬基（broad-based），表面有多個 polypoid 突起伸入腸腔，鋇劑滲入突起間隙形成 lace／frond／soap-bubble 花樣，故 B 正確。A 錯：為寬基而非窄基；C 錯：環狀狹窄阻塞屬晚期或惡變表現，非典型 villous lesion；D 錯：鋇劑會困陷於絨毛間隙，而非覆蓋不良。→ [[gi-villous-lesions]]
