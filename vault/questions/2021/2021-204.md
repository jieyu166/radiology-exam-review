#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Which one is correct about Three-Column Classification System for tibial plateau fractures?
(A) Zero-column tibial plateau fractures are pure articular surface depression fractures with no associated cleavage-type fracture
(B) One-column tibial plateau fractures involve only the medial, lateral, or posterior column, with a fracture line disrupting the cortical surface of the column wall
(C) Two-column fractures involve two of the three columns and must have two discrete fracture fragments, each of which has a fracture line extending to the cortical surface of the involved column
(D) Three-column fractures involve all three columns of the tibial plateau, with fracture lines in each column extending to the cortical surface of the respective column wall
(E) All above are correct
??
**Ans: E**
【2021 官方詳解】
Zero-column tibial plateau fractures are pure articular surface depression fractures with no associated cleavage-type fracture.
One-column tibial plateau fractures involve only the medial, lateral, or posterior column, with a fracture line disrupting the cortical surface of the column wall. The key to diagnosis is the presence of a split fracture with a single separate fragment in the involved column.
Two-column fractures involve two of the three columns and must have two discrete fracture fragments, each of which has a fracture line extending to the cortical surface of the involved column. For example, a lateral tibial plateau fracture would be considered a two-column fracture if there is an anterolateral fracture plus a separate posterolateral fracture fragment. This distinction is important because recent orthopedic literature advocates for an altered surgical approach to allow dorsal plating of a separate posterolateral fracture fragment for the best surgical outcome.
Three-column fractures involve all three columns of the tibial plateau, with fracture lines in each column extending to the cortical surface of the respective column wall. Three-column fractures are typically the result of high-energy trauma, resulting in highly comminuted fractures. These fractures must have at least one separate fracture fragment in each column
