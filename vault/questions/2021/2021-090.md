#交換 #2021交換 #PED About typical Imaging Findings of Fetal Liver Tumor, which of the following is not appropriate?
(A) Arterial venous malformations or fistulas : Multiple enlarged intrahepatic vessels
(B) Intrahepatic meconium pseudocysts: Related to perforation, multiple intrahepatic complex cysts
(C) Malignant Hepatoblastoma: Solid, spoke-wheel, vascular multilobular mass with or without calcifications
(D) Metastatic neuroblastoma : Homogeneous hypoechoic nodule with or without vascularity
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。本題問胎兒肝腫瘤典型影像何者「不恰當」。D 錯在回音型態：胎兒肝的轉移性 neuroblastoma 應為 heterogeneous hyperechoic（不均質高回音）結節、可有或無血流，而非題目所寫的 homogeneous hypoechoic。A 正確：動靜脈畸形／瘻管表現為多條擴張的肝內血管。B 正確：肝內胎便假囊腫與（腸）穿孔相關、呈多發複雜囊腫。C 正確：hepatoblastoma 為實質性、輻輪狀（spoke-wheel）、富血管的多分葉腫塊，可含或不含鈣化。
