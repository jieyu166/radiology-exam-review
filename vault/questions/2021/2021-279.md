#交換 #2021交換 #NR For cholesterol granuloma of middle ear, which one is less likely?
(A) Smooth, expansile mass of middle ear
(B) High T1 signal due to cholesterol component and methemoglobin
(C) Hyperintense on b=1000 non-echo planar DWI series
(D) The most common cystic lesion of the petrous apex
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。膽固醇肉芽腫典型不會擴散受限，non-EPI DWI 上呈高訊號／擴散受限者應想 cholesteatoma 或 epidermoid，故「b=1000 non-EPI DWI 高訊號」對膽固醇肉芽腫最不可能，C 為答案；A 正確，為中耳平滑膨脹性腫塊；B 正確，因膽固醇成分與 methemoglobin 而呈 T1 高訊號；D 正確，膽固醇肉芽腫是岩尖最常見的囊性病灶。→ [[cholesterol-granuloma-petrous-apex]]
