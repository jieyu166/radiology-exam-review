#交換 #2021交換 #CV Which of the following descriptions about aortoiliac occlusive disease is **wrong**?
(A) It is also known as Leriche syndrome
(B) It is characterized by severe atherosclerotic stenosis or occlusion from suprarenal aorta to iliac arteries
(C) Many patients remain asymptomatic due to the development of robust collateral networks
(D) It is a type D lesion according to the Trans Atlantic Inter Society Consensus II (TASC II) and surgery is recommended as the treatment of choice
??
**Ans: B**
答案 B。主動脈髂動脈阻塞病（Leriche syndrome）侵犯的是「腎動脈以下（infrarenal）」的腹主動脈及/或髂動脈，並非從腎上（suprarenal）主動脈開始，因此 B 的「from suprarenal aorta」為錯誤敘述，為本題所選。其餘皆正確：(A) 本病又稱 Leriche syndrome，典型三聯為跛行、股動脈搏動消失與陽痿；(C) 因側枝循環發達（如腰動脈-髂動脈側枝），不少病人可長期無明顯症狀；(D) 依 TASC II 分類，瀰漫性的腎下主動脈髂動脈阻塞屬 type D 病灶，傳統上以外科繞道手術為首選治療。→ [[pad-peripheral-arterial]]

**Table F1. TASC classification of aorto-iliac lesions**

| Type | Lesions |
|---|---|
| Type A lesions | Unilateral or bilateral stenoses of CIA；Unilateral or bilateral single short (≤3 cm) stenosis of EIA |
| Type B lesions | Short (≤3 cm) stenosis of infrarenal aorta；Unilateral CIA occlusion；Single or multiple stenosis totaling 3–10 cm involving the EIA not extending into the CFA；Unilateral EIA occlusion not involving the origins of internal iliac or CFA |
| Type C lesions | Bilateral CIA occlusions；Bilateral EIA stenoses 3–10 cm long not extending into the CFA；Unilateral EIA stenosis extending into the CFA；Unilateral EIA occlusion that involves the origins of internal iliac and/or CFA；Heavily calcified unilateral EIA occlusion with or without involvement of origins of internal iliac and/or CFA |
| Type D lesions | Infra-renal aortoiliac occlusion；Diffuse disease involving the aorta and both iliac arteries requiring treatment；Diffuse multiple stenoses involving the unilateral CIA, EIA and CFA；Unilateral occlusions of both CIA and EIA；Bilateral occlusions of EIA；Iliac stenoses in patients with AAA requiring treatment and not amenable to endograft placement or other lesions requiring open aortic or iliac surgery |

CIA = common iliac artery；EIA = external iliac artery；CFA = common femoral artery；AAA = abdominal aortic aneurysm。

**Recommendation 36 — Treatment of aortoiliac lesions**
- TASC A and D lesions: Endovascular therapy is the treatment of choice for type A lesions and surgery is the treatment of choice for type D lesions [C].
- TASC B and C lesions: Endovascular treatment is the preferred treatment for type B lesions and surgery is the preferred treatment for good-risk patients with type C lesions. The patient's co-morbidities, fully informed patient preference and the local operator's long-term success rates must be considered when making treatment recommendations for type B and type C lesions [C].
