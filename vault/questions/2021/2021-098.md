#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which of the followings about angiomyolipoma (AML) is **wrong**?
(A) 80% of patients with tuberous sclerosis have AML
(B) AML less than 4cm is unlikely to bleed
(C) AML should be considered when renal mass with well enhanced components and calcifications are demonstrated on CT
(D) AML may appear as hypovascular mass on angiography
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。AML 罕見鈣化，故「腎腫塊有明顯強化成分＋鈣化」時反而應警覺 RCC（鈣化亦見於脂肪肉瘤），以此推論 AML 並不恰當，C 為錯誤敘述、本題正解。A 結節性硬化症病人約 70–80% 有 AML（原述 80% 可接受）、B AML <4 cm 較不易出血（出血風險與 ≥4 cm 相關）、D 非典型／少脂 AML 於血管攝影可呈乏血管表現，皆屬可接受敘述。→ [[renal-angiomyolipoma]]
