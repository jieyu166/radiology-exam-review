#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which of the finding about the acute renal infarction can be seen in the computed tomography?
(A) Cortical rim sign
(B) Edematous enlargement
(C) Wedged-shaped area of absent enhancement
(D) All of the above
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
Acute renal infarction
CT:
Wedge-shaped area of absent enhancement
Edematous enlargement of kidney (with large infarction)
Cortical rum sign within several days after global infarction
US:
Normal echogenicity (Echoes appear within 7 days)
Disminished echogenicity (within 24hr)
