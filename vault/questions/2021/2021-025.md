#交換 #2021交換 #CH Which of the following tracheal disorder is characterized by nodularity of tracheal wall with sparing of the posterior tracheal wall?
(A) Relapsing polychondritis
(B) Tracheobronchial papillomatosis
(C) Mournier-Kuhn disease
(D) Tracheobronchopathia osteochondroplastica
??

**TABLE 1: Imaging Findings of Various Diffuse Tracheal Diseases**

| Tracheal Disease | CT Appearance | Tracheal Narrowing | Wall Thickened | Wall Calcification | Useful Differentiators |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tracheomalacia | Severe expiratory collapse of the tracheal lumen; lunate-shaped trachea | Yes | No | No | Tracheal collapse; history of predisposing condition or trauma |
| Saber-sheath trachea | Decreased coronal diameter with concomitant increase in sagittal diameter | Yes | No | No | Characteristic bullet-shaped trachea on axial CT; history or imaging findings suggesting COPD |
| Relapsing polychondritis | Thickening of the cartilaginous trachea with sparing of the posterior membrane | Yes | Yes | Sometimes | Sparing of the posterior trachea; cartilaginous abnormalities of the ears or nose |
| Tracheobronchopathia osteochondroplastica | Calcified or ossified nodules in the cartilaginous trachea with sparing of the posterior membrane | Yes | Yes | Always | Nodular calcified or nodular ossified tracheal wall thickening sparing the posterior tracheal membrane and superior trachea |
| Amyloidosis | Calcified or ossified nodular or concentric tracheal wall thickening | Yes | Yes | Often present | Concentric calcified or ossified nodular or concentric wall thickening without posterior sparing; may involve the larynx and upper trachea |
| Wegener granulomatosis | Circumferential tracheal wall thickening or cartilage defects; subglottic region most often involved | Yes | Yes | No | Subglottic narrowing when focal; history of sinus or renal disease, pulmonary cavitary nodules, or pulmonary hemorrhage |
| Mounier-Kuhn syndrome | Tracheal diameter > 3 cm; main bronchial diameter > 2.4 cm; scalloping of tracheal wall and diverticula | No | No | No | Only diffuse tracheal abnormality resulting in both anteroposterior and lateral tracheal dilatation |
| Sarcoidosis | Secondary tracheal narrowing from lymphadenopathy or primary tracheal narrowing from tracheal noncaseating granulomas | Yes | Yes | Yes | Hilar and mediastinal lymphadenopathy; perilymphatic pulmonary nodules |
| Inflammatory bowel disease | Diffuse or focal tracheal narrowing | Yes | Yes | No | History of inflammatory bowel disease or chronic abdominal pain |
