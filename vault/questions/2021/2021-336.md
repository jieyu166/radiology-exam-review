**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】
(以上皆對) Ref: Review of axillary web syndrome: What the radiologist should know, European Journal of Radiology
Axillary web syndrome
common after axillary surgery, frequently affecting breast cancer patients.
one or more linear bands of firm tissue, also known as “cords”, in the axilla and arm, associated with pain and limited range of motion of the shoulder and arm
Etiology:
lymphovenous tissue damage with disruption of superficial lymphatics and veins during surgery, resulting in superficial venous and lymphatic stasis and local hypercoagulability and thrombosis.
US: deformation of the dermis with shoulder abduction, an anechoic cord with hyperechoic edge, likely the lumen of a vessel and vesselwall, and hyperechoic structures thought to represent fibrosis.
no flow on Doppler images.
MRI: the cords were visible as low signal subcutaneous linear bands or dots
