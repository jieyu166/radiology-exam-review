#交換 #2021交換 #PED A well performed pediatric voiding cystourethrography allows visualization of disease and detection of most common pathologic conditions with very low radiation exposure. Which of the following description is **incorrect**?
(A) In boys, the external sphincter is the most common site of resistance to catheter advancement.
(B) A ureterocele or bladder tumor may only be seen during early filling and may become obscured as more contrast material enters the bladder.
(C) Approximately 20% of reflux will be missed if voiding does not occur.
(D) All grades of reflux (international reflux system) can be outgrown in both infant and pediatric patients except for grade V, which requires surgery.
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。本題問何者敘述「錯誤」。D 過於絕對而錯：各級 VUR 皆可能自行緩解（outgrown），只是低級別緩解更頻繁、所需時間更短；即使 grade V 也非「一律需手術」——嬰兒的 grade V 已被證實可自行縮小、消退，故不能說「除 grade V 外皆可 outgrown、grade V 一律開刀」。A 正確：男嬰在導管推進時最常於外括約肌處遇到阻力。B 正確：ureterocele 或膀胱腫瘤僅在早期充盈時可見，對比劑充填增多後易被遮蔽。C 正確：若未涵蓋排尿期影像，約 20% 的逆流會被漏診。
