#交換 #2021交換 #CV 有關 pulmonary venous (PV) anatomy 下列何者為非? RSPV: right superior pulmonary vein LSPV: left superior pulmonary vein RIPV: right inferior pulmonary vein LIPV: left inferior pulmonary vein RUL: right upper lobe RML: right middle lobe
(A) 大部分情況有 有四條 主要PV drained 至 LA
(B) 大部分情況RUL 及 RML 的 PV drained 至 RSPV
(C) 大部分情況下 四條主要PV 中RSPV 管徑最大
(D) 大部分情況下四條主要PV中 RIPV管徑最小
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
Pulmonary Vein and Left Atrium Anatomy (Two superior two inferior)
Right superior vein: RUL + RML
Left superior vein: LUL (+lingual)
Inferior vein: lower lobes
Largest: right superior pulmonary vein
Cronin and coworkers: RSPV: ostia was 17.6 mm; the LSPV ostia was 16.6 mm, compared with 17.1 mm for the RIPV ostia, and 14.8 mm for theLIPV
Variant:
Common trunk (left more common)
Accessory vein (Right: separate RMPV, LMPV for lingula )
