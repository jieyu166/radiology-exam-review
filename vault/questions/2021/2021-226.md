【2021 官方詳解】●
By definition, a progressive fibrosing ILD characterised by decline in lung function and early mortality.
Most clinical trials and observational studies in patients with ILDs have defined disease progression in
terms of decline in FVC, measured as the change from baseline in mL or as a percentage of the
predicted value, or as a categorical change (typically ⩾10% predicted), or as a composite of categorical
change and mortality.
●
Patients with certain other types of chronic fibrosing ILD are also at risk of developing a progressive
phenotype. These include idiopathic nonspecific interstitial pneumonia (NSIP), unclassifiable
idiopathic interstitial pneumonia, autoimmune ILDs, chronic sarcoidosis, chronic hypersensitivity
pneumonitis (HP) and exposure-related diseases such as asbestosis and silicosis
●
Treatment of autoimmune ILDs usually involves corticosteroids or other immunosuppressants such as
azathioprine, CYC or MMF, based on clinical experience rather than scientific evidence.
