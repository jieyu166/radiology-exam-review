#交換 #2021交換 #MSK What is the recommended treatment for adult idiopathic scoliosis with a Cobb angle 25°?
(A) leave alone
(B) observation
(C) bracing
(D) surgery
??
**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】
Observation:
Adolescent: Cobb angle <20°
Adult: Cobb angle < 30°
Bracing:
Adolescent: 20-45°
Surgery:
Adolescent: Cobb angle >45° Adult: Cobb angle > 45° ofr
