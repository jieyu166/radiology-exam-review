#交換 #2021交換 #Breast Which one is the most reliable characteristic of breast cancer on MRI:
(A) Irregular shape
(B) Rapidly enhanced pattern with rapidly washout on dynamic study
(C) DWI-hyperintense mass
(D) Heterogenous enhancement
??
**Ans: A**
答案 A。本題問乳癌在 MRI 上「最可靠」的特徵。（A）對：形態學上的不規則/針狀形狀（irregular shape）是最可靠且獨立的惡性判準，形態優先於動力學。（B）錯：快速強化併快速 washout（type III 動力學）雖提示惡性，但須與形態並用，單看強化率特異度低，良性病灶（如纖維腺瘤、乳突瘤）亦可出現。（C）錯：DWI 高訊號（擴散受限）為輔助線索，特異度較低，良性病灶亦可呈現。（D）錯：異質性強化（heterogeneous enhancement）特異度較低，非最可靠指標。

**Breast MRI（BI-RADS MRI descriptors）**

- **Focus and foci**：< 5mm - cannot be specified
- **Masses**
  - **Shape**：round - oval - lobulated - irregular (32% ca)
  - **Margin**：smooth - irregular - spiculated (80% ca)
  - **T1** ↑：lymphnode - fat necrosis - hamartoma
  - **T2 fatsat** ↑：cyst - lymphnode - fibroadenoma - fat necrosis - colloid carcinoma
  - **Enhancement**：homogeneous - rim - septa etc.
  - **Kinetics**：type I (6% ca) - type II - type III (29-77% ca)
- **Nonmass-like enhancement**：Linear - ductal - segmental - clumped etc.
