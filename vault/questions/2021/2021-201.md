#交換 #2021交換 #MSK About temporomandibular joint (TMJ), which one is “**WRONG**”?
(A) The normal TMJ disk has an asymmetric biconcave configuration and the anterior band of the disk is usually smaller than the posterior band
(B) Internal derangements of the TMJ occur more often in young women
(C) Anterior displacement of TMJ disc is diagnosed according to sagittal MR images with mouth opening
(D) An erosion of the condyle may be the earliest osseous manifestation of degenerative changes of the TMJ
??
**Ans: C**
【2021 官方詳解】The study should include oblique sagittal spin and gradient echo T2 WIs on each TMJ separately both in
open and closed mouth positions
Direct signs of disc displacement include abnormal orientation in open and closed mouth positions.
●
anterior disc displacement: is the commonest abnormality and includes anterior disc displacement in
closed mouth that reduces in open mouth and a non reduced anterior displacement.
●
posterior disc displacement: occurs when the posterior band is displaced posteriorly and exceeds 1
o'clock position.
●
stuck disc: occurs when the disc fails to displace in open or closed mouth position and becomes fixed to
the temporal bone due to adhesion.
Indirect signs of internal derangement include large joint effusion, rupture of retrodiscal layers, thickening
of lateral pterygoid muscle attachment and osteoarthritic changes.
