#交換 #2021交換 #MSK About temporomandibular joint (TMJ), which one is “**WRONG**”?
(A) The normal TMJ disk has an asymmetric biconcave configuration and the anterior band of the disk is usually smaller than the posterior band
(B) Internal derangements of the TMJ occur more often in young women
(C) Anterior displacement of TMJ disc is diagnosed according to sagittal MR images with mouth opening
(D) An erosion of the condyle may be the earliest osseous manifestation of degenerative changes of the TMJ
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。本題問「何者錯誤」。C 稱僅憑開口（mouth opening）矢狀 MR 即可診斷關節盤前移位，為錯：前移位須同時取閉口與開口兩種斜矢狀 MR 判定——閉口位判斷盤是否移位、開口位判斷是否回位並排除 stuck disc，單一位置不足以診斷。A 正常盤呈不對稱雙凹、前帶較後帶小（薄），正確；B 內部紊亂好發年輕女性，正確；D 髁突侵蝕可為 TMJ 退化性變化最早的骨性表現，正確。→ [[tmj-internal-derangement]]
