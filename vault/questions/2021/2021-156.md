#交換 #2021交換 #PED Which description about leukemia is **wrong**?
(A) Radiological bone changes are more likely to be seen in children than adults
(B) Radiological bone changes can develop before there is a change in the peripheral blood picture
(C) Osteosclerosis is a recognized feature
(D) Myeloid leukemia is commoner in adult than children
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
LEUKEMIC BONE changes are for many reasons more common, more extensive, and more varied in children than in adults. (because more active bone marrow)
manifestations of bone marrow replacement
- Ostepenia
- Radiolucent metaphyseal bands (pediatric patient)
- Periosteal reaction
- coarse trabeculation: lead to an osteosclerotic appearanc
- Vertebral body collapse (vertebra plana)
Acute myeloid leukemia is common in > 65 y/o
Chronic myeloid leukemia is common in around 64 y/o
