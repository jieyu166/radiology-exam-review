#交換 #2021交換 #ABD 請選出typical prostate cancer 之MRI findings?
(A) T2 hypointensity, ADC dark signal, type III enhancement
(B) T2 hyperintensity, ADC dark signal, type III enhancement
(C) T2 hypointensity, ADC dark signal, type I enhancement
(D) T2 hyperintensity, ADC dark signal, type II enhancement
??
**Ans: A**
【2021 官方詳解】
T2: prostate cancer usually appears as a region of low signal within a normally high signal peripheral zone
DWI/ADC: often shows restricted diffusion
The normal prostate produces a large amount of citrate from the peripheral zone, which tumors do not 3.
Tumor = Increased choline: citrate ratio
There are three common dynamic curve types after initial uptake: type 1, persistent increase; type 2, plateau; and type 3, decline after initial upslope. Type 3 is considered the most suspicious for prostate cancer, especially if there is a focal asymmetric enhancing lesion
