(A) 17-year-old male presents with 2 months of lower extremity pain that is worse at night. The patient states he first noticed the pain following an injury during a high school football game. Radiographs show a bone tumor with deposition of calcified osteoid matrix and an associated sunburst periosteal reaction. Which of the following joints is most commonly affected by this disease entity? A. The shoulder
(B) The wrist
(C) The hip
(D) The knee
(E) The ankle
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
The sunburst appearance occurs when the lesion grows too fast and the periosteum does not have enough time to lay down a new layer and instead the Sharpey's fibers stretch out perpendicular to the bone. It is frequently associated with osteosarcoma but also in: Ewing sarcoma and osteoblastic metastases.
Ewing sarcoma typically occurs in P’t 10-20 y/o male, image show wide zone of poorly defined margin and 80 % demonstrate extension into adjacent soft tissues. ====> 比較不像
Osteaosarcoma tyically occurs in P’t 10-20 y/o male, image show osteoid matrix with neoplastic bone formation and permeative growth and non-expansile cortical destruction =>符合
Osteosarcoma typically occur at the metadiaphysis of long bones in the appendicular skeleton: femur(40%) > tibia(16%) > humerus(15%) > ….
