#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which of the following descriptions about intra-abdominal solid organ is **wrong**?
(A) In T2 weighted-images, normal liver parenchyma shows lower signal intensity than spleen parenchyma
(B) In in-phase and out-of-phase images, normal liver shows similar signal intensity with spleen
(C) In T1 weighted-images, normal pancreatic parenchyma shows hyperintensed signal
(D) In in-phase and out-of-phase images, hepatic lipomatous angiomyolipoma shows no signal drop
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D（答案有爭議）。官方答案 D，官方詳解也寫含脂較少（lipid-poor）的 AML 才在反相位（opposed-phase）掉訊號。lipomatous AML 以巨觀脂肪為主：脂肪抑制會掉訊號，但 opposed-phase 時病灶內部不掉訊號、只在脂肪與非脂肪交界出現一圈黑線（India ink）。因此 (D)「lipomatous AML 在 IP/OP 沒有 signal drop」可能是對的敘述；反方看法是 lipomatous AML 並非純脂肪，交錯處仍可局部掉訊號，邊緣黑線也算訊號下降。本題答案有爭議，詳見概念。A 正確：正常肝實質 T2 訊號低於脾。B 正確：正常肝與脾都不含脂肪，in/out-of-phase 訊號相近。C 正確：正常胰臟 T1WI 呈相對高訊號。
