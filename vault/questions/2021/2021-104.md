#交換 #2021交換 #US Which one of the following descriptions about Power and color doppler is not **true**?
(A) The main difference between color and power is that color uses amount of signal; power uses frequency
(B) Power and color are equally sensitive to fast flow, but the power is more sensitive to slow flow
(C) An advantage of color Doppler over power Doppler is directional information
(D) Power Doppler is subject to artifacts
??
**Ans: A**
答案 A。本題要選「錯誤」敘述。
(A) 把兩者說反了——彩色都卜勒（color）以平均頻移（mean frequency shift）編碼，能量都卜勒（power）則以訊號振幅（amount of signal/power）編碼，故「color 用訊號量、power 用頻率」為錯。
其餘皆正確：(B) 兩者對快速血流敏感度相當，但 power 因振幅編碼、不依賴角度，對慢速血流更敏感；(C) 相較 power，color 的優勢在於能提供血流方向資訊；(D) power 都卜勒仍會有偽影（如運動造成的 flash artifact）。
