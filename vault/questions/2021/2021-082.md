【2021 官方詳解】
The conventional aortic CTA protocol :120 kVp and 400 mg iodine/kg (答案應該是D)
For reduction the radiation dose=>
low-tube voltage acquisition (70~80 kVp)
high pitch mode
iterative reconstruction techniques
New imaging protocol called “double low”CTA (DLCTA) of the aorta (low tube voltage and low iodine
load) has been developed.
Conclusion:
Double-low computed tomography angiography (DLCTA): with 70 kVp and 200 mg iodine/kg is feasible for whole-aortic CT angiography
