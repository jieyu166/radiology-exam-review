#交換 #2021交換 #CH Which statement about COVID-19 on chest imaging is incorrect?
(A) Chest CT findings reported in more than 70% of RT-PCR test–proven COVID-19 cases included ground-glass opacities, lower lobe involvement, and posterior predilection
(B) Chest imaging is indicated as a screening test for COVID-19 in asymptomatic patients or in patients with mild respiratory symptoms of COVID-19
(C) A negative chest CT examination result certainly does not exclude COVID-19. The proportion of false-positive chest CT examination results is substantial and due to overlapping imaging features with numerous other diseases
(D) CT is not the standard for the diagnosis of COVID-19, but its findings help suggest the diagnosis in the appropriate setting
??
**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】
B. Chest imaging is not indicated as a screening test for COVID-19 in asymptomatic patients or in patients with mild respiratory symptoms of COVID-19 (ie, absence of significant pulmonary dysfunction or damage).
A negative chest CT examination does not exclude COVID-19. 沒症狀或輕微症狀就不要浪費時間跟金錢了，切了還會不小心看到一些 Incidentalomas.
Several chest CT findings have been reported in more than 70% of RT-PCR test–proven COVID-19 cases, including ground-glass opacities, vascular enlargement, bilateral abnormalities, lower lobe involvement, and posterior predilection.
