#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Which of the following statements about ankle imaging is **FALSE**?
(A) Normal ankle ligaments are readily identified as thin, linear, and low signal-intensity structures in MRI
(B) Deltoid ligament injury is commonly caused by eversion sprain
(C) Fatty striation of the posterior tibiofibular ligament (PTFL) indicates partial tear or chronic inflammatory process
(D) Sinus tarsi is a lateral space located between the talus and the calcaneus
(E) Anterolateral impingement syndrome typically shows a “meniscoid” mass within the lateral gutter of the ankle in MRI
??
**Ans: C**
【2021 官方詳解】
C.
PTFL often appear striated and discontinuous owing to fat interposed between the fascicles of the ligaments. => Normal appearance
