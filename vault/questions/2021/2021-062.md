#交換 #2021交換 #NR About toxic and metabolic brain disorders, which following statement is **false**?
(A) Common sites involved in Wernicke encephalopathy include the regions surrounding the third ventricle, the tectal plate and the periaqueductal gray matter, and the putamina.
(B) In nearly all cases of metronidazole-induced brain toxicity, MR images show bilateral symmetric lesions in the cerebellum.
(C) The characteristic imaging finding of chronic hepatic encephalopathy is bilateral and symmetric T1 hyperintensity involving the globi pallidi and substantiae nigrae, most likely due to magnesium accumulation.
(D) The globus pallidus is sensitive to hypoxia, its bilateral symmetric necrotic involvement is the hallmark of carbon monoxide poisoning.
(E) Extrapontine osmotic demyelination syndrome commonly involve the basal ganglia, thalami, and hemispheric white matter, often symmetrically.
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。慢性肝性腦病的雙側對稱蒼白球與黑質 T1 高訊號是「錳（manganese）」沉積所致，非選項所述的「鎂（magnesium）」，故 C 敘述錯誤。(A) 正確：Wernicke 腦病好發於第三腦室周圍、頂蓋板與導水管周圍灰質及殼核。(B) 正確：metronidazole 腦毒性幾乎都呈雙側對稱小腦（齒狀核）病灶。(D) 正確：蒼白球對缺氧敏感，其雙側對稱壞死是一氧化碳中毒的標誌。(E) 正確：腦橋外滲透壓性脫髓鞘常對稱侵犯基底核、視丘與大腦半球白質。→ [[toxic-metabolic-brain-imaging]]
