#交換 #2021交換 #PED The following description about molecular subgroups of the medulloblastoma, which one is **wrong**?
(A) Group 4 is more common than WNT
(B) WNT is with better prognosis compared to group 3
(C) Group 4 usually demonstrates vivid enhancement after Gd
(D) WNT is very rare in infants
??
**Ans: C**
D. mild or no enhancement
Medulloblastoma:
WNT =>(least common, children and adults), cerebellar peduncle, best prognosis
SHH =>(infants and adult) cerebellar hemisphere, intermediate prognosis,
Group3 =>(infants and children) midline, ill-defined margins but prominent enhancement, worst prognosis
Group4=> (most common,typically children) well-defined margins but mild or no enhancement

**Table 3: Molecular Subgroups of Medulloblastoma**

| Feature | Wnt | SHH | Group 3 | Group 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frequency | ~10% | ~30% | ~20% | ~40% |
| Age | Older children | Infants, older children, adolescents | Young children | All age groups to adulthood |
| Male-to-female ratio | 1:1 | 1:1 | 2:1 | 3:1 |
| Oncogenic drivers | CTNNB1 mutation, monosomy 6 | PTCHD1, smoothened, and SUFU mutations; GLI 1, GLI 2, and MYC amplifications | MYC and OTX2 amplifications, i17q | i17q, MYCN and OTX2 amplifications, SNCAIP duplication |
| Survival | Very good (p53 status independent) | Intermediate (p53 status dependent) | Poor | Intermediate |
| Location | CP angle | Cerebellar hemisphere | Midline or fourth ventricle (tumor may be small) | Midline or fourth ventricle |
| Metastasis | Rare | Rare | Frequent | Frequent |
| Enhancement | Variable | Yes, often intense | Yes | Variable, may be poor to none |

Note.—CP = cerebellopontine, CTNNB1 = catenin beta 1 gene, GLI = zinc finger protein gene, OTX2 = orthodenticle homeobox 2 gene, PTCHD1 = patched-1 gene, SNCAIP = synuclein alpha interacting protein gene, SUFU = suppressor of fused gene.
