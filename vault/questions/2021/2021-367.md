#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which of the following descriptions of NMOSD (Neuromyelitis optica spectrum disorder) is INCORRECT?
(A) Neuromyelitis optica (NMO) is an autoimmune demyelinating disorder for which the aquaporin-4 (AQP4) water channels are the major target antigens
(B) In pediatric-onset NMOSD, children usually have a better outcome in terms of disability than adults
(C) MOG-IgG–positive NMOSD patients usually have younger age of onset than AQP4-IgG–positive patients and tend to have relapsing course with a favorable outcome
(D) In MOG-IgG-postive NMOSD, optic neuritis is usually bilateral and anterior involvement
??
**Ans: C**
【2021 官方詳解】
C:
MOG-IgG–positive NMOSD patients usually have younger age of onset than AQP4-IgG–positive patients and tend to have monophasic course with a favorable outcome

**Table 3: Clinical and Radiologic Features of NMOSD Patients with AQP4-IgG–positive Status versus MOG-IgG–positive Status**

| Features | AQP4-IgG–positive Status | MOG-IgG–positive Status |
|---|---|---|
| Main target | Astrocytes | Myelin |
| Evolution | Relapsing | Usually monophasic |
| Prognosis | Poor | Favorable |
| Gender | Female predominance | Lower female-to-male ratio or male predominance |
| Approximate age of onset | More common in adults than in children | Younger age |
| Phenotypes | Multiple phenotypes, but the most common are the core clinical syndromes of NMOSD | Various phenotypes, but the most common is optic neuritis |
| Most common MR imaging patterns | Optic nerve: bilateral, long-length, and posterior involvement, usually with chiasmatic extension. Spinal cord: LETM; central/gray matter lesions; bright spotty lesions on T2-weighted images; dark lesions on T1-weighted images. Brain lesions: periependymal lesions and corticospinal tract involvement. Gadolinium enhancement: cloud-like enhancement; thin periventricular enhancement | Optic nerve: bilateral, long-length, and anterior involvement; severe optic disk swelling; perineural enhancement; optic chiasm less involved. Spinal cord: involvement of conus or thoracolumbar cord. Brain lesions: deep gray matter lesions; ADEM-like lesions (large cerebral lesions); fluffy lesions in the pons and cerebellar peduncles and adjacent to the fourth ventricle; periependymal areas rarely involved. Gadolinium enhancement: optic perineural enhancement |
| Associated disorders | NMOSD, ADEM in children, autoimmune disorders | AQP4-IgG–negative NMOSD status, multiple sclerosis, ADEM, recurrent optic neuritis, LETM |

Note.—ADEM = acute disseminated encephalomyelitis.
