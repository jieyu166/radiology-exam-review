#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which of the following descriptions of NMOSD (Neuromyelitis optica spectrum disorder) is INCORRECT?
(A) Neuromyelitis optica (NMO) is an autoimmune demyelinating disorder for which the aquaporin-4 (AQP4) water channels are the major target antigens
(B) In pediatric-onset NMOSD, children usually have a better outcome in terms of disability than adults
(C) MOG-IgG–positive NMOSD patients usually have younger age of onset than AQP4-IgG–positive patients and tend to have relapsing course with a favorable outcome
(D) In MOG-IgG-postive NMOSD, optic neuritis is usually bilateral and anterior involvement
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。本題採官方判定：官方詳解認為 MOG-IgG 陽性 NMOSD 的病程應為「單相（monophasic）」而非「復發（relapsing）」，故將選項 C 判為錯誤、為本題答案。（A）NMO 為以 aquaporin-4（AQP4）水通道為主要標的抗原之自體免疫脫髓鞘疾病，正確。（B）兒童發病之 NMOSD 在失能預後上通常較成人為佳，正確。（D）MOG-IgG 陽性者之視神經炎常為雙側且偏前段（視神經頭／眶內段）侵犯，正確；MOG 相較 AQP4 確實發病較年輕、整體預後較良好亦屬實。⚠️ 存疑：此鑑別點偏軟且文獻分歧——MOGAD 相當比例會復發（兒童約 1/3、成人系列可達 40–50%），「typically monophasic」並非定論，官方把「relapsing」定為唯一錯點宜保留質疑，判讀時對照原檔。

| Features | AQP4-IgG–positive Status | MOG-IgG–positive Status |

| Evolution | Relapsing | Usually monophasic |
| Prognosis | Poor | Favorable |
| Gender | Female predominance | Lower female-to-male ratio or male predominance |
| Approximate age of onset | More common in adults than in children | Younger age |
| Phenotypes | Multiple phenotypes, but the most common are the core clinical syndromes of NMOSD | Various phenotypes, but the most common is optic neuritis |
| Most common MR imaging patterns | Optic nerve: bilateral, long-length, and posterior involvement, usually with chiasmatic extension. Brain lesions: periependymal lesions and corticospinal tract involvement. Gadolinium enhancement: cloud-like enhancement; thin periventricular enhancement | Optic nerve: bilateral, long-length, and anterior involvement; severe optic disk swelling; perineural enhancement; optic chiasm less involved. Gadolinium enhancement: optic perineural enhancement |
| Associated disorders | NMOSD, ADEM in children, autoimmune disorders | AQP4-IgG–negative NMOSD status, multiple sclerosis, ADEM, recurrent optic neuritis, LETM |
