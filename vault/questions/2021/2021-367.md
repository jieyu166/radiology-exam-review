#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which of the following descriptions of NMOSD (Neuromyelitis optica spectrum disorder) is INCORRECT?
(A) Neuromyelitis optica (NMO) is an autoimmune demyelinating disorder for which the aquaporin-4 (AQP4) water channels are the major target antigens
(B) In pediatric-onset NMOSD, children usually have a better outcome in terms of disability than adults
(C) MOG-IgG–positive NMOSD patients usually have younger age of onset than AQP4-IgG–positive patients and tend to have relapsing course with a favorable outcome
(D) In MOG-IgG-postive NMOSD, optic neuritis is usually bilateral and anterior involvement
??
**Ans: C**
【2021 官方詳解】
C:
MOG-IgG–positive NMOSD patients usually have younger age of onset than AQP4-IgG–positive patients and tend to have monophasic course with a favorable outcome
