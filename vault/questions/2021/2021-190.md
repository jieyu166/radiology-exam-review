#交換 #2021交換 #CH 下列何者不屬於 Hemoptysis 之 CT findings?
(A) Ground-glass opacity
(B) Bronchial artery hypertrophy
(C) Filling defects in in bronchial tree
(D) Atelectasis
(E) 以上皆可以是 hemoptysis 之 CT findings
??
**Ans: E**
【2021 官方詳解】CT Findings of Hemorrhage
Blood in the alveoli typically manifests as ground-glass opacity or consolidation
When hemorrhage is subacute, septal lines may also be present
Blood products in the bronchial tree may manifest as detectable filling defects at CT
Obstruction of larger airways with blood products may result in atelectasis
Blood products in the more distal airways typically manifest as centrilobular nodules that may have
a tree-in-bud appearance
When bronchial arteries are hypertrophied, cardiac output to them can increase from a normal
output of 1% to up to 30%. Furthermore, these hypertrophied arteries are thin walled and fragile
and are exposed to systemic pressure, making them prone to rupture into the airways, which can
cause hemoptysis.
