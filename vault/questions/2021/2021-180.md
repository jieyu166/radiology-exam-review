#交換 #2021交換 #CH Which one is “**Wrong**” about chest CT in COVID-19?
(A) Chest CT Abnormalities with High Incidence (>70%), including ground-glass opacities, vascular enlargement, bilateral, lower lobe involvement, and posterior predilection
(B) Chest imaging is indicated as a screening test for COVID-19 in asymptomatic patients or in patients with mild respiratory symptoms of COVID-19
(C) A negative chest CT examination result certainly does not exclude COVID-19
(D) In cases of clinical worsening, chest imaging is advised to assess for COVID-19 progression or secondary cardiopulmonary complications such as ARDS, pulmonary embolism, superimposed pneumonia, or heart failure that can potentially be secondary to COVID-19–induced cardiac injury
??
**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】
Chest imaging is not indicated as a screening test for COVID-19 in asymptomatic patients or in patients with mild respiratory symptoms of COVID-19 (ie, absence of significant pulmonary dysfunction or damage).
