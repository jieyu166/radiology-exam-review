#交換 #2021交換 #CV About Congenitally Corrected Transposition of the Great Arteries (CCTGA), which of the following is **not** appropriate?
(A) there is leftward looping of the primitive heart tube instead of the usual D-loop
(B) connection of the aorta to the RV and the PA to the LV
(C) Deoxygenated blood enters the LA and through the mitral valve flows into the discordant LV and then to the PA
(D) The oxygenated pulmonary venous blood returns to the left atrium, and through the tricuspid valve flows into the discordant RV and into the aorta
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。CCTGA（L-TGA）為房室不一致加上心室大動脈不一致的「雙重不一致」，兩處錯接互相抵銷而達生理矯正。全身回流的去氧血是進入「右心房」，再經二尖瓣流入不一致的形態學左心室、送往肺動脈；C 卻寫成去氧血「進入左心房（LA）」，房室腔搞錯，為本題所選的錯誤敘述。其餘皆正確：(A) 原始心管為左旋（L-looping）而非一般的 D-loop；(B) 主動脈接形態學右心室、肺動脈接形態學左心室；(D) 含氧的肺靜脈血回左心房，經三尖瓣流入不一致的形態學右心室，再送入主動脈。→ [[d-tga]]
