#交換 #2021交換 #MSK About Calcium Pyrophosphate Deposition Disease (**CPPD**), which of the following is **not** appropriate?
(A) also known as pseudogout
(B) represents an inflammatory response to the deposition of calcium pyrophosphate crystals in hyaline cartilage, fibrocartilage, and periarticular structures
(C) occurs more frequently in the spine, where it most commonly involves the cervical region
(D) In the spine, crystals can be found most commonly in the ligamentum flavum, posterior longitudinal ligaments, and atlanto-occipital ligaments and less frequently can affect the intervertebral disks
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。CPPD 造成的軟骨鈣化好發於大關節（膝、髖、腕、恥骨聯合），脊椎受侵犯相對罕見；因此「較常發生於脊椎」的敘述錯誤——正確應為大關節優先、脊椎為少見的例外（例外時才多在頸椎）。其餘正確：A CPPD 又稱假性痛風（pseudogout）；B 其本質為對焦磷酸鈣結晶沉積於透明軟骨、纖維軟骨與關節周圍結構的發炎反應；D 一旦侵犯脊椎，結晶最常見於黃韌帶、後縱韌帶與寰枕韌帶，較少影響椎間盤。→ [[cppd-chondrocalcinosis]]
