#交換 #2021交換 #2023交換 #Physics 當喝入含有約1000 cc dilute鋇劑,當腸道的顯影劑時, 以下敘述何者**錯誤**?
(A) 在CT 下, 以high density 表現
(B) 在 MRI T1-weighted study 下, 腸道是hypointense
(C) 在 MRI T2-weighted study 下, 腸道是hypointense
(D) 在 超音波下, 與水的效果相當
(E) 在 有腸道破漏的疑慮時, 不要使用
??
**Ans: C**
dilute 代表是water based agent故如同biphasic agent
【2021 官方詳解】
1000 cc dilute鋇劑=> water based agent=> biphasic contrast agents
Enteric MR contrast materials
Biphasic contrast agents :
lengthen both T1 and T2 values, resulting in decreased intraluminal signal on T1WI and increased signal on T2WI . ex: all water-based agent
Nonabsorbable additives (mannitol) prevent water absorption by jejunum
Positive contrast agents:
shorten T1 relaxation times and increase intraluminal signal on T1-weighted images.
Agent with low concentrations of ferric, manganese, or gadolinium ions (but Neither in production)
Orally administered lipids with short T1 values
Negative contrast agents:
shorten T2 relaxation times and decrease intraluminal signal on T2WI
diamagnetic materials (barium, kaolin) or relatively high concentrations of paramagnetic ions
superparamagnetic ion oxides (SPIOs) to produce marked T2* shortening.
