#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which of the followings is NOT **true** while talking about mesenteric venous thrombosis (MVT)？
(A) The characteristic finding for MVT is the presence of a filling defect within a mesenteric vein. Other nonspecific findings include bowel wall thickening, indistinct bowel margins, ascites, and a thickened mesentery
(B) Recent investigations suggest that nonoperative management results in improved survival and fewer complications compared with surgical intervention
(C) Systemic anticoagulation is the second-line treatment for MVT
(D) Catheter-directed fibrinolysis is usually recommended only in patients with refractory symptoms despite anticoagulation but in whom surgical intervention is not necessary
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。系統性抗凝（systemic anticoagulation）是腸繫膜靜脈栓塞（MVT）的主要、第一線治療，而非第二線，故 C 敘述錯誤、為本題正解。A 正確：MVT 特徵影像為腸繫膜靜脈內的充盈缺損，其他非特異表現包括腸壁增厚、腸緣模糊、腹水與腸繫膜增厚。B 正確：近期研究顯示非手術治療較手術介入有較佳存活率與較少併發症。D 正確：導管導向溶栓通常僅用於抗凝治療後症狀仍頑固、但尚不需手術介入的病人。→ [[acute-mesenteric-ischemia]]
