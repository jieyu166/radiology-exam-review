#交換 #2021交換 #CH Which sign is specific for usual interstitial pneumonia due to connective tissue disease?
(A) Straight-edege sign
(B) anterior upper lobe sign
(C) exuberant honey combing sign
(D) All of the above
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
the exuberant honeycombing sign, the anterior upper lobe sign, and the straight-edge sign have high specificity but modest sensitivity in suggesting that UIP on CT images is due to CTD-ILD rather than IPF
