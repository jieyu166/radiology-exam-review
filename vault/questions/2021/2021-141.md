#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Which of the following ligaments is the strongest ligament in the lateral ligaments of ankle?
(A) Anterior talofibular ligament
(B) Calcaneofibular ligament
(C) Posterior talofibular ligament
(D) Spring ligament
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。踝關節外側副韌帶由前而後為 ATFL、CFL、PTFL，其中後距腓韌帶（PTFL）最強、最厚、也最少受傷，故為「最強者」。A 前距腓韌帶（ATFL）反而最弱、且在內翻扭傷中最常撕裂；B 跟腓韌帶（CFL）強度居中；D Spring ligament（跟舟足底／彈簧韌帶）屬內側／足底支撐結構，並非踝外側韌帶，為干擾選項。→ [[lateral-ankle-ligaments]]
