#交換 #2021交換 #Breast According to the ACR BI-RADS Atlas® 5th Edition, which description of the Kinetic Curve Assessment is **not** **true**?
(A) In initial phase, slow is deﬁned as an increase in signal intensity of less than 50% within the ﬁrst 2 minutes
(B) In initial phase, fast is an increase of more than 100%
(C) During the delayed phase, persistent is deﬁned as signal intensity that does not change over time after the initial rise
(D) During the delayed phase, washout is deﬁned as a decrease of more than 10% from the highest signal intensity during the initial rise. Washout curves are speciﬁcally denoted “suspicious.”
??
**Ans: C**
「持續性」（persistent）的定義是：在初始上升後，信號強度繼續**緩慢增加>10%**，而不是「不隨時間變化」。
【2021 官方詳解】C. During the delayed phase, persistent is defined as signal intensity more than 10% of initial enhancement.
It represents the initial increase in contrast enhancement in the kinetic assessment for BI-RADS. An
intensity increase < 50% is classified as slow, 50–100% as medium, and > 100% as fast enhancement. SER
indicates the kinetic curve shape that represents the delayed phase in BI-RADS assessment. Persistent is
more than 10% of initial enhancement, washout is less than 10% of initial enhancement, and plateau is
between the two.

The kinetic curve can be classified into three categories. Early
enhancement (2 minutes after agent injection), during which
the initial rise of the enhanced curve can be divided into
“slow”, “medium”, and “rapid”.
The signal intensity 2 minutes after contrast injection is
defined as “delayed phase, which is divided into “persistent”
(type I), “plateau” (type II), and “washout” (type III)
