#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which of the following statements describes the signal intensity characteristics of villous lesions at MRI?
(A) Intermediate to high signal intensity on T2-weighted images
(B) High signal intensity, due to hemorrhage, on T1-weighted images
(C) Low surface signal intensity, due to mucin coating, on T2-weighted images
(D) Intermediate to high signal intensity on T1-weighted images
??
**Ans: A**
【2021 官方詳解】In one study, investigators assessed 23 rectal villous lesions on MR images and found that the majority of
them (n = 21) had a “vegetating” shape, with only two lesions characterized as flat. Most of the lesions had
polycyclic margins and a thick T2-hyperintense layer along their surface, which was possibly due to high
cellularity or a mucinous film coating. All lesions showed heterogeneously intermediate to high signal
intensity on T2-weighted MR images, with low signal intensity on T1-weighted MR images and pronounced
heterogeneous enhancement in 61% of cases.
