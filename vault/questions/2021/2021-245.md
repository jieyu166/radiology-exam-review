#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which of the following statements describes the signal intensity characteristics of villous lesions at MRI?
(A) Intermediate to high signal intensity on T2-weighted images
(B) High signal intensity, due to hemorrhage, on T1-weighted images
(C) Low surface signal intensity, due to mucin coating, on T2-weighted images
(D) Intermediate to high signal intensity on T1-weighted images
??
**Ans: A**
答案 A。MRI 上 villous lesion 於 T2 呈異質性中至高訊號，表面常有一層厚 T2 高訊號層（高細胞或黏液膜覆蓋所致），故 A 正確。B 錯：T1 為低訊號、並非因出血呈高訊號；C 錯：表面黏液層在 T2 為高訊號而非低訊號；D 錯：T1 為低訊號，非中至高訊號。→ [[gi-villous-lesions]]
