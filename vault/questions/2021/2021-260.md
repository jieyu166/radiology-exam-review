#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Which one of the following radiographic features on radiography is NOT usually seen in patients with a subtle fracture?
(A) Periosteal reaction
(B) Intraarticular fat-fluid level
(C) Double cortical line
(D) Irregular epiphyseal corners
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
(A) The causes of periosteal reaction are broad, including trauma, infection, arthritis, tumors, and drug-induced and vascular entities.
(B) = Lipohemarthrosis, associated with a tibial plateau fracture or distal femoral fracture; rarely a patellar fracture. They have also been described in hip, shoulder, elbow and wrist fractures
(C) This finding indicates a subtle but depressed fracture. The actual fracture line may not be apparent, but the double contour of the cortex reflects impaction.
(D) may related to osteonecrosis, infraction, infection….
