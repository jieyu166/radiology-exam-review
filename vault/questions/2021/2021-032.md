#交換 #2021交換 #CV Which of the following is **not** a characteristic of a vulnerable plaque on coronary computed tomography angiography?
(A) Positive remodeling.
(B) Spotty calciﬁcation.
(C) Napkin ring sign.
(D) High attenuation.
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。冠狀動脈 CT 上易損（高風險）斑塊的指標是「低密度」而非高密度——富含脂質的壞死核心呈 low-attenuation plaque（多 <30 HU），因此「high attenuation」與易損斑塊特徵相反，為本題所選的非特徵。其餘三者皆為公認的高風險斑塊徵象（一般需符合至少兩項）：(A) positive remodeling，斑塊處管壁向外代償擴張、管腔反而保留；(B) spotty calcification，直徑 <3 mm 的點狀鈣化；(C) napkin-ring sign，中央低密度核心外圍環繞一圈較高密度的「餐巾環」。→ [[ccta]]

## 詳解附圖（OCR）
Positive remodeling: outer vessel diameter with a threshold of 1.1 was used.

Low HU plaque was defined as the mean CT number within these 3 regions of interest <30 HU.

The napkin-ring sign was defined as a ring-like peripheral higher attenuation.

Spotty calcium was defined as calcified plaque with a diameter <3 mm in any direction.
