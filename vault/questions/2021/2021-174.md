#交換 #2021交換 #NR About the femoral artery pseudoaneurysm (Iatrogenic pseudoaneurysm) (IPA) following percutaneous intervention, the following state was **wrong** ?
(A) Incidence <1 % of all interventional procedures
(B) Risk factors including obesity, large sheath size (>6 Fr)
(C) For uncompicated IPA <3cm, obseveration with serial ultrasound every 2 weeks for up to 6 week was the best way
(D) For uncompicated IPA >3cm, open surgical repair was better than ultrasound-guided thrombin injection
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
Iatrogenic pseudoaneurysm (IPA)
- <1 percent of all interventional procedures
- risk factors for IPA include female sex, increasing age, concomitant venous puncture, hypertension, severely calcified vessels, larger access sheath size (>6 French), and use of anticoagulation either at the time of arterial cannulation or in the immediate postprocedure period
- Treatment:
  - Complicated pseudoaneurysm (Hemodynamic instability, Neurologic deficit (motor or sensory) or pulse deficit attributable to the IPA, Expanding hematoma, Extensive skin and subcutaneous damage, Concern for soft tissue infection) 🡪 Open surgical repair
  - Uncomplicated pseudoaneurysm <3cm: obseveration
  - Uncomplicated pseudoaneurysm >3cm: ultrasound-guided thrombin injection as the first-line technique
