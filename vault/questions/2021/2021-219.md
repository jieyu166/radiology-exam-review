#交換 #2021交換 #CH 下列何者「不」符合對於 pleuroparenchymal fibroelastosis 的描述？
(A) Patients are most often non-smokers
(B) It usually shows a progressive clinical course and ends in progressive massive fibrosis
(C) It shows marked bilateral apical pleural thickening
(D) Roughly half of cases occur as a sequela of lung transplantation or bone marrow transplantation
(E) Patients have reduced lung function of a mixed (obstructive and restrictive) pattern
??
**Ans: B**
答案 B。PPFE 雖屬進行性、預後差的纖維化疾病，但其終點是慢性高碳酸血症呼吸衰竭，而非 progressive massive fibrosis（PMF）——PMF 是塵肺症／complicated pneumoconiosis 的融合纖維腫塊，與 PPFE 病理無關，故 (B) 的敘述錯誤。(A) 患者多為非吸菸者、(C) 以雙側肺尖胸膜及胸膜下實質明顯增厚為特徵、(D) 約半數為肺移植或骨髓移植後之續發表現、(E) 肺功能常呈混合（阻塞＋限制）型，皆符合 PPFE 的描述。
