#交換 #2021交換 #CH Which statement about radiation-induced lung disease is incorrect?
(A) It is customary to use the radiation therapy first date as a reference point for determining the dates of tissue changes
(B) Radiographically evident changes are uncommon when the total radiation dose delivered is less than 30 Gy
(C) More frequently changes are detected with CT by 4 weeks
(D) Radiographic findings typically appear as heterogeneous opacities or consolidation
??
**Ans: A**
【2021 官方詳解】
A. It is customary to use the radiation therapy completion date as a reference point for determining the dates of tissue changes.
Regardless of the radiation therapy delivery technique used, radiographically evident changes are uncommon when the total radiation dose delivered is less than 30 Gy, but they are almost always seen with doses higher than 40 Gy.
Radiographic findings seen during the acute phase typically appear as heterogeneous opacities or consolidation in the treatment field approximately 3–4 weeks after the radiation therapy ends.
CT can depict the radiation damage before it is evident at radiography. Although acute radiation pneumonitis may be detected at CT as early as 1 week after treatment, more frequently changes are detected with CT by 4 weeks.
