#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which of the following about ovarian neoplasms is **WRONG**?
(A) Four categories by histopathologic features into germ cell tumors, sex cord–stromal tumors, epithelial surface cell tumors, and metastases
(B) Germ cell tumors are the most common category of ovarian neoplasms
(C) Serous ovarian neoplasms are often unilocular and bilateral. Mucinous ovarian neoplasms are often larger, unilateral, and multilocular
(D) Endometrioid and clear cell carcinomas can arise within endometriomas and are both associated with Lynch syndrome
??
**Ans: B**
答案 B。卵巢腫瘤中最常見的大類為上皮性腫瘤（epithelial surface cell tumors，約占 60–70%），其次才是生殖細胞腫瘤（germ cell，約 20%），故選項 B「germ cell 為最常見大類」為錯誤敘述（正解）。其餘皆正確：依組織病理可分為生殖細胞、性索間質、上皮性與轉移四大類（A）；漿液性腫瘤常為單房且雙側、黏液性腫瘤常較大、單側且多房（C）；子宮內膜樣癌與透明細胞癌皆可源自子宮內膜異位瘤，並與 Lynch 症候群相關（D）。→ [[ovarian-tumors-imaging]]

## 詳解附圖（OCR）
- Both endometrioid and clear cell carcinomas are associated with Lynch syndrome, which is often accompanied by synchronous endometrial carcinoma. Both subtypes are also highly associated with endometriosis.
- There are characteristic imaging findings of mature teratomas owing to the presence of sebaceous material or fat, hair, teeth (calcification), bone, or cartilage. At MRI, the fatty component of the mass demonstrates high T1 and T2 signal intensity with loss of signal intensity at fat-suppressed MR imaging. The T1-weighted fat-saturated sequence is crucial in distinguishing fat signal intensity from hemorrhage (which remains T1 hyperintense).

**TEACHING POINTS**
- Imaging features that suggest a borderline or malignant tumor include mural nodules, papillary projections, enhancing solid (nonfatty, nonfibrous) components, and thickened walls and septa (greater than 3 mm), particularly when vascularized.
- At imaging, serous cystadenomas most often manifest as a simple unilocular cyst with an absence of solid components, thickened septa, or mural nodularity, although some may have a few locules separated by thin septa.
- Mucinous tumors manifest as a predominantly cystic, multilocular unilateral mass, often with varying signal intensity, attenuation, and echogenicity of the mucin-containing cysts at MRI, CT, and US, respectively.
