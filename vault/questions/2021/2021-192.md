#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which statement about differentiation of solid renal tumor with MR imaging is correct?
(A) Clear cell renal cell carcinomas (RCC) typically showed high T2 signal intensity
(B) Papillary RCCs usually demonstrate low T2 signal intensity
(C) Chromophobe RCCs shared overlapping image features as oncocytomas
(D) Classic angiomyolipoma showed India ink artifact on opposed-phase images because of macroscopic fat
(E) All statements are correct
??
**Ans: E**
【2021 官方詳解】
Clear cell RCC
- T1: heterogeneous appearance
- T2: hyperintense
- IP/OP: frequently containing microscopic fat resulting in focal or diffuse non-curvilinear signal loss in opposed phase (in 60% 7)
Papillary renal cell carcinoma
- T1: hypointense
- T2: hypointense
- T1 C+ (Gd): contrast enhancement tends to be less intense than in the more common clear cell RCC
- DWI: restricted diffusion may be useful for differentiating from a hemorrhagic cyst
Chromophobe renal cell carcinoma
- They have a similar origin to oncocytomas
- isointense to kidney / maybe T2 hypointense
- relatively homogeneous contrast enhancement
- necrosis not common
Renal angiomyolipoma
- Macroscopic fat: loss of signal following fat saturation, India ink artifact at the interface between fat and non-fat components
