#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Which is **incorrect** concerning Osgood-Schlatter Disease?
(A) Osteochondrosis of the tibial tuberosity
(B) More commonly found in adult than adolescents
(C) Fragmental on of the tibial tuberosity becomes apparent after 3 or 4 weeks after injury.
(D) Patient present with pain, so tissue swelling and tenderness in the anterior knee
??
**Ans: B**
答案 B。Osgood-Schlatter disease 是活躍青少年的脛骨粗隆牽引性骨骺炎，好發男性 10–15 歲、女性 8–12 歲，並非成人較常見，故 B 敘述不正確。其餘正確：A 其本質為脛骨粗隆的骨軟骨病（osteochondrosis）；C 脛骨粗隆骨化中心的碎裂化通常在受傷後約 3–4 週才顯現；D 病人典型以前膝疼痛、局部軟組織腫脹與壓痛表現。→ [[osgood-schlatter-disease]]
