#交換 #2021交換 #Breast 關於ACR BI-RADS categories, 以下何者為**非**？
(A) Abnormality之hierarchy,其嚴重程度排序由低🡪高為1236045
(B) Category 4A指惡性可能程度為>2%但≦10%
(C) Category 4B指惡性可能程度為>25%但≦50%
(D) 到目前的版本為止(2013版)，breast MRI的category 4並未建議一定 要再細分為4A,4B,4C。
??
**Ans: C**
10-49%
【2021 官方詳解】A.
When there are multiple findings, the BI-RADS category for the exam is assigned the highest category in the
following hierarchy, from lowest to highest: 1, 2, 3, 6, 0, 4, 5.
The vast majority of screening mammograms fall into BI-RADS 1 or 2
Screening mammograms with suspicious findings should generally be assigned BI-RADS 0
=>  indicate a callback for diagnostic evaluation, meaning additional views to confirm and further evaluate
the finding.
C.
BI-RADS 4A: low suspicion for malignancy (2-9%)
BI-RADS 4B: moderate suspicion for malignancy (10-49%)
BI-RADS 4C: high suspicion for malignancy (50-94%)
