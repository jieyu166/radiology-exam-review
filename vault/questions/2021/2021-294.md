#交換 #2021交換 #CH Which of the following is NOT a common etiology fibrosing mediastinitis?
(A) Sarcoidosis
(B) Mycobacterium tuberculosis
(C) Histoplasma capsulatum
(D) Schistosoma mansoni
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
Ref: radiopaedia
Etiology
- idiopathic: most cases , thought to be a form of IgG4-related disease
- Histoplasma capsulatum infection (histoplasmosis): common in the United States and often gives a localized pattern
- Mycobacterium tuberculosis infection (pulmonary tuberculosis)
- concurrent intrathoracic malignancy
- sarcoidosis
- radiation therapy
- drugs, e.g. methysergide therapy
