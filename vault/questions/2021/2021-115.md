#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which of the following descriptions about temporomandibular joint disorder (TMD) on MRI is **wrong**?
(A) The most frequent cause is internal derangement, which is defined as an abnormal relationship of the disk to the condyle
(B) Abnormal disc displacement is considered if the angle between the posterior band and the vertical orientation of the condyle (twelve o’clock position) exceeds 30°
(C) An anteriorly displaced disk has been seen in up to 34% of asymptomatic volunteers
(D) Rupture of superior retrodiskal layer fibers may produce significant disk instability
??
**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】
B. 嚴格來說超過正負10度就算Pathological了．雖然paper也同意說小於正負30度可以是normal.
