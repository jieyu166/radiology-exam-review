#交換 #2021交換 #NR Elevated 2-hydroxyglutarate peak (2-HG) resonating at 2.25 ppm can be found in (可複選)
(A) Diffuse Astrocytoma, IDH-Mutant
(B) Anaplastic Astrocytoma, IDH-Mutant
(C) Diffuse Astrocytoma, IDH-Wild-Type
(D) Glioblastoma, IDH-Wild-Type
??
**Ans: A,B**
答案 A,B。2-hydroxyglutarate（2-HG）是 IDH 突變腫瘤專屬的代謝物——突變型 IDH 把 α-ketoglutarate 還原成 2-HG 並堆積，於 MRS 上約 2.25 ppm 出現波峰；只要是 IDH-mutant 的星細胞瘤都可測得，故 (A) Diffuse astrocytoma, IDH-mutant 與 (B) Anaplastic astrocytoma, IDH-mutant 皆為正解。(C) Diffuse astrocytoma, IDH-wild-type 與 (D) Glioblastoma, IDH-wild-type 為 IDH-wildtype，不產生 2-HG，故無此峰。本題為複選。→ [[2-hydroxyglutarate-idh-mutant-glioma]]
【醫師裁決 2026-07-11】答案更正 A → A,B。題幹可複選；2-HG peak 見於所有 IDH-Mutant astrocytoma，故 diffuse(A)與 anaplastic(B)皆對。答案 A→A,B。
