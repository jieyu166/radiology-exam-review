#交換 #2021交換 #CV Which one of the following options about “Buerger’s disease” is “incorrect”?
(A) Also known as “thrombangitis obliterans”. Strong association with smoking
(B) Unknown etiology. About 20% of all chronic vascular diseases
(C) Instep claudication with distal ulceration. Symptoms abate on cessation of smoking and return on its resumption
(D) Location at legs (80%) and arms (10-20%). Skip lesions. Absence of generalized arteriosclerosis
??
**Ans: B**
答案 B。Buerger disease 與大量吸菸高度相關，並非病因不明（unknown etiology），且「約佔所有慢性血管疾病 20%」的比例明顯過高——依 Radiology Review Manual 的歐美數據，其盛行率僅約 0.5–0.6%（亞洲、中東較高），因此 B 為錯誤敘述，為本題所選。其餘皆正確：(A) 又稱 thromboangiitis obliterans（血栓閉塞性血管炎），與吸菸強相關；(C) 表現為足弓（instep）跛行合併遠端潰瘍，戒菸後症狀緩解、復抽則復發；(D) 好發下肢（約 80%）與上肢（10–20%），呈跳躍性病灶，且無全身性動脈粥狀硬化背景。→ [[buerger-disease]]
