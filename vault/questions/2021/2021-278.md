#交換 #2021交換 #NR For external auditory canal (EAC) exostosis, which one is less likely
(A) Most common solid tumor in EAC
(B) Usually bilateral
(C) Usually a narrow-based lesion
(D) Also called Surfer’s ear
??
**Ans: C**
【2021 官方詳解】
Ref: Radiopaedia
Exostosis of the external auditory canal (also known as surfer's ear), is a benign bony overgrowth of the bony external auditory canal brought about by exposure to cold wind and water combined.
- broad-based or more focal circumferential bony overgrowth of the osseous external auditory canal
- bilateral
Ref: Indian J Radiol Imaging. 2011 Oct-Dec; 21(4): 274–278. doi: 10.4103/0971-3026.90687
Osteochondroma (exostosis) is the most common benign tumor found in the EAC.
