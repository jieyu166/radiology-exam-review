#交換 #2021交換 #Physics About Gadolinium deposition in the brain, which of the following statements is **false**?
(A) Gadolinium can deposit in the dentate nucleus, globus pallidus, pons and thalamus, through CSF in intact blood-brain barrier patients.
(B) Exposure to gadolinium-based contrast agents (GBCAs) may result in gadolinium deposition in human brain and bone tissue in those patients with normal renal function.
(C) Repeated GBCAs administration results in gadolinium deposition which affects cognitive function and causes cerebellar syndrome.
(D) The administration of linear GBCAs was associated with significant high gadolinium concentration in the brain compared to macrocyclic GBCA administration.
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。目前並無動物或人體研究證實神經組織的釓沉積會造成認知功能損害或小腦症候群等臨床、行為或生物學上的不良變化，其臨床意義仍未明，故宣稱「重複注射會影響認知功能並造成小腦症候群」的 C 為錯誤敘述。
(A) 釓可穿過完整的血腦障壁、沉積於齒狀核、蒼白球、腦橋與視丘（推測經腦脊髓液/glymphatic 途徑），正確。
(B) 即使腎功能正常者，暴露於含釓對比劑後仍可能在腦部與骨骼組織產生沉積，正確。
(D) 線狀製劑去螯合較多，腦內釓濃度顯著高於環狀製劑，正確。

## 詳解附圖（OCR）
**TEACHING POINTS**

- The risk of NSF after GBCA exposure depends on which agent is used, with most cases reported after use of nonionic linear agents.
- Administration of higher than standard doses (>0.1 mmol/kg) of GBCAs has been associated with development of NSF.
- The dramatic reduction of new cases of NSF over the last decade may have occurred because of more routine screening for kidney disease, less use of the GBCAs that have been associated with the most cases of NSF (particularly in patients with renal dysfunction), and less use of the high-dose GBCA-enhanced examinations that were sometimes used in MR angiography.
- To our knowledge, there have been no studies in animals or humans demonstrating clinical, behavioral, or biologic changes associated with neuronal gadolinium deposition.
- Neuronal GBCA deposition has been reported after administration of linear and macrocyclic agents. However, less retention is seen after use of macrocyclic agents.
