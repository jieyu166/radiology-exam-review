(A) 12-year-old male presents to his provider with a 2-month history of pain in his lower right thigh. He describes the pain as "achy" in character, moderate in severity, and persistent in duration. On physical examination, there is a palpable mass in the distal right thigh. He adds that it is tender. He also exhibits a. limited range of motion at the knee. Radiographs of the lower extremity reveal an osseous lesion with a radiodense matrix and a "sunburst" appearance. Local soft tissue extension is apparent. Which organ is the most common site of spread of the most likely lesion in this patient with inadequate treatment? A. Brain
(B) Lungs
(C) Liver
(D) Adrenal glands
(E) Kidneys
??
**Ans: B**
答案 B。緻密（radiodense）基質加 sunburst 骨膜反應與局部軟組織侵犯符合骨肉瘤。骨肉瘤以血行轉移為主，最常見的遠端轉移器官為肺（Lung ≫ 骨、淋巴結），約 5–10% 於診斷時已有肺轉移。A brain、C liver、D adrenal glands、E kidneys 皆非骨肉瘤的典型轉移部位。→ [[osteosarcoma]]
