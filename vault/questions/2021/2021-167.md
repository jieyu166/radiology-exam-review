#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which one is not the branch of inferolateral trunk ?
(A) Anastomosis to ophthalmic artery
(B) Artery of the foramen lacerum
(C) Basal tentorial artery
(D) Anastomosis to accessory meningeal artery
??
**Ans: C**
【2021 官方詳解】
A: Inferior lateral trunk
B: Recurrent branch: along CN VI, supply territory of marginal tentorial or lateral tentorial arteries
C: Anterior medial branch: through superior orbital fissure anastomosis with recurrent meningeal bran of ophthalmic artery
D: Artery to foramen rotundem: Supply maxillary division of CN V
E: Artery of foramen ovale: anastomoses with accessory meningeal artery
Artery of foramen lacerum: anastomosis with carotid branch from ascending pharyngeal artery
