#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which of the following description is **false** regarding the adult-onset neuronal intranuclear inclusion disease (NIID)
(A) Can be diagnosed by skin biopsy
(B) It is a slowly progressive neurodegenerative disease
(C) MRI shows high DWI signal in deep periventricular white matter of brain
(D) MRI shows remarkable leukoencephalopathy
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。神經元核內包涵體病（NIID）的招牌 DWI 高訊號沿皮質–髓質交界（corticomedullary junction）呈緞帶狀分布，且不擴及深部腦室旁白質，即使晚期亦然，故 C「深部腦室旁白質 DWI 高訊號」之敘述錯誤；A：可由皮膚切片見嗜酸性核內包涵體診斷，正確；B：為緩慢進展之神經退化病，正確；D：MRI 可見顯著白質腦病（leukoencephalopathy），正確。題目問「錯誤」者，故選 C。→ [[bilateral-subcortical-dwi-hyperintensity-ddx]]
