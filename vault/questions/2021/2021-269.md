#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which of the following description is **false** regarding the adult-onset neuronal intranuclear inclusion disease (NIID)
(A) Can be diagnosed by skin biopsy
(B) It is a slowly progressive neurodegenerative disease
(C) MRI shows high DWI signal in deep periventricular white matter of brain
(D) MRI shows remarkable leukoencephalopathy
??
**Ans: C**
【2021 官方詳解】
(C) Head MRI examination detected remarkable leukoencephalopathy and DWI high intensity signal in the corticomedullary junction in the dementia-dominant group. In this study, we found that the DWI high intensity signal extended along the corticomedullary junction, but did not expand into the deep white matter, even in the advanced stage.
