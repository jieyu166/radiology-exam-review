#交換 #2021交換 #US Which one of the following descriptions about grey-scale image optimization of ultrasonography is not **true**?
(A) Superficial structures should be scanned with high-frequency probes
(B) When the transmitted pulse is stronger, the resulting image is darker
(C) Similar to the power output, the overall gain is usually displayed as a percent of maximum or as a decibels level
(D) The trade-off for multiple level focusing is a decrease in the frame rate
??
**Ans: B**
答案 B。本題要選「錯誤」敘述。
(B)「發射脈波越強、影像越暗」為錯——發射功率（output power）決定發射脈波的強度，脈波越強→返回回音越強→影像越亮（而非變暗）；此與接收端增益（gain）僅放大回音的機轉不同。
其餘皆正確：(A) 表淺構造應以高頻探頭掃描以取得較佳解析度；(C) overall gain 與發射功率類似，通常以最大值百分比或分貝（dB）顯示；(D) 多層聚焦可改善側向解析度，但每條掃描線需多次發射，代價是 frame rate 下降。
