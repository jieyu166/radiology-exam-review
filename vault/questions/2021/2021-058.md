#交換 #2021交換 #NR According to cIMPACT (the Consortium to Inform Molecular and Practical Approaches to CNS Tumor Taxonomy) proposed (2020) changes to IDHmt diffuse gliomas, which one is **wrong**?
(A) The new recommendations to the classification system involve the removal of IDHmt glioblastoma and the institution of IDHmt astrocytoma grading up to WHO grade 4.
(B) IDHmt astrocytoma will be graded based on histology and the presence or absence of a novel genetic parameter, CDKN2A/B homozygous deletion (note the use of Arabic numerals, another proposed change)
(C) All (2016) IDHmt glioblastoma (defined by microvascular proliferation or necrosis) will become (2020) IDHmt astrocytoma WHO grade 4.
(D) Previous (2016) diffuse or anaplastic astrocytoma that harbor a CDKN2A/B mutation will become (2020) IDHmt Astrocytoma WHO grade 4.
(E) All the above are correct.
??
**Ans: E**
答案 E。cIMPACT-NOW（2020）／WHO CNS5 對 IDH-mutant 瀰漫性膠質瘤的四項敘述皆成立，故「全部正確」為正解。(A) 正確：第五版廢除「IDH-mutant glioblastoma」一詞，glioblastoma 僅保留給 IDH-wildtype，IDH-mutant 星細胞瘤改採型內分級並可至 grade 4。(B) 正確：IDH-mutant astrocytoma 依組織學加上 CDKN2A/B 純合缺失此一新分子參數分級，並改以阿拉伯數字書寫。(C) 正確：凡 2016 版以微血管增生或壞死定義之 IDH-mutant glioblastoma，一律重新歸為 IDH-mutant astrocytoma WHO grade 4。(D) 正確：舊 diffuse／anaplastic astrocytoma 若帶 CDKN2A/B 純合缺失即升為 grade 4（即使無微血管增生或壞死）。→ [[who-cns-tumor-grading]]
