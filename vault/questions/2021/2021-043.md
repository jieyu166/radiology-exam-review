#交換 #2021交換 #2023交換 #MSK Regarding the MRI protocol for evaluation of diabetic pedal disease, which of the following statements is **TRUE**?
(A) The head coil is recommended for imaging both feet at once.
(B) Field of view should always be large to include the lower calf.
(C) No fat suppression is needed for T2-weighted imaging.
(D) A STIR sequence can be useful to provide more homogeneous fat suppression.
(E) IV gadolinium contrast material provides no benefit.
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
A. Imaging both feet with a head coil appears more efficient but results in suboptimal imaging of both
sides. Each foot should be imaged separately.
B.
The calf should be included if there is clinical concern for proximal spread of infection.
However, this is relatively rare , and the large field of view renders interpretation of the small bones of the foot limited because they are subject to volume averaging effects
C.
On T2-weighted spin-echo and especially fast spin-echo imaging, fat in marrow and subcutaneous tissues is hyperintense, which can obscure subtle areas of edema. Fat suppression should be used when available unless there is significant artifacts
E.
IV gadolinium contrast material facilitates identification of abscesses, sinus tracts, and devitalized regions; differentiation of cellulitis from diabetic soft-tissue edema; and differentiation of bland fluid from
septic arthritis and septic tenosynovitis
