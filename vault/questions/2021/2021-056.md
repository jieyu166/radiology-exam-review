#交換 #2021交換 #NR According to cIMPACT (the Consortium to Inform Molecular and Practical Approaches to CNS Tumor Taxonomy) proposed (2020) changes to diffuse glioma classification, which one is **wrong**?
(A) All glioblastoma will be IDHwt
(B) All glioblastoma will be IDHmt
(C) All diffuse astrocytoma will be IDHmt,
(D) All diffuse astrocytoma will be graded based on histological and molecular phenotype to grades 2 through 4
??
**Ans: B**
答案 B。本題問「何者錯誤」，B 稱所有膠質母細胞瘤都會是 IDH-mutant 為錯——依 cIMPACT-NOW 與 2021 WHO CNS 分類，glioblastoma 一詞已專指 IDH-wildtype，並維持 grade 4；帶 IDH 突變者不再稱 GBM，改歸為 astrocytoma。故 A（所有 GBM 皆 IDH-wildtype）正確，與 B 恰相反；C（所有 diffuse astrocytoma 皆 IDH-mutant）正確；D（diffuse astrocytoma 依組織學與分子表型分為 grade 2 至 4）正確。→ [[gbm]]
