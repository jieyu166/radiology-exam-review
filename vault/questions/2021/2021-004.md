#交換 #2021交換 #Breast When axillary adenopathy is seen at screening mammography with no suspicious findings in the breasts. Which of the following is **false** as according to ACR BI-RADS Atlas® 5th Edition?
(A) Bilateral axillary sonography should be performed to confirm that the finding is asymmetric/unilateral.
(B) In the absence of a known infectious or inflammatory cause, isolated unilateral axillary adenopathy should receive a suspicious (BI-RADS category 4) assessment.
(C) Bilateral axillary adenopathy should be assessed as benign (BI-RADS category 2) because they are frequently reactive/infectious in origin.
(D) Unilateral axillary adenopathy suggests occult breast carcinoma or, much less commonly, lymphoma, metastatic melanoma, ovarian cancer, or other metastatic cancer. Consequently, a careful search of the ipsilateral breast images is warranted.
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。雙側腋下淋巴腫大的分類須視臨床情境而定，並非一律給良性（BI-RADS 2）：若有已知的反應性／感染性病因（如結節病、系統性紅斑狼瘡、乾癬、HIV）才評為 BI-RADS 2，但若無已知原因、尤其為新出現者，可能是淋巴瘤／白血病的表現，應給可疑（BI-RADS 4）並建議超音波導引 FNA 或粗針切片，故 C 稱「一律評為良性」為錯誤（false）敘述。其餘正確：(A) 可做雙側腋下超音波確認為不對稱／單側；(B) 在無已知感染或發炎病因下，孤立單側腋下淋巴腫大應給可疑（BI-RADS 4）；(D) 單側腋下淋巴腫大提示隱匿性乳癌，較少為淋巴瘤、轉移性黑色素瘤、卵巢癌或其他轉移癌，故須仔細檢查同側乳房影像。→ [[axillary-adenopathy-mammography]]
