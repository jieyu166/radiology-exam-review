#交換 #2021交換 #Physics Which CT image most likely uses the greatest display window width?
(A) Head (tissue)
(B) Chest (mediastinum)
(C) Chest (lung)
(D) Abdomen (liver)
??
**Ans: C**
【2021 官方詳解】Windowing, also known as grey-level mapping, contrast stretching, histogram modification or contrast
enhancement is the process in which the CT image greyscale component of an image is manipulated via the
CT numbers
Wide window
Defined as 400-2000 HU best used in areas of acute differing attenuation values, a good example is lungs or cortical tissue, where air
and vessels will sit side by side
Typical window width and levels:
●
brain W:80 L:40
●
mediastinum W:350 L:50
●
lungs W:1500 L:-600
●
liver W:150 L:30
