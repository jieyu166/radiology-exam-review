#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Which of the following description regarding Milwaukee Shoulder is **FALSE**?
(A) Complete rotator cuff tear
(B) Rapidly progressive osteoarthritic changes
(C) Typically involve older men
(D) Frequent history of trauma
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。此題問「何者敘述錯誤」。C 錯誤：Milwaukee shoulder（鹼性磷酸鈣結晶破壞性關節病變）最常見於老年女性（約 50–90 歲），並非老年男性。其餘皆正確：A 常見完全性旋轉肌袖撕裂；B 呈快速進行的破壞性骨關節炎變化；D 患側常有創傷病史。

## 詳解附圖（OCR）
**Milwaukee Shoulder** = association of
1. complete rotator cuff tear
2. osteoarthritic changes
3. noninflammatory joint effusion containing calcium hydroxyapatite and calcium pyrophosphate dihydrate (CPPD) crystals
4. hyperplasia of synovium
5. destruction of cartilage + subchondral bone
6. multiple osteochondral loose bodies

- **Age:** older woman
- frequent history of trauma
