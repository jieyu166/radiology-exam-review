#交換 #2021交換 #ABD A 52-year-old male with autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease presents with 12 hours of anuria and abdominal pain postoperative day 1 status post renal transplantation. Which of the following is the most likely explanation for the spectral waveform?
(A) Renal artery stenosis
(B) Renal arteriovenous fistula
(C) Renal vein thrombosis
(D) Normal superimposed waveforms
??
**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】
Reversed diastolic flow in the transplant renal artery => highly suggestive of renal vein thrombosis.
Other less common cause:
Disorders such as allograft torsion
Severe allograft rejection
Acute tubular necrosis (ATN)
