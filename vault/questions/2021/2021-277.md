#交換 #2021交換 #NR For external acoustic canal (EAC) atresia, which one is less likely?
(A) Dysmorphic malleus and incus
(B) Normal morphology of stapes
(C) Aberrant course of tympanic and mastoid portions of facial nerve
(D) Inner ear and internal acoustic canal are usually stenotic
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】Of the syndromes resulting in microtia and EAC atresia, Goldenhar syndrome or hemifacial microsomia is
the most common and is also the second most common craniofacial anomaly after cleft lip and palate.
There are four major components to Goldenhar syndrome: deformity of the auricle, EAC atresia,
malformation of the tympanic cavity, and ossicular abnormalities causing conductive hearing loss.
Occasionally, there is also sensorineural hearing loss due to stria vascularis and semicircular canal or
cochlear abnormalities as well as hypoplastic or atretic oval window. Pfeiffer syndrome is another cause of
EAC stenosis/atresia that results in moderate to severe conductive or mixed hearing loss in patients with
cranio-facial abnormalities. In addition to the stenosis and/or atresia of the EAC, there is hypoplasia of the
middle ear cavity and occasionally hypoplastic ossicles. Typically, the inner ear anatomy is normal, but
middle ear effusions are frequently seen. Therefore, when Pfeiffer syndrome patients receive CT for
craniofacial anomalies, examination of the temporal bones should also be performed.
