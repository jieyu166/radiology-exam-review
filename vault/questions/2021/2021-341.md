#交換 #2021交換 #CH According to IASLC Lymph Node Classification for Lung Cancer Staging, which one is **wrong**?
(A) The upper border of the supraclavicular zone is the lower margin of cricoid cartilage
(B) The lower border of the left upper paratracheal lymph node is the superior border of the aortic arch
(C) The boundary of the right and left upper paratracheal lymph nodes is the midline of trachea
(D) The lower border of right lower paratracheal lymph nodes is the lower border of the azygos vein
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。依 IASLC 淋巴結分站，右側與左側上（及下）氣管旁淋巴結的分界是氣管的「左外側壁（left lateral tracheal wall）」，而非氣管中線；2R／4R 會越過中線延伸至氣管左緣，這是 IASLC nodal map 的核心特徵，故 (C) 以「midline」為界的敘述錯誤，為本題答案。其餘皆正確：(A) 鎖骨上區的上界為環狀軟骨下緣、(B) 左上氣管旁（2L）淋巴結下界為主動脈弓上緣、(D) 右下氣管旁（4R）淋巴結下界為奇靜脈下緣。附帶記憶點：胸廓入口在軸位 CT 上最可靠的標誌是可見的左右第一肋。
