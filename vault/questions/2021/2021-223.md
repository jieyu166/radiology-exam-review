#交換 #2021交換 #CH Screening low dose CT is appropriate for asymptomatic individuals at high risk for lung cancer. How much is the radiation dose of the technique should be set to yield a dose index volume (CTDIvol) for a standard-sized patient by ACR–STR?
(A) 1 mGy or less
(B) 3 mGy or less
(C) 5 mGy or less
(D) 10 mGy or less
??
**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】Key Elements
• One breath-hold (thoracic motion is problematic);
• Thin image thicknesses (≤2.5 mm, ≤1.0 mm preferred); reconstruction of coronal and sagittal
reformations as well as MIPS may be helpful and are encouraged.
• CTDIvol < 3.0 mGy for a standard sized patient (see table), with adjustments made for smaller and larger
patients.
• This typically requires a 16 detector row (or greater) scanner to meet these requirements.
