#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Which of the following components of the ulnar collateral ligament is often intact even when the radial head and coronoid process are both fractured ?
(A) Anterior bundles
(B) Transverse bundles
(C) Posterior bundles
(D) None of above
??
**Ans: A**
答案 A。anterior bundle 為 UCL（MCL 複合體）抗外翻的主力，附著於 coronoid 的 sublime tubercle。肘關節骨折-脫位時 coronoid 常為小的 tip 型骨折，位置在 sublime tubercle 遠端，故即使 radial head 與 coronoid 同時骨折，anterior bundle 的附著點常未被波及而保持完整。B transverse（Cooper's）bundle 幾乎不參與肘關節穩定；C posterior bundle 附著於內側鷹嘴，非本題所指；D none of above 錯，因 A 成立。→ [[ulnar-collateral-ligament-injury]]

## 詳解附圖（OCR）
**Box 2. Elbow instability: teaching points**

1. The most important static soft tissue constraints are the LUCL and the anterior bundle of the UCL.
2. Caution should be exercised when making the diagnosis of partial UCL detachment: remember the striated appearance of the ligament.
3. UCL midsubstance disruptions account for most traumatic injuries.
4. Conventional MR imaging shows sensitivity, specificity, and accuracy that approach 100% for full-thickness UCL tears.
5. The anterior bundle of the UCL is often intact even when the radial head and coronoid process are both fractured.
6. Posttraumatic laxity, disruption, or avulsion of the LUCL is considered to be the main contributing factor in PLRI.
7. The most common injury to the LUCL is soft tissue avulsion at the proximal humeral attachment, often with concomitant injury to the common origin of the RCL.
8. Elbow dislocation is most commonly associated with a torn capsule, but the capsule may be intact if the coronoid process is fractured.
9. An abnormal posterolateral plicae usually reflects incompetence of the UCL.
