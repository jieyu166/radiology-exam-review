#交換 #2021交換 #CH Which one is not radiation-induced malignancy of thorax?
(A) Sarcoma
(B) Breast cancer
(C) Lung cancer
(D) Mesothelioma
(E) None of the above
??
**Ans: E**
答案 E。胸部放療的晚期併發症之一是放射線誘發的續發惡性腫瘤，已報告者包括 (A) 肉瘤、(B) 乳癌、(C) 肺癌；而 (D) 間皮瘤的致病因子除石綿外，放射線暴露亦為公認因素之一（官方另提食道癌亦有報告）。由於四個具體選項皆屬已報告的放射線誘發胸部惡性腫瘤，沒有任何一項可被排除，故「何者不是」的答案為 (E) 以上皆非。判讀上，續發惡性多潛伏數年至數十年、出現於或鄰近舊照射野並隨追蹤逐漸增大，可藉此與治療野內短期的預期放射性變化區分。

**TEACHING POINTS**
- When the intent in treating the primary malignancy is curative, the goal in performing radiation therapy is to deliver a therapeutic radiation dose to the target tissue in a precise and accurate manner while minimizing the radiation delivered to surrounding normal structures.
- Pulmonary opacities due to radiation damage are usually confined to the radiation portal, as radiation pneumonitis outside the radiation field is extremely rare.
- During the first few months after radiation therapy, there is diffuse FDG uptake in the area of radiation pneumonitis. Owing to this uptake, the sensitivity of FDG PET for the detection of any residual FDG-avid tumor in the radiation field, while the inflammation is still active, is decreased. Therefore, it is best to perform FDG PET at least 6 months after the completion of radiation therapy.
- To correctly interpret chest imaging findings after thoracic irradiation, the radiologist must be familiar with the newer radiation delivery techniques, the date of the last radiation therapy delivery, the treatment dose, and the treatment field. This information helps in distinguishing radiation-induced changes from infection or malignancy progression.
- Direct radiation damage to the heart may affect the coronary arteries, valves, pericardium, and/or myocardium. These cardiac effects are associated with increased mortality and may take their toll years after the radiation therapy; thus, they are usually seen in long-term survivors whose mediastinum was irradiated when they were younger.
