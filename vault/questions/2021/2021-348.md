#交換 #2021交換 #CV About cardiac amyloidosis, which description is **wrong**?
(A) In cardiac amyloidosis, the typical LGE pattern involves global epicardial LGE distributed in a non–coronary artery territory with a dark blood pool
(B) Many patients with heart failure, particularly those with HFpEF, also have “hidden” cardiac amyloidosis
(C) Most patients with cardiac amyloidosis remain undiagnosed and do not receive appropriate treatment
(D) Cardiac amyloidosis generally is associated with a poor prognosis, especially for patients with advanced heart failure
??
**Ans: A**
【2021 官方詳解】C.
In cardiac amyloidosis, typical LGE pattern involves global subendocardial  LGE distributed in a
non–coronary artery territory with a dark blood pool.
