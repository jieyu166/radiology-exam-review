#交換 #2021交換 #ABD With regard to endometrial carcinoma staging, which of the following is **true**?
(A) Bladder bullous edema represents stage IV disease
(B) Myometrial invasion represents stage IB disease
(C) Invasion of cervical stroma correspond to an IB stage
(D) Must include evaluation of the upper abdomen in grade 3 endometrioid and non-endometrioid tumors
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。Grade 3 內膜樣及非內膜樣（高惡性度）腫瘤較易發生腹膜與上腹部轉移，分期評估須納入上腹部影像，故 D 正確、為正解。A 錯：真正膀胱黏膜侵犯才屬 stage IVA，單純 bullous edema（膀胱黏膜水腫）不足以證明侵犯；B 錯：肌層侵犯（<50%）屬 IA，≥50% 深層侵犯才是 IB；C 錯：子宮頸間質侵犯屬 stage II，而非 IB。→ [[endometrial-cancer-mri]]
