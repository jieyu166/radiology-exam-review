(A) Bladder bullous edema represents stage IV disease
(B) Myometrial invasion represents stage IB disease
(C) Invasion of cervical stroma correspond to an IB stage
(D) Must include evaluation of the upper abdomen in grade 3 endometrioid and non-endometrioid tumors
??
