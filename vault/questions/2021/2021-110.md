#交換 #2021交換 #PED In the pediatric population, which of the following clinical scenario is indicated for voiding cystourethrography (VCUG)?
(A) Recurrent urinary tract infection
(B) Congenital anomalies of the genitourinary tract
(C) Neurogenic dysfunction of the urinary bladder
(D) All of the above
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。反覆泌尿道感染（A）、先天泌尿生殖道異常（B）與神經性膀胱功能障礙（C）皆為兒童 VCUG 的公認適應症，故正解為「以上皆是」。VCUG 於泌尿道感染的 bottom-up 評估（併腎臟超音波）、先天異常的解剖評估，以及續發性 VUR（如神經性膀胱、輸尿管膨出、後尿道瓣膜）的偵測皆有明確角色，三種情境都適用。

## 詳解附圖（OCR）
Clinical indications for VCUG include, but are not limited to, the following:

- Hydronephrosis and/or hydroureter
- Abnormal US (any degree of hydronephrosis, uroepithelial thickening, scarring) after first urinary tract infection (UTI), especially if febrile or non-Escherichia coli (E. coli)
- Recurrent UTI
- Congenital anomalies of the urinary tract
- Dysfunctional voiding, such as neurogenic dysfunction of the bladder
- Urinary incontinence
- Bladder outlet obstruction
- Postoperative evaluation of the urinary tract
- Dysuria/difficulty voiding
- Hematuria
- Trauma
