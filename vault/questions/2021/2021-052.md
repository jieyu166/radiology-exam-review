#交換 #2021交換 #NR Regarding moyamoya syndrome, which of the following causes is not an established etiology?
(A) Neuroﬁbromatosis type I
(B) Down syndrome
(C) Kasabach-Merritt syndrome
(D) Bacterial meningitis
??
**Ans: C**
【2021 官方詳解】Thyroid disease: Graves’ disease….
Moyamoya syndrome after bacterial meningitis => case report
Kasabach-Merritt syndrome  (hemangioma thrombocytopenia syndrome)
Rapidly growing vascular tumor =>  thrombocytopenia, microangiopathic hemolytic anemia and
consumptive coagulopathy
DLP based method:
CT dose estimates =  DLP* conversion factor
DLP= CTDIvol *Scan length
C: 20mGy*30cm*0.002mSv/mGy*cm= 1.2mSv
答案
3
題目
陳先生今年67歲，剛做完頭部電腦斷層，急著想知道他接受了多少輻射劑量。根據檢查
參數：
64切電腦斷層
kVp 120
0.6-sec rotation time
CTDIvol 20 mGy
Scan length 30 cm
Pitch 2
雖然光憑以上數據並無法用來精確計算陳先生接受的輻射劑量，但若以 CT dose index 的
概念來進行估算，假設 k conversion coefficient factor 為 0.002 mSv/(mGy*cm)，請問此次檢
查的有效劑量(effective dose)大約是多少？
選項
1. 0.6 毫西弗(mSv)
2. 1 毫西弗(mSv)
3. 1.2 毫西弗(mSv)
4. 2 毫西弗(mSv)
