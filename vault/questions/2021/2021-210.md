#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which of the following imaging appearance is NOT related to agenesis of corpus callosum?
(A) Racing car sign
(B) Moose head sign
(C) Colpocephaly
(D) Molar tooth sign
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】Ventricles:
Racing car appearance on axial image (ventricles run parallel)
Colpocephaly: dilatation of trigones and occipital horns 🡪 Moose head, viking helmet appearance
*** Molar tooth sign: Joubert syndrome
