#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which of the following imaging appearance is NOT related to agenesis of corpus callosum?
(A) Racing car sign
(B) Moose head sign
(C) Colpocephaly
(D) Molar tooth sign
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。臼齒徵象（molar tooth sign）為 Joubert 症候群及相關疾病之中腦徵象（上小腦腳拉長似臼齒），與胼胝體發育不全（ACC）無關；A 賽車徵象（racing car sign）、B 麋鹿頭徵象（moose head sign）與 C 枕角擴大（colpocephaly）皆源自 Probst bundles 造成側腦室平行外移／後角不成比例擴大，為 ACC 之典型影像表現。題目問「無關」者，故選 D。→ [[corpus-callosum-agenesis-signs]]
