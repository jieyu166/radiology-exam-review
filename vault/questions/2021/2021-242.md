#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which of the following is a described characteristic of villous adenomas?
(A) They are the most common type of adenoma
(B) They are usually smaller than other types of adenoma
(C) Among the adenomatous polyps, they have the lowest potential for malignancy
(D) They are considered one type of advanced adenoma
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
Adenomas are divided into three subtypes: tubular adenomas, tubulovillous adenomas, and villous adenomas.
Villous adenomas are one type of advanced adenoma. These lesions are typically larger than tubular adenomas—75% of them are larger than 2 cm—and they have a higher potential for malignant transformation.
Tubular adenomas are the most common, accounting for more than 80% of adenomas, and they have tubular glands or crowded branching tubules embedded in the lamina propria. Villous adenomas are the least common of the adenomas, accounting for less than 5% of cases.
