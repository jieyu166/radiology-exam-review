#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which of the following imaging findings is the least likely related to cerebral autosomal dominant arteriopathy with subcortical infarcts and leukoencephalopathy (CADASIL)?
(A) Diffuse cerebral leukoencephalopathy
(B) Intracranial major vessel stenosis
(C) Microbleeds in the thalamus and subcortical regions
(D) Multiple old lacunes in the cerebral white matter
??
**Ans: B**
答案 B。CADASIL 屬小血管病，侵犯腦與軟腦膜的穿通小動脈，傳統血管攝影（DSA）正常，不會出現顱內大血管狹窄，故 B（顱內主要血管狹窄）與 CADASIL 最不相關；A 瀰漫白質腦病、C 視丘與皮質下微出血、D 白質內多發陳舊性腔隙皆為 CADASIL 典型影像表現。→ [[lacunar-infarction]]
