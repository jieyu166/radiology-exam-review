#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which is **wrong** about long common channel of common bile duct and pancreatic duct?
(A) An anatomic variant rather than acquired etiology
(B) Increased incidence of pancreatitis
(C) Association with type 1 choledochal cyst
(D) Two ducts (common bile duct and main pancreatic duct) fusion exactly at but not prior to the sphincter of Oddi complex
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。APBDU 的胰膽管匯合發生在十二指腸壁「外」、且位於 Oddi 括約肌「近端（之前）」，形成過長共同通道使括約肌無法調控交界；故 D「恰在括約肌處而非其近端匯合」的描述錯誤，即為本題要選的錯誤敘述。A 正確：屬先天解剖變異而非後天；B 正確：胰液反流使胰臟炎風險增加；C 正確：與第一型膽總管囊腫（choledochal cyst）高度相關。→ [[anomalous-pancreatobiliary-duct-union]]
