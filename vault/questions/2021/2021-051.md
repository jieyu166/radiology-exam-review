#交換 #2021交換 #NR Mixed tumor of salivary gland is associated with all of the following choices **EXCEPT**?
(A) The most common benign neoplasms of major salivary glands.
(B) Also named pleomorphic adenoma.
(C) Mixed tumors are usually solitary and unilateral.
(D) Mixed tumors occur most often in the submandibular gland.
(E) At US, pleomorphic adenomas are hypoechoic, well-defined, lobulated tumors with posterior acoustic enhancement and may contain calcifications.
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。本題問混合瘤（pleomorphic adenoma）「除何者以外」皆相關，D 稱它最常發生於下頜下腺為錯——多形性腺瘤絕大多數發生於腮腺（約 84%，且多在淺葉），下頜下腺僅約 8%、小唾液腺約 6.5%。A 正確：為主要唾液腺最常見的良性腫瘤。B 正確：即又名 pleomorphic adenoma。C 正確：通常為單發、單側。E 正確：超音波下呈低回音、邊界清楚、分葉狀、具後方聲增強，並可含鈣化。→ [[salivary-gland-ultrasound]]
