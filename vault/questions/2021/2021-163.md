#交換 #2021交換 #ABD 有關Meckel's diverticulum 下列何者為非?
(A) The most common congenital anomaly of the gastrointestinal tract
(B) It resulted from improper absorption of allantois
(C) Symptomatic Meckel's diverticulum is more common in men than women
(D) Hemorrhage is the most common complication
??
**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】
Meckel’s diverticulum
fibrous degeneration of the umbilical end of the omphalomesenteric (vitelline) duct
** allantois(尿囊) 🡪 Urachus
the most common structural congenital anomaly of the gastrointestinal tract
Complication:
- GI hemorrhage (most common)
- Small bowel obstruction (2nd most common)
- Meckel diverticulitis
complications are more common in men than in women, with a ratio of aproximately 2:1
