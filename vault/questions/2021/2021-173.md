#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which statement is “**WRONG**” about dural AVF (arteriovenous fistula)?
(A) Most common site: cavernous sinus
(B) Symptoms vary with site, type of shunt
(C) Enlarged cortical draining veins presuming aggressive DAVF
(D) Cognard classification correlates venous drainage patterns with increasingly aggressive neurological clinical course and stratifies the risk of intracerebral hemorrhage
??
**Ans: A**
答案 A。硬腦膜動靜脈瘻管（DAVF）最常見部位為橫竇–乙狀竇（transverse／sigmoid sinus）而非海綿竇，故 A（most common site: cavernous sinus）敘述錯誤（此為常見誤植陷阱）；B：症狀確實隨瘻管部位與分流型態而異，正確；C：皮質引流靜脈擴大代表血流逆流入皮質靜脈系統，屬侵犯性（aggressive）DAVF 之影像標記，正確；D：Cognard 分級依靜脈引流型態分層，與臨床病程侵犯性及顱內出血風險相關，正確；題目問「錯誤」者，故選 A。→ [[dural-avf]]
