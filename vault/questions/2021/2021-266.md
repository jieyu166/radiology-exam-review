#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Chose the correct option:
(A) The talus ossification center ossifies after the calcaneous ossification center does, and is radiographically invisible after birth
(B) The primary ossification centers have high signal intensity on T2WI and low signal intensity on T1WI due to high vascularity
(C) The “starry sky apperance” of secondary ossification centers is commonly seen in hindfoot
(D) The “starry sky appearance” in primary ossification centers may persist in young adults
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】(A) The tibial and fibular diaphyseal ossification centers are partially ossified in the prenatal period (by
approximately 7–9 weeks in utero), as are the calcaneus (3 monthsin utero) and talus (6 months in utero).
Therefore, these centers are visualized radiographically from birth.
(B) On MR images, normal primary ossification centers are predominantly T1 hyperintense and
are hypointense with STIR and fat-suppressed T2-weighted sequences, corresponding to yellow marrow
signal intensity. . Early on, secondary ossification centers may demonstrate low T1-weighted and high
T2-weighted or STIR signal intensity, distinct from the background of the cartilage precursor, and reflect red
marrow composition.
(C) In children, normal primary ossification centers can have inhomogeneous marrow signal intensity, with
multiple foci of rounded T2-weighted or STIR hyperintensities, creating a starry sky appearance.
