#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Choose the correct option:
(A) At PDFS and T1WI , bone marrow edema has high signal intensity
(B) Presence of bone marrow edema indicates the onset of irreversible bone changes in inflammatory arthritis
(C) In rheumatoid arthritis, bone marrow edema begins at the bare areas of the joint and spread to the entire phalanx
(D) In psoriatic arthritis, bone marrow edema begins at the corner of the phalanx at the enthesis of the capsule
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
(A) low signal intensity
(B) In contrast to radiographic erosions, which reflect irreversible bone damage, bone marrow edema is
considered to be a potentially reversible phenomenon.
(C)(D) Bone marrow edema in RA is usually located adjacent to bare areas. In PsA, bone marrow edema is thought to begin at the corner of the phalanx at the enthesis of the capsule and spread to involve the entire bone as the disease progresses.
