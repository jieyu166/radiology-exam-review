#交換 #2021交換 #Unknown About **gout**, which of the following is **not** appropriate?
(A) Gout is a metabolic disease that results from monosodium urate crystal deposits in the synovium and juxta-articular tissues
(B) The peak incidence occurs at 30–50 years of age
(C) Gouty lesions mainly involve axial skeleton such as laminae, pedicles, vertebral bodies, ligaments, facet joints, intervertebral disks, and epidural spaces
(D) CT is helpful in depicting bone erosions caused by tophi, which are present in 50% of cases of tophaceous gout, as well as soft-tissue involvement
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。痛風病灶主要侵犯周邊關節（以第一蹠趾關節 podagra 最典型，其次手足小關節、踝、腕），呈不對稱多關節分布；中軸骨（椎板、椎弓根、椎體、韌帶、facet、椎間盤、硬膜外腔）雖「可能」受侵，但並非主要部位，故稱「主要侵犯中軸骨」為錯。其餘正確：A 痛風為單鈉尿酸鹽（MSU）結晶沉積於滑膜與關節旁組織所致的代謝性關節病；B 發病高峰約 30–50 歲；D CT 對痛風石造成的骨糜爛與軟組織侵犯顯示佳，骨糜爛見於約半數（50%）慢性痛風石病例。→ [[gout-imaging]]
