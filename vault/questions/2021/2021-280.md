#交換 #2021交換 #NR Regarding to acute transverse myelitis, which of the following statements are **wrong**?
(A) It is the most common cause of acute myelitis
(B) It is a diagnosis of exclusion
(C) CSF inflammation with unsual pleocytosis and occasionally elevated IgG is common
(D) Long extensive transverse myelitis (LETM) pattern on T2W MRI is pathogomonic acute transverse myelitis
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。長節段橫貫性脊髓炎（LETM ≥3 椎節）並非特發性急性橫貫性脊髓炎的特徵性（pathognomonic）表現，反而高度提示 NMOSD（抗 AQP4 星狀細胞病），故 D 為錯誤敘述；A 大致正確，發炎性（特發性）橫貫性脊髓炎是急性脊髓炎最常見的成因；B 正確，特發性 ATM 屬排除性診斷；C 正確，常見 CSF 發炎、可有 pleocytosis 及偶爾 IgG 上升。→ [[neuromyelitis-optica]]
