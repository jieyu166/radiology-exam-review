#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Which of the following statements regarding axial spondyloarthritis (AxSpA) is incorrect?
(A) The sensitivity of X ray to detect sacroiliitis is low and is easily missed
(B) If imaging finding of X ray is negative and non-radiographic axial spondyloarthritis (nr-AxSpA) is suspected, MRI is strongly recommended. If MRI is not available, CT scan may be used instead
(C) Dunn view is a modified AP pelvic X-ray to produce a clearer accessed view of the sacroiliac joint
(D) Ultrasound is recommended for peripheral SpA evaluation, but is not recommended for axSpA
(E) Bone marrow edema is an important hallmark of acute inflammatory lesion or osteitis whereas fat deposition and erosions represent chronic structural change
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。本題問「何者錯誤」，C 稱 Dunn view 是為了更清楚顯示薦腸關節而改良的 AP 骨盆攝影，此敘述張冠李戴——正確者應為 Ferguson view（向頭側傾斜的 AP，使射束對齊薦腸關節斜面）；Dunn view 其實是評估股骨頭頸 offset／股髖夾擊（FAI）的髖部投照，與薦腸關節無關。A 正確：X 光偵測 sacroiliitis 敏感度低、早期易漏。B 正確：X 光陰性而懷疑 nr-axSpA 時強烈建議 MRI，無 MRI 時可改用 CT。D 正確：超音波用於周邊型 SpA 評估，不建議用於中軸（axSpA）。E 正確：骨髓水腫是急性發炎／骨炎的重要標誌，脂肪沉積與侵蝕則代表慢性結構變化。
