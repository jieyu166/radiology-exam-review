#交換 #2021交換 #PED Which of the following descriptions about VACTERL association is incorrect?
(A) VACTERL association is three or more anomalies that are not pathogenetically related, and the etiology of associations is not defined.
(B) The finding of one abnormality should prompt the clinician to look for related anomalies.
(C) V stands for ventricular septal defect.
(D) The association does not include cranial / CNS anomalies.
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。本題問關於 VACTERL association 何者「錯誤」。C 錯：VACTERL 的「V」代表 Vertebral（椎體異常），而非 ventricular septal defect（心臟異常另由字母 C＝Cardiac 涵蓋）。A 正確：VACTERL 定義為三項以上、彼此無共同致病機轉的先天異常之非隨機聚集，病因未明。B 正確：一旦發現其中一項異常，臨床上應系統性搜尋其他相關畸形。D 正確：VACTERL 本身不包含顱骨／中樞神經系統異常。
