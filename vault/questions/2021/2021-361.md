#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Which of the following descriptions about rheumatoid arthritis (RA) is correct?
(A) For most cases of RA, the diagnosis can be made clinically and image study has no role in the early detection of RA
(B) The carpometacarpal (CMC) joints are among the most common and earliest eroded joints
(C) For the diagnosis of synovitis, the sensitivity of ultrasound is inferior to contrast enhanced MR imaging
(D) In RA, the presence of bone marrow edema can predict the development of erosion
(E) Tuberculous arthritis and RA can have overlapping imaging findings and presence of uniform synovial thickening, large size of bone erosion with rim enhancement, and extra-articular cystic masses favors a diagnosis of RA
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。本題問「何者正確」，D 正確——骨髓水腫（BME）是 RA 活動性發炎的表現，可先於軟骨下囊腫與侵蝕出現，並且是後續 erosion 最重要的預測因子，只能以水敏感 MRI 序列偵測。A 錯：RA 早期變化為非骨性（滑膜炎、積液、BME），診斷不確定時 US 或 MRI 反而有助早期偵測與提升診斷信心，影像並非「無角色」。B 錯：最早、最常受侵蝕的是蹠趾（MTP）關節而非腕掌（CMC）關節，第 5 蹠趾關節蹠側為良好評估起點。C 錯：偵測滑膜炎時，超音波（含 power Doppler）與對比增強 MRI 的敏感度相當，並非 US 明顯較差。E 錯：均勻滑膜增厚、大型且緣強化的骨侵蝕、關節外囊性腫塊反而偏向結核性關節炎，而非 RA。
