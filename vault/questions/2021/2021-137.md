#交換 #2021交換 #CV Regarding coronary CTA, which one of the followings is most likely to be **wrong**?
(A) For optimal interpretation, once you find different degree of coronary stenosis in 2 different cardiac phases (with optimal image quality), you should prefer the one with more severe stenosis
(B) For optimal assessment, it is better to have at least two phases of coronary images in cases of CAD RADS 3 or 4 lesions
(C) In comparison with the invasive coronary angiography, coronary diameter stenosis at systolic phase is more pronounced than that at diastolic phase
(D) Overestimation of stenosis is more pronounced in calcified lesions. In general, it is not necessary to evaluate CTA (with good image quality) in multiple phases once you find a lesion which is CAD-RADS 1
??
**Ans: A**
答案 A。CT 血管攝影相較侵入性導管攝影（ICA）本就系統性「高估」狹窄，且收縮期高估幅度大於舒張期；若在兩個皆屬良質的期相看到不同狹窄程度時，一律挑「較嚴重」的那一期，只會放大這種系統性高估，因此 A 的作法錯誤，為本題所選。其餘皆正確：(B) CAD-RADS 3、4 病灶宜至少取兩個期相綜合判讀；(C) 相較 ICA，收縮期量得的管徑狹窄較舒張期明顯（QCT 收縮期高估約 9.4–11.9%、舒張期約 5.7–8.5%）；(D) 鈣化病灶因 blooming 高估更明顯，而 CAD-RADS 1 的良質影像通常不需多期相評估。→ [[ccta]]
