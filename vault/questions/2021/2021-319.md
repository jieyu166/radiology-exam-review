#交換 #2021交換 #NR All of the following locations are relative T2 hyperintensity in health term neonates, EXCEPT? (題目應該改為T2 hypointensity)
(A) Far lateral putamen
(B) Posterior limb of the internal capsule
(C) Ventrolateral thalamus
(D) Medial globus pallidus
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】Hypoxic-Ischemic Injury in Term Neonates
Peripheral pattern:
- mild hypoxia or ischemia of prolonged duration
- predominant in  cerebral cortex and subcortical white matter along the inter-vascular boundary zones.
Basal ganglia–thalamus pattern
- secondary to more severe hypoxic or ischemic event of short duration
- abnormal hyperintensity on T1WI and hypointensity on T2WI in posterolateral putamen and ventrolateral
thalamus.
- Associated loss of normal hyperintensity on T1WI and  hypointensity on T2WI  in the posterior
limb of the internal capsule
