#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which is not significantly associated with good clinical outcomes in endovascular thrombectomy for acute ischemic stroke?
(A) Using of IV-tPA
(B) Collateral status on CTA
(C) mTICI grade
(D) NIHSS at admission
(E) General anesthesia
??
**Ans: E**
【2021 官方詳解】Pretreatment collateral statuses, NIHSS scores, time lapses between symptoms to needle, and the
occurrence of hemorrhage at 24 h are all significant predictors for good outcomes with odds ratios of 2.88,
0.91, 0.99, and 0.31 respectively.
