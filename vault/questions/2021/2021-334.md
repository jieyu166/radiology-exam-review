#交換 #2021交換 #MSK An MRI appearance of homogeneous low T1-and T2-weighted signal with enhancement is MOST typical of which foot and ankle mass?
(A) Morton's neuroma
(B) Lipoma
(C) Ganglion cyst
(D) Plantar fibromatosis
(E) Hemangioma
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
Morton neuromas ( interdigital or intermetatarsal neuromas=> misnomer)
focal areas of symptomatic perineural fibrosis around a plantar digital nerve of the foot,
due to chronic entrapment of the nerve by the intermetatarsal ligament.
=> Morton metatarsalgia
Mulder sign (Sign of physical examination)
Location: The 3rd web-space (between 3rd and 4th metatarsal heads) is the most commonly affected site
US:
round to ovoid, well-defined, hypoechoic lesion in the intermetatarsal space proximal to the metatarsal head. Not compressible
MRI
Dumbbell/ovoid-shaped lesion at a similar position to that described on ultrasound:
T1: typically low-to-iso signal
T2: typically low signal but can sometimes be intermediate in signal
T1 C+ (Gd): variable enhancement
