#交換 #2021交換 #Breast 以下何者為乳癌在breast MRI最可能之表現？
(A) Very high signal on T2WI
(B) dark internal septation on T2WI and dynamic contrast-enhanced MRI且 oval-shape
(C) 和舊片相比沒有變化，且呈現persistent kinetic pattern
(D) 在dynamic contrast-enhanced MRI有clustered ring之顯影表現。
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】A.
Bright T2WI lesion => cysts, lymph nodes and fat necrosis. These are all benign lesions.
Unfortunately there is one malignant lesion that has a high signal intensity on T2WI => colloid carcinoma.
B.
Dark internal septation on T2WI and dynamic contrast-enhanced MRI, oval-shape;
Classical picture of fibroadenoma
D.
Irregular or spiculated margin, cancer diagnosis (PPV 84%– 91%)
Rim like enhancement , cancer diagnosis (PPV, 84%)
Clumped enhancement , (PPV, 60% PPV)
linear or branching ductal distribution of enhancement (PPV 24% to 85%)
