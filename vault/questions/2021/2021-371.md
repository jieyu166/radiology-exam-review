#交換 #2021交換 #PED Concerning Wyburn-Masson syndrome, which one is incorrect?
(A) Simultaneous involvement of both intracranial and maxillofacial regions is rare
(B) Regarded as a cerebrofacial arteriovenous metameric syndrome (CAMS)
(C) Orbital involvement is not unusual
(D) May be with Unilateral or bilateral craniofacial AVMs
??
**Ans: A**
【2021 官方詳解】
B. typically unilateral vascular malformations that primarily involve the brain, orbits and facial structures
Wyburn-Mason syndrome
Nonhereditary neurocutaneous disorder=> craniofacial arteriovenous metameric syndrome (CAMS)
typically presents with unilateral vascular malformations that primarily involve the brain, orbits and facial structures
Features include:
facial vascular nevus
visual pathway and orbital arteriovenous malformations (AVMs), e.g. racemose angioma
intracranial arteriovenous malformations (AVMs) commonly in midbrain
