#交換 #2021交換 #Breast 以下何者為是？
(A) Vaccine-induced lymphadenopathy can present as a diagnostic dilemma for radiologists
(B) Unilateral lymphadenopathy is rarely reported with vaccines as BCG, influenza, and HPV vaccines
(C) Documentation of vaccination status can decrease unnecessary biopsy and alleviate patient anxiety
(D) 以上皆是
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
Current imaging fuidelines related to vaccine-induced axillary lymphadenopathy.
- Document vaccination dates, injection site/laterality, vaccine type
- Scheduling exam: Screening exam prior to 1st dose or 4-6 weeks following 2nd dose
- Incidentally detected unilateral lymphadenopathy on imaging:
  - BIRADS-0 🡪 if COVID19 vaccine in 4 weeks 🡪 BIRADS-3 🡪 follow up in 4-12 weeks 🡪 if persisted 🡪 BIRADS-4
  - Another suggestion: BIRADS-2 after recent COVID 19 vaccination in ipsilateral arm
