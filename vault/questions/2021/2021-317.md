#交換 #2021交換 #PED 最常見的 pediatric liver tumor, 是以下哪一種 ?
(A) Infantile hemangioendothelioma
(B) Mesenchymal hamartoma
(C) Hepatoblastoms
(D) Focal nodular hyperplasia
(E) Hepatocellular carcinoma
??
**Ans: A**
【2021 官方詳解】
Infantile hemangioendothelioma, or infantile hepatic hemangioma, is a vascular neoplasm and
the most common benign hepatic tumor of infancy.
