#交換 #2021交換 #NR Regarding to Fabry disease, which one is **wrong**
(A) An X-linked lysosomal storage disorder of glycosphingolipid metabolism
(B) NECT: bilateral, often symmetric calcification in basal ganglia and thalami
(C) Never dolichoectasia
(D) Abnormal accumulation of glycosphingolipids in various tissues
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。法布瑞氏症典型即會出現基底動脈 dolichoectasia（醣鞘脂堆積導致的血管病變），故「Never dolichoectasia（絕不會擴張迂曲）」為錯誤敘述。(A) 正確：X 性聯之溶酶體儲積症、α-半乳糖苷酶 A 缺乏所致醣鞘脂代謝障礙。(B) 正確：NECT 上可見雙側、常對稱之基底核與視丘鈣化。(D) 正確：醣鞘脂異常堆積於多種組織（血管內皮、腎、心、神經等）。→ [[fabry-disease-pulvinar]]
