## 詳解附圖（OCR）
The location of the tumor is an important factor for the prognosis (27). Main duct IPMNs are more likely to be malignant, with approximately 60%–70% of cases demonstrating invasive carcinoma (28,29), whereas only 22% of branch duct IPMNs demonstrate foci of carcinoma (30). IPMNs are frequently multifocal, and 5%–10% involve the entire pancreas (31).
