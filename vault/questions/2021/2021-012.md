#交換 #2021交換 #Breast Which of the following statement about breast sarcoidosis of the breast is **incorrect**?
(A) Ultrasound shows an irregular, hypoechoic mass
(B) Enlarged axillary or intramammary lymph nodes are rare.
(C) Mammograms shows ill-defined, lobular, solid masses with equal densities.
(D) MRI shows irregularly shaped masses with spiculations and heterogeneous internal enhancement.
??
**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】Breast sarcoidosis
Unilateral or bilateral disorder
Mammography:  irregular or ill-defined and/or spiculated masses
US:  irregular hypoechoic masses, as well as enlarged axillary or intramammary  lymph nodes
MRI:
Irregular shaped mass with heterogeneous internal enhancement pattern
rapid enhancement and washout phenomenon or gradually increasing signal intensity
Ref:
Breast sarcoidosis: Clinical features, imaging, and histological findings
