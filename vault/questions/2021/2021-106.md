#交換 #2021交換 #US Which one of the followings is not the sonographic feature of liver cirrhosis?
(A) The ratio of the caudate lobe width to the right lobe width is an indicator of cirrhosis
(B) The nodularity corresponds to the presence of regenerative nodules and fibrosis
(C) Regenerating nodules tend to be isoechoic or hyperechoic with a thin hypoechoic border
(D) In patients with liver cirrhosis, the normal increase in postprandial resistive index is blunted
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。本題要選「非」肝硬化超音波徵象/敘述有誤者。
(C) 錯在「邊界」的描述——再生結節與正常肝結構相近，傾向 isoechoic 或 hypoechoic，且帶一圈薄的「高回音（echogenic）」邊界（對應纖維脂肪結締組織），而非題目所述的低回音（hypoechoic）邊界。
其餘皆為肝硬化徵象：(A) 尾葉相對右葉的寬度比可作為硬化指標（尾葉因獨立靜脈回流而代償肥大）；(B) 表面結節狀對應再生結節與纖維化；(D) 肝硬化病人進食後肝動脈阻力指數（RI）正常應上升的反應被鈍化。
