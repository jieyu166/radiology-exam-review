#交換 #2021交換 #2023交換 #MSK Which of the following statements about fractures around the ankle is **TRUE**?
(A) **Pilon fractures** are usually due to external rotation injuries.
(B) Extension of a **talar neck** fracture through the talar dome articular surface is associated with a high rate of posttraumatic arthritis.
(C) The risk of **talar dome** avascular necrosis after a talar neck fracture is high if the fracture extends through the anterior process.
(D) Fractures of the **sustentaculum tali** are usually due to avulsion by the deltoid ligament.
(E) The blood supply to the talar dome enters at the anterior process.
??
**Ans: B**
答案 B。距骨頸骨折若延伸穿過距骨頂（talar dome）關節面，會造成關節面不平整，日後創傷後關節炎發生率高，B 為正確敘述。其餘為錯：A pilon 骨折多為軸向負荷機轉（如高處墜落），而非外旋損傷；C 距骨缺血性壞死（AVN）風險與距下關節或踝關節的半脫位／脫位相關，與是否侵犯 anterior process 無關；D sustentaculum tali 骨折多源於軸向負荷造成的垂直剪力，而非三角韌帶（deltoid ligament）撕脫；E 距骨頂的血供來自脛前動脈分支，於距骨頸（talar neck）進入，而非 anterior process。→ [[talus-anatomy]]
