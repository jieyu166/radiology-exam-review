#交換 #2021交換 #CH Volume CT lung-cancer screening of high-risk former and current smokers, with the introduction of growth-rate assessment as an imaging biomarker for indeterminate tests, resulted in low referral rates for additional assessments and substantially lower lung-cancer mortality. Which is the trial name?
(A) JECS
(B) NELSON
(C) TALENT
(D) NLST
??
**Ans: B**
答案 B。題幹描述的是以「volume CT＋結節生長速率（growth-rate／體積倍增時間）作為不確定結果之影像生物標記」，在高風險現行及曾吸菸者中達到低轉介率、且雙性別皆顯著降低肺癌死亡率——這正是荷蘭–比利時 NELSON 試驗的特徵，故正解為 (B)。(D) NLST 是美國試驗，採直徑判讀、為首個證實 LDCT 降低肺癌死亡率 20% 者，但並非以體積生長速率作為生物標記；(A) JECS、(C) TALENT 與此描述不符。
