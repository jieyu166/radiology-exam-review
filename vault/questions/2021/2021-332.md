#交換 #2021交換 #CV A solitary coronary calcification spot was found over LAD on LDCT of lung cancer screen of a 50 y/o woman
(A) It is quite common
(B) Should control the risk factors of CAD, if any
(C) The CAC burden is low for the age and sex
(D) All are correct
??
**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】
Agatston score= highest attenuation value (HU) multiplied by the area of the calcification speck
Density factor
130-199 HU: 1
200-299 HU: 2
300-399 HU: 3
400+ HU: 4
if a calcified speck has a maximum attenuation value of 400 HU and occupies 8 sq mm area, then its calcium score will be 32.
Grading of coronary artery disease (based on total calcium score)
calcium score
no evidence of CAD: 0
minimal: 1-10
mild: 11-100
moderate: 101-400
severe: >400
B: information is not enough in this question
