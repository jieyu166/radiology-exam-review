#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which of the following statements concerning MR enterography in Crohn’s disease is **true**:
(A) Presence of asymmetric mural hyperenhancement is a specific finding
(B) Homogeneous symmetric mural hyperenhancement is a specific finding
(C) Diagnosis of intramural edema can be made when high signal intensity on non–fat-suppressed T2-weighted image are found
(D) Wall thickness should be evaluated and measured in any bowel segment
(E) Stricture is defined as a bowel segment with luminal narrowing and unequivocal dilation (ie, ≥5 cm) of the upstream bowel segment
??
**Ans: A**
答案 A。沿腸繫膜緣較嚴重的「不對稱」壁強化（asymmetric mural hyperenhancement）為 Crohn 的特異徵象，故 A 正確。B 錯，對稱、均質的壁強化並不特異，可見於多種情況；C 錯，腸壁水腫應於「脂肪抑制」T2 加權像或低／高 b 值 DWI 判讀，而非非脂肪抑制 T2；D 錯，壁厚須在以水或腸道對比劑「充分擴張」的腸段量測，塌陷腸段易高估；E 錯，狹窄定義為管腔狹窄＋上游無疑義擴張，門檻常定為 >3 cm 而非 ≥5 cm。→ [[inflammatory-bowel-disease]]
