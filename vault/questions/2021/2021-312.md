#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which statement about the ectopic pregnancy is incorrect?
(A) Risk factors including: history of PID, prior tubal/pelvic surgery, previous ectopic pregnancy
(B) An ectopic pregnancy most often occurs in an ovary
(C) The CT findings include an adnexal cystic mass with some degree of peripheral enhancement at contrast-enhanced CT
(D) Low or slow-rising β-hCG levels
??
**Ans: B**
【2021 官方詳解】
(A) The risk factors for ectopic pregnancy include the following:
- Previous ectopic pregnancy
- Prior fallopian tube surgery
- Previous pelvic or abdominal surgery
- Certain sexually transmitted infections (STIs)
- Pelvic inflammatory disease
- Endometriosis
Other factors that may increase a woman’s risk of ectopic pregnancy include:
- Cigarette smoking
- Age older than 35 years
- History of infertility
- Use of assisted reproductive technology, such as in vitro fertilization (IVF)
Ref: ACOG.org
(B) 95% of ectopic pregnancies are tubal, and most commonly occur in the ampulla of the fallopian tube.
