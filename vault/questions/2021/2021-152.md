【2021 官方詳解】Posterior urethral valves
= congenital thick folds of mucous membrane located in posterior uretra distal to verumontanum
Time of discovery: prenatal (8%), neonatal (34%), 1st year (32%), 2-16 year(23%), adult (3%)
Symptom: UTI (36%), obstructive symptom (32%), palpable kidney/bladder in neonate(21%), failure to
thrive (13%), hematuria (5%)

US: Keyhole sign, Bilateral hydronephroureter, oligohydramnios
