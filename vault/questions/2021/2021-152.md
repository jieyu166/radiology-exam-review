#交換 #2021交換 #ABD (???No answer) Which of the description is not related to type I urethral valves?
(A) It is an urogenital membrane
(B) It may result in the vesicoureteral reflux
(C) It may cause urinary tract infection
(D) It is discovered most commonly in the neonatal period
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。Type I 後尿道瓣（PUV）嚴重者多於「產前」經超音波（羊水過少＋雙側腎積水）診斷，較輕者於嬰兒／兒童期表現，並非「最常於新生兒期」被發現，故 D 為與 type I 較不相關／不正確之敘述。A（本質為 urogenital／後尿道膜性結構）正確；B（約 50% 合併膀胱輸尿管逆流 VUR）正確；C（膀胱小樑、尿滯留易致泌尿道感染）正確。→ [[urethral-anomalies-congenital]]
