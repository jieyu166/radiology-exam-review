#交換 #2021交換 #NR When considering a pineal region mass, which characteristic favors Non-germ cell neoplasms?
(A) radiosensitive; significantly decreased in size after CT scans
(B) male
(C) calcification with exploded appearance
(D) another tumor in suprasellar region
??
**Ans: C**
Exploded calcification會偏向pineoblastoma之類的，非germinoma.
