#交換 #2021交換 #PED About Imaging Findings of Fetal Liver Tumor, which of the following is not appropriate?
(A) Focal nodular hyperplasia : Hyper-echoic lesion without vascularity at Doppler US
(B) Hemangioma : Hypoechoic cystic lesion with or without punctate calcifications; vascularity at Doppler US
(C) Hemangioendothelioma : Dilated intrahepatic vessels, elevated maternal serum α-fetoprotein
(D) Mesenchymal hamartoma : Usually a large multiloculated cystic mass
??
**Ans: A**
答案 A。本題問胎兒肝腫瘤影像描述何者「不恰當」。A 錯在回音特性：胎兒 focal nodular hyperplasia 應為 hypoechoic（低回音）病灶、都卜勒無明顯血流，而非題目所寫的 hyper-echoic。B 正確：hemangioma 為低回音囊性病灶、可含點狀鈣化、都卜勒可見血流。C 正確：hemangioendothelioma 表現為擴張的肝內血管，母體血清 α-fetoprotein 可升高。D 正確：mesenchymal hamartoma 典型為大型多房囊性腫塊。
