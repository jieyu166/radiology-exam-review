#交換 #2021交換 #2023交換 #Breast Regarding the type of errors in **false** negative breast MRI, which of the following statements is **incorrect**?
(A) Perceptual errors are the most common type of diagnostic radiology, reported for 60-80% of radiologists’ errors.
(B) Perceptual error occurs when an abnormality is identified only retrospectively and was not detected prospectively.
(C) Cognitive errors occur when an abnormality is identified prospectively but its significance is incorrectly interpreted.
(D) Common causes of cognitive errors include subtle findings, satisfaction of search, workplace distraction, radiologist fatigue, poor lesion conspicuity.
(E) Lack of medical knowledge, radiologist bias, incorrect clinical information may cause the cognitive errors.
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。本題問 false-negative breast MRI 錯誤類型何者「錯誤」。（A）對：知覺性錯誤（perceptual error）是放射診斷最常見的錯誤，約占放射科醫師錯誤的 60%–80%。（B）對：知覺性錯誤指異常僅在回溯（retrospective）時才被看見、當下（prospective）未偵測到，即「漏看」。（C）對：認知性錯誤（cognitive error）指異常當下已被看見，但對其臨床意義判讀錯誤。（D）錯：subtle findings、satisfaction of search、工作環境干擾、醫師疲勞、病灶顯著度不佳等，屬於造成「知覺性錯誤」的原因，而非認知性錯誤，本選項張冠李戴故為不正確者。（E）對：醫學知識不足、醫師偏誤、錯誤的臨床資訊等才是認知性錯誤的常見成因。→ [[radiology-diagnostic-error-types]]
