#交換 #2021交換 #ABD What sign associated with retrorectal schwannoma and neurofibroma appears as multiple low-signal-intensity (SI) strands with peripheral high SI on T2-weighted images?
(A) Bridging vessel sign
(B) Claw sign
(C) Fascicular sign
(D) Fat split sign
??
**Ans: C**
【2021 官方詳解】The fat split, target, and fascicular signs have been described in association with schwannomas.
The fat split sign is a thin rim of fat around the lesion, seen as high T1 SI.
The target sign consists of a peripheral high-SI myxoid component and a central low-SI fibrous component
on T2-weighted images.
The fascicular sign corresponds to multiple low-SI strands with peripheral high SI on T2-weighted images,
representing the fascicular bundles within the nerves.
