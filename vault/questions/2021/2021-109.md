#交換 #2021交換 #PED Which statement is **wrong** about the desmoplastic small round cell tumor?
(A) Predominantly affects the male adolescent
(B) The male to female ratio is 6:1
(C) The tumor typically develops in peritoneal cavity without an apparent primary visceral site of origin
(D) It has a very good prognosis
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
D. Desmoplastic small round cell tumor (DSRCT) is a rare and highly aggressive mesenchymal tumor, mostly affecting young males and carrying a poor prognosis.
  - DSRCT predominantly affects male patients between the ages of 15 and 25 years. The male to female ratio is 6:1
  - The tumor typically develops in the peritoneal cavity without an apparent primary visceral site of origin, and usually occupies the retrovesical or retrouterine space.
  - The liver is the most common site of distant metastasis
