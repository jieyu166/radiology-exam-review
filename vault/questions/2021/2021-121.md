#交換 #2021交換 #ABD Which of the following statement about metastatic ovarian tumor is **wrong**?
(A) About 50% of ovarian malignancy are metastatic lesions
(B) Imaging findings in metastatic lesions are nonspecific, consisting of predominantly solid components or a mixture of cystic and solid areas
(C) The colon and stomach are the most common primary tumor sites in ovarian metastasis, followed by the breast, lung, and contralateral ovary
(D) Krukenberg tumors are metastatic tumors to the ovary that contain mucin-secreting “signet ring” cells and usually originate in the gastrointestinal tract
(E) None of the above
??
**Ans: A**
【2021 官方詳解】
A. Only 10% of all ovarian tumors are due to metastatic tumor.
The colon and stomach are the most common primary tumor sites in ovarian metastasis, followed by the breast, lung, and contralateral ovary. The tumors represent 10% of all ovarian tumors and develop during the reproductive years (52). Krukenberg tumors are metastatic tumors to the ovary that contain mucin-secreting “signet ring” cells and usually originate in the gastrointestinal tract
