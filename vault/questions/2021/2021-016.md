#交換 #2021交換 #Breast Which of the following statement about malignant phyllodes tumor is **incorrect**?
(A) Phyllodes tumors are a rare fibroepithelial neoplasm of the breast.
(B) Definitive diagnosis requires surgical excision.
(C) Breast ultrasound (US) demonstrated a large heterogeneous hypoechoic mass.
(D) Metastatic spread is primarily lymphatic spread with nodal involvement
??
**Ans: D**
hematogenous metastases。因很少lymphatic metastasis，所以不需清axillary LN。
【2021 官方詳解】
Malignant phyllodes tumor:
A tumor diameter of 3 cm or greater => higher likelihood of malignancy
Fewer than 20% of the malignant tumors metastasize. When metastatic disease does occur, the metastases usually spread hematogenously to the lungs, pleura, or bone.
lymphatic metastasis is rare, axillary nodal surgery is not indicated.
