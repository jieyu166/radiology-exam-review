#交換 #2021交換 #ABD LIRADS v2018**不**適用在下列何種病人?
(A) Cirrhosis
(B) Chronic hepatitis C viral infection without cirrhosis
(C) Current or prior HCC.
(D) Recipient post adult liver transplantation with chronic hepatitis B viral infection
??
**Ans: B**
台大HCV也可以，其他醫院/原版不行
【2021 官方詳解】
- Apply in patients at high risk for HCC, namely those with:
  - Cirrhosis OR
  - Chronic hepatitis B viral infection OR
  - Current or prior HCC
  - Including adult liver transplant candidates and recipients posttransplant
- Do not apply in patients:
  - Without the above risk factors
  - < 18 years old
  - With cirrhosis due to congenital hepatic fibrosis
  - With cirrhosis due to a vascular disorder such as hereditary hemorrhagic telangiectasia, Budd-Chiari syndrome, chronic portal vein occlusion, cardiac congestion, or diffuse nodular regenerative hyperplasia
