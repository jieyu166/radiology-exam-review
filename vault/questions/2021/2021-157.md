#交換 #2021交換 #PED Which description of features of Ebstein’s disease is incorrect?
(A) enlargement of right ventricle
(B) enlargement of right atrium
(C) enlargement of azygos vein
(D) cyanosis
??
**Ans: A**
【2021 官方詳解】
Ebstein anomaly:
Abnormal tricuspid valve displace apically into RV
- Atrialization of part of RV 🡪 enlarged RA and small RV 🡪 Boxlike cardiomegaly
- Congenital tricuspid regurgitation 🡪 IVC and azygos dilatation
- Cynosis in neonatal period
- CHF in utero/ in neonate (50%)
