#交換 #2021交換 #MSK Which one is correct about avascular necrosis of talus?
(A) Three extraosseous arterial contributors branch to supply the bone: the posterior tibial artery, the dorsalis pedis artery and the perforating peroneal artery
(B) The Hawkins sign describes subchondral lucency of the talar dome seen in AP view
(C) Talar AVN typically manifests as an increase in talar dome opacity (sclerosis), followed by deformity and, in severe cases, articular collapse and bone fragmentation
(D) All above are correct
??
**Ans: D**
【2021 官方詳解】
Osteonecrosis of the talus follows a characteristic radiographic sequence that generally begins with an increase in talar dome opacity (sclerosis), then advances to deformity and, in severe cases, to articular collapse and bone fragmentation
The Hawkins sign, which becomes evident 6–8 weeks after injury, is an area of disuse osteopenia caused by resorption of the subchondral bone and is visualized as a thin subchondral radiolucent line along all or part of the talar dome
