#交換 #2021交換 #Physics X-rays produced following a photoelectric interaction in tissue are best characterized as:
(A) Characteristic x-rays
(B) Compton scatter
(C) Coherent scatter
(D) Bremsstrahlung photons
??
**Ans: A**
考定義：光電交互作用產生的X光，就是特性輻射。
【2021 官方詳解】
Most common is Characteristic x-rays (photoelectric effect), then Compton scatter.
Least likely is coherent (Rayleigh) scatter.
