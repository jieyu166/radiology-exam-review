#交換 #2021交換 #ABD 有關The Liver Imaging Reporting And Data System (LI-RADS), 以下敘述何 者有**誤**?
(A) 使用在肝硬化, 慢性B肝, 或有肝癌病史的病人才用.
(B) LR-5 代表definite HCC, 不需要再切片確診.
(C) LR-5, 一定要有 non-rim arterial phase hyperenhancement才是
(D) 另外三個主要徵象, 包括enhancing “capsule”, nonperipheral “washout”, 以及threshold growth
(E) 所謂threshold growth, 是指size increase of a mass by ≥ 50% in ≤ 12 months
??
**Ans: E**
size increase ≥ 50% in ≤ **6** months
【2021 官方詳解】D.
threshold growth
diameter increase ≥50% increase in ≤6 months
other prior criteria are now considered subthreshold growth, an ancillary feature
if prior exam >6 months, diameter ≥100% increase
a new lesion ≥10 mm in <24 months
threshold growth should be compared on similar sequences between studies
only apply this criterion if the lesion is definitely a mass (e.g. not perfusion alteration)
