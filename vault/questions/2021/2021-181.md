#交換 #2021交換 #CH 下列何種影像發現為 COVID-19 的胸部X光 characteristic finding?
(A) Lobar consolidation
(B) Pleural effusion
(C) Bilateral consolidations in a peripheral and mid to lower lung zone distribution
(D) Pneumothorax
??
**Ans: C**
⚠️ 本題含影像（官方詳解 PDF 第 136 頁），影像尚未匯入，判讀請對照原檔。
【醫師裁決 2026-07-11】答案更正 A → C（原答案為 A）。COVID-19 CXR 之 characteristic finding 為雙側、周邊、中下肺帶之實變/毛玻璃（C）；lobar consolidation（A）非典型、屬 nonspecific。依官方詳解 Table 2（Chest Radiograph Characterization by Findings）：**Characteristic** ＝ Bilateral patchy and/or confluent, bandlike ground-glass opacity or consolidation in a peripheral and mid to lower lung zone distribution（即選項 C）；Nonspecific ＝ 上述以外之任何肺-胸膜異常（lobar consolidation 屬此類）；Negative ＝ 無可辨識之肺-胸膜異常。

**Table 2: Chest Radiograph Characterization by Findings**

| Chest Radiograph Characterization | Chest Radiograph Findings |
| --- | --- |
| Characteristic | Bilateral patchy and/or confluent, bandlike ground-glass opacity or consolidation in a peripheral and mid to lower lung zone distribution |
| Nonspecific | Any pleuropulmonary abnormality other than the above |
