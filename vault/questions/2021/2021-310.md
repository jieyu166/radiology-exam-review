#交換 #2021交換 #NR Which statement regarding subdural empyema is **true**?
(A) Chronic subdural hematoma may appear identical
(B) High diffusion weighted signal within subdural empyema is rare
(C) Less common than epidural abscess
(D) In adults, usually related to meningitis
??
**Ans: A**
【2021 官方詳解】
(B) epidural empyema (EDE) DWI variable signal
subdural empyema (SDE) DWI typically restricted diffusion
(C) subdural empyema much more common
(D) Infants & young children: complication of bacterial meningitis
older children &adult: related to paranasal sinus disease (>2/3)
