#交換 #2021交換 #CV Pericardial calcification is caused by all **except**?
(A) Radiotherapy to the mediastinum
(B) Methysergide therapy
(C) Anticoagulant therapy
(D) Benign pericarditis
(E) Dermatomyositis
??
**Ans: E**
困難
C會出血之後鈣化。B少見。E不會。
【2021 官方詳解】1. pericarditis: tuberculous, fungal, viral or pyogenic
2. previous trauma (hemopericardium) , cardiac surgery, hemopericardium due to anticoagulant
3. collagen vascular diseases: SLE, RA, scleroderma
4. uremic pericarditis
5. later sequelae of rheumatic heart disease
6. malignant pericardial involvement (e.g. mediastinal teratoma)
7. post-radiotherapy
8. idiopathic
9. calcified pericardial mass or cyst
Constrictive pericarditis due to methysergide therapy (case report)
