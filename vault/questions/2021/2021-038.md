#交換 #2021交換 #CV Pericardial calcification is caused by all **except**?
(A) Radiotherapy to the mediastinum
(B) Methysergide therapy
(C) Anticoagulant therapy
(D) Benign pericarditis
(E) Dermatomyositis
??
**Ans: E**
困難
C會出血之後鈣化。B少見。E不會。
【2021 官方詳解】
- pericarditis: tuberculous, fungal, viral or pyogenic
- previous trauma (hemopericardium) , cardiac surgery, hemopericardium due to anticoagulant
- collagen vascular diseases: SLE, RA, scleroderma
- uremic pericarditis
- later sequelae of rheumatic heart disease
- malignant pericardial involvement (e.g. mediastinal teratoma)
- post-radiotherapy
- idiopathic
- calcified pericardial mass or cyst
Constrictive pericarditis due to methysergide therapy (case report)
