#交換 #2021交換 #Breast Which one of the following descriptions about anatomy imaging issues of breast sonography is **not true** ?
(A) The thin, echogenic skin line is normally less than 2 mm in thickness
(B) Echogenicity is defined relative to fibroglandular tissue
(C) Anterior suspensory ligament is usually visible in subcutaneous zone
(D) Interlobular stroma and glandular elements are usually hyperechoic
??
**Ans: B**
Echo pattern跟fat做比對。Mammo是跟gland比對，要寫equal density
【2021 官方詳解】
B. Subcutaneous fat, the tissue relative to which echogenicity is compared, is medium gray and darker than fibroglandular tissue, which is light gray.
