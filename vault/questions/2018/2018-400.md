(A) Predominantly in the proximal 1/3 ureter.
(B) The lesion is a malignant condition has high potential for degeneration.
(C) Does not cause ureteral obstruction.
(D) Related to transformation of the epithelial nest of Brunn.
(E) Predominantly in adult female.
??
