#交換 #2022交換 #NR A patient's external auditory canal is atretic with a hypoplastic middle ear cavity and the ossicles are absent. What type of hearing loss does the patient have?
(A) Conductive
(B) Mixed
(C) Sensorineural
??
**Ans: A**
外耳道閉鎖＋中耳腔發育不良＋聽小骨缺如阻斷聲音的機械傳導路徑；先天性外中耳畸形（CEMEM）的內耳（耳蝸、CN VIII）因較早由 otocyst 形成通常正常，故為純傳導性聽力障礙。conductive hearing loss 為 CEMEM 最常見症狀。→ [[congenital-aural-atresia]]
