#交換 #2022交換 #ABD What is the most common gastric cancer?
(A) Gastrointestinal stromal tumor
(B) Adenocarcinoma
(C) Gastric lymphoma
(D) Carcinoid tumor
??
**Ans: B**
胃惡性腫瘤中腺癌（adenocarcinoma）約佔 80–90%，遠多於 GIST、gastric lymphoma（約 5%）與 carcinoid；GIST 雖為最常見之間葉腫瘤，整體佔比仍遠低於腺癌，故最常見的胃癌為腺癌。→ [[gastric-adenocarcinoma]]
