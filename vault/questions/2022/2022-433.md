#交換 #2022交換 #CH Which of the following pathologies presented with hemoptysis has benefited least from bronchial artery embolization (BAE)?
(A) idiopathic bronchiectasis
(B) pulmonary malignancy
(C) active tuberculosis
(D) post-TB sequelae
??
**Ans: B**
答案 B（pulmonary malignancy）。BAE 對良性、慢性發炎性咳血（特發性支氣管擴張、活動性肺結核、結核後遺症）效果最好，這些正是 BAE 的核心適應症與最常見病因；相對地肺部惡性腫瘤的新生血管脆弱、腫瘤持續進展，BAE 多屬姑息，再出血率高、長期獲益最差。→ [[bronchial-artery-embolization-hemoptysis]]
