#交換 #2022交換 #ABD Which one is CORRECT concerning the landmarks of the important ligaments?
(A) Gastrohepatic ligament: left gastric vessels, right gastric vessels
(B) Hepatoduodenal ligament: short gastric vessels
(C) Falciform ligament: portal triad
(D) Greater omentum: meddle colic vessels
??
**Ans: A**
A 正確：肝胃韌帶（小網膜一部分）內含左、右胃血管（left/right gastric artery 與 left gastric/coronary vein、right gastric vein）。B 錯：hepatoduodenal ligament 含 portal triad，short gastric vessels 走 gastrosplenic ligament。C 錯：falciform ligament 含 ligamentum teres／paraumbilical vein，portal triad 在 hepatoduodenal ligament。D 錯：middle colic vessels 走 transverse mesocolon，非 greater omentum。→ [[peritoneal-ligaments-and-spaces]]。
