#交換 #2022交換 #ABD Which of the following statement(s) is(are) **true** concerning MR imaging of endometriosis?
(A) The presence of T1-weighted hyperintensity within a dilated fallopian tube is not suggestive of endometriosis.
(B) Decidualized endometriosis has been described as a mimic of ovarian cancer at ultrasonography and MR imaging.
(C) Most endometriomas are easily recognized on the basis of their T2 hyperintensity.
(D) All of the above
??
**Ans: B**
B 正確：孕期蛻膜化（decidualized）子宮內膜異位可出現強化壁結節/血流，於超音波與 MRI 上模擬卵巢癌，為已知陷阱。A 錯：擴張輸卵管內出現 T1 高訊號（血性積液/hematosalpinx）反而「支持」子宮內膜異位，並非不支持。C 錯：endometrioma 的 MRI 指紋是 T1 高訊號＋T2 低訊號（shading），多數靠 T2 變暗（而非 T2 高訊號）辨識，此敘述與典型表現相反。故唯一正確為 B。→ [[endometrioma]]
