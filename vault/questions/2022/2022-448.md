#交換 #2022交換 #PED Which is incorrect about juvenile nasopharyngeal angiofibroma?
(A) A highly vascular benign neoplasm that exhibits local aggressive and invasive behavior
(B) Occurs in adolescent boys
(C) Angiography depicts a tumor blush, which is usually arising from branches of the external carotid artery
(D) Biopsy is needed for diagnosis
??
**Ans: D**
JNA 為富血管、良性但局部侵犯性腫瘤（A 正確），好發青春期男性（B 正確），血管攝影 tumor blush 多源自外頸動脈（ECA）分支尤其 internal maxillary artery（C 正確）。選項 D「需切片診斷」為錯誤：JNA 極度富血管，切片可致命性大出血（biopsy can be fatal），故不做切片，診斷依臨床＋特徵性影像（強烈強化、PPF 增寬、Holman-Miller、T2 flow voids）＋血管攝影。→ [[juvenile-nasopharyngeal-angiofibroma]]
