#交換 #2022交換 #MSK What three muscles are attached by tendons to the coracoid process?
(A) Pectoralis major, coracobrachialis, short head of biceps brachii.
(B) Pectoralis minor, short head of biceps brachii, coracobrachialis.
(C) Coracobrachialis, anterior deltoid, long head of biceps brachii.
(D) Long head of biceps brachii, pectoralis minor, coracobrachialis.
??
**Ans: B**
喙突（coracoid process）尖端為 coracobrachialis 與 biceps brachii 短頭之共同起點，喙突內側緣上表面為 pectoralis minor 附著處，故三條經肌腱附著於喙突的肌肉為 pectoralis minor、coracobrachialis、short head of biceps brachii（選項 B）。選項 A、D 誤植 pectoralis major 或 long head of biceps（長頭起自 supraglenoid tubercle 而非喙突），選項 C 的 anterior deltoid 與 long head of biceps 亦非喙突附著肌肉。原始答案欄留空，經查證後補為 B。詳見 [[coracoid-process]]
