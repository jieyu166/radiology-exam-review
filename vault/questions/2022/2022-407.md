#交換 #2022交換 #Breast A premenopausal woman requires a breast MRI with contrast. Which week of the menstrual cycle is the best choice to perform the MRI?
(A) Days 1 to 6
(B) Days 7 to 14
(C) Days 15 to 21
(D) Days 22 to 28
??
**Ans: B**
B（Days 7 to 14）正確。停經前婦女對比乳房 MRI 最佳時機為週期第 2 週（days 7-14, 濾泡／增生期），此時荷爾蒙誘發之背景實質強化（BPE）最低，可減少背景強化掩蓋病灶與偽陽性；黃體期（第 3–4 週）BPE 最強應避開。→ [[breast-mri-bpe]]
