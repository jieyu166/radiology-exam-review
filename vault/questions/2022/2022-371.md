#交換 #2022交換 #MSK Which description about calcified tendinitis is **WRONG**?
(A) The etiology is unknown but thought to result from microtrauma and decreased oxygen tension, leading to secondary mineralization.
(B) Calcific tendinitis can affect any tendon in the body but is typically seen in the rotator cuff, especially the supraspinatus tendon
(C) The calcifications typically appear as well-defined oval or lobular foci in the rotator cuff and are of low signal intensity on all pulse sequences of MRI
(D) The use of gradient echo sequences of MRI is not helpful in detecting tiny calcification
??
**Ans: D**
本題問「哪一項描述錯誤」。D 為錯：梯度回訊（GRE）序列因磁化率效應在低訊號鈣化周圍產生 blooming artifact，反而有助偵測微小鈣化（STATdx 明載 slight blooming artifact around low-signal calcific deposits），故「GRE 對偵測微小鈣化無幫助」不正確。A（病因不明，與微創傷及氧張力降低致續發礦化有關）、B（可侵犯任何肌腱、典型旋轉肌袖尤棘上肌約 80%）、C（邊界清楚卵圓/分葉狀、各序列皆低訊號）皆為正確描述。→ [[calcific-tendinitis]]
