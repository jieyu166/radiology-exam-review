#交換 #2022交換 #Physics About adverse reactions of Gadolinium-based contrast agents (GBCA), which of the following are not correct?
(A) The stability of the complexation of gadolinium with linear GBCAs is significantly lower than that with macrocyclic GBCAs.
(B) GBCA of low stability are likely to undergo transmetallation and release free Gd ions that may deposit in tissues and attract circulating fibrocytes to initiate the process of fibrosis.
(C) Nonionic linear GBCAs showed the much higher rate of immediate allergic adverse events compared with ionic linear and nonionic macrocyclic GBCAs.
(D) The ionic, protein-binding and macrocyclic GBCAs were linked to a higher rate of undesirable effects than linear GBCAs without protein binding.
??
**Ans: C**
⚠️（原題答案空白，依概念推定）此題問「何者不正確」。C 錯誤：文獻上 immediate 過敏樣反應率較高者為 ionic linear 與 nonionic macrocyclic，nonionic linear（如 Omniscan）反而過敏率最低，故「nonionic linear 過敏率遠高」敘述相反。A 正確：linear 螯合穩定度顯著低於 macrocyclic（環狀鎖得緊）。B 正確：低穩定度製劑易 transmetallation 釋出游離 Gd 沉積並誘發纖維化（NSF 機轉）。D 描述 macrocyclic／ionic／protein-binding 相對過敏風險略高，方向與概念「macrocyclic 過敏反應風險相對略高」一致，屬相對可接受敘述，故最不正確者取 C。詳見 → [[gadolinium-contrast]]
