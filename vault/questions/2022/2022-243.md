#交換 #2022交換 #Physics About gadolinium retention in the human tissue, which of the following are not correct?
(A) GBCAs pass through the blood-brain barrier through periarterial and pial-glial pathways (glymphatic system).
(B) Significant less gadolinium retention was observed after macrocyclic chelate exposure, compared to linear chelate exposure.
(C) With regard to elimination, complexed gadolinium in intact GBCA appears to leave the brain more easily in order to be excreted afterwards than what is released from the chelator.
(D) Absolute concentrations of gadolinium were significantly higher in brain than in the skin and bone.
??
**Ans: D**
此題問「何者不正確」，答案 D。D 錯誤：皮膚與骨骼（尤其骨是主要儲庫）之絕對釓濃度遠高於腦部，並非腦部最高。A 正確：釓可穿過完整 BBB，推測經 periarterial／pial-glial 之 glymphatic 途徑進入間質（概念載約 18–42% 進入間質）。B 正確：macrocyclic 較 linear 去螯合少，滯留量顯著較低。C 正確：完整螯合的釓較易離腦排除，游離出螯合基的釓則較易滯留。故不正確者為 D。詳見 → [[gadolinium-contrast]]
