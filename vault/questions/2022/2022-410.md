#交換 #2022交換 #ABD Pancreas divisum, a variation in pancreatic ductal anatomy, in the majority of cases, by the __ directly entering the minor papilla with no communication with the ventral duct and thus, the major papilla.
(A) Duct of Wirsung
(B) Duct of Santorini
(C) Accessory pancreatic duct
(D) Replaced pancreatic duct
??
**Ans: B**
Pancreas divisum 為最常見的胰管變異，胚胎期腹側與背側胰管未融合。多數病例中，背側胰管（即主胰管與 duct of Santorini）直接進入副乳頭（minor papilla），與腹側胰管（duct of Wirsung，連主乳頭/major papilla）無交通。故直接進入副乳頭者為 Duct of Santorini。→ [[pancreas-divisum]]
