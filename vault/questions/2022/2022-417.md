#交換 #2022交換 #US Which of the following statements about the ultrasound appearance of gallbladder disease is not **true**?
(A) The wall-echo-shadow complex is a sign of a partially stone-filled gallbladder.
(B) Common findings of acute cholecystitis include gallstones, wall thickening, pericholecystic fluid and sonographic Murphy's sign.
(C) A characteristic finding of gallbladder adenomyomatosis is comet-tail artifact.
(D) Cholesterolosis may produce cholesterol polyps, appearing as a nonmobile, nonshadowing ball on the wall.
??
**Ans: A**
答案 A。⚠️ 原輸入四項敘述全擠在單一選項 A，已依來源重建為 A–D 四選項。「Wall-echo-shadow（WES）複合徵是膽囊『部分』充滿結石的表現」為錯誤——WES 實為膽囊『幾乎完全填滿/packed』結石時的表現（前壁弧狀高回音 wall＋結石表面 echo＋濃密後方 shadow，膽囊腔消失），非部分充滿。其餘皆正確：急性膽囊炎常見結石＋膽囊壁增厚＋周圍積液＋超音波墨菲氏徵；腺肌症特徵為 comet-tail artifact；膽固醇沉積症可形成不動、無聲影的膽固醇息肉。→ [[cholelithiasis-ultrasound]]
