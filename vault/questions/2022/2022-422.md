#交換 #2022交換 #ABD A 62-year-old man with diabetes and hypertension has scrotal swellings. Which of the following would be included in the differential diagnosis A: Inguinal hernia B: Liposacroma C: Spermatic cord lipoma D: Undescended testis
(A) A only
(B) A and C
(C) A,B,C
(D) All of above
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C（A,B,C）。62 歲成人之陰囊/腹股溝腫脹（尤其含脂肪腫塊）鑑別包含 A 腹股溝疝氣、B 脂肪肉瘤（成人最常見惡性間葉腫瘤之一）、C 精索脂肪瘤（睪丸外最常見良性腫瘤）。D 未下降睪丸（隱睪）不列入——隱睪多於嬰幼兒/兒童診斷、睪丸不位於陰囊，非 62 歲以陰囊腫脹表現者之鑑別。故 A,B,C。→ [[extratesticular-scrotal-mass-ddx]]
