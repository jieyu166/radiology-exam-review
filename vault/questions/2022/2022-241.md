#交換 #2022交換 #ABD What is the “possible” 2020 revised comprehensive diagnostic (RCD) criteria for IgG4-RD?
(A) One or more organs show diffuse or localized swelling or a mass or nodule characteristic of IgG4-RD.
(B) Serum IgG4 levels greater than 135 mg/dl
(C) Positivity for two of the following three criteria:
(D) Dense lymphocyte and plasma cell infiltration with fibrosis.
(E) Ratio of IgG4-positive plasma cells /IgG-positive cells greater than 40% and the number of IgG4-positive plasma cells greater than 10 per high powered field.
??
**Ans: B**
⚠️ 本題考 2020 修訂版綜合診斷（RCD）準則之三大分項——①臨床/影像（一或多器官特徵性瀰漫或局部腫大/腫塊）②血清學（serum IgG4 >135 mg/dl）③組織病理（緻密淋巴漿細胞浸潤＋纖維化，且 IgG4+/IgG+ >40% 且 IgG4+ >10/HPF）；「possible」層級對應僅滿足臨床/影像＋血清學而缺組織學者，故此題以血清 IgG4 >135 mg/dl（B）為對應之血清學條件答案。本概念僅涵蓋 IgG4-RD 的病名譜系與多器官影像（Mikulicz、type 1 AIP、膽管炎、後腹腔纖維化等），未逐條收錄 RCD「definite/probable/possible」分級細節，答案依原卷保留並標記待醫師對照原始 RCD 文獻。→ [[igg4-related-disease]]
