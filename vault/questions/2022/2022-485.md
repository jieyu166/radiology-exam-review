#交換 #2022交換 #MSK About osteochondral injury of ankle, which is **wrong**?
(A) Posteromedial or anterolateral talar dome can both due to inversion ankle injuries
(B) Medial talus impacts the medial tibial plafond caused by inversion ankle injuries
(C) Lateral collateral ligament injuries are associated with inversion injury
(D) 3-tesla magnets are preferred for a more accurate evaluation of the thin cartilage layer
(E) Non
??
**Ans: E**
此題問「哪個錯」，A~D 皆正確故答 E（以上皆無錯）：(A) 後內側與前外側距骨圓頂病灶皆由內翻損傷造成；(B) 內側距骨於內翻時撞擊內側脛骨關節面（plafond）形成深碗狀病灶；(C) 內翻機轉幾乎必伴外側副韌帶（含 ATFL）損傷；(D) 距骨軟骨薄，偏好 3 T 高場強以更準確評估。⚠️ 原題選項 E 文字受損為「Non」，依 source 應為「None（以上皆無錯）」。→ [[osteochondral-lesion-talus]]
