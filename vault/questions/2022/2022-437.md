#交換 #2022交換 #ABD For a recipient with hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), which of the following conditions is considered an absolute contraindication for liver transplantation?
(A) an HCC with size less than 5 cm
(B) enlarged regional lymph nodes
(C) extrahepatic metastases
(D) acute portovenous thrombosis
??
**Ans: C**
肝外轉移(extrahepatic metastases)是 HCC 患者肝移植的絕對禁忌——Milan criteria 明訂須「no extra-hepatic metastases」與「no major vessel involvement」，一旦肝外播散即無法藉移植治癒。其餘選項均非絕對禁忌：HCC <5 cm 本身符合 Milan、區域淋巴結腫大多為反應性(須組織學證實轉移方為禁忌)、急性(bland)門靜脈血栓為良性血栓而非腫瘤性瘤栓。→ [[hcc-imaging-diagnosis]]
