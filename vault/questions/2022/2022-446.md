#交換 #2022交換 #NR Regarding lesions that may occur in the CPA region, perfusion-weighted MRI may potentially differentiate between each of the following EXCEPT:
(A) Vestibular schwannoma and meningioma.
(B) Lymphoma and metastasis.
(C) Abscess and metastasis.
(D) Hemangioblastoma and lymphoma.
(E) Meningioma and metastasis.
??
**Ans: E**
perfusion-weighted MRI（PWI）藉 rCBV 差異鑑別 CPA 病灶：schwannoma（低-中）vs meningioma（高）（A）、lymphoma（低）vs metastasis（高）（B）、abscess（環壁灌流、核心低）vs metastasis（C）、hemangioblastoma（極高）vs lymphoma（低）（D）皆可區分。選項 E 的 meningioma 與 metastasis 皆為高灌流（高 rCBV），PWI 無法可靠區分，故為 EXCEPT 答案。→ [[cpa-masses]]
