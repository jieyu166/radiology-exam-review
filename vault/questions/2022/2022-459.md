#交換 #2022交換 #MSK Regarding MRI features of sarcoidosis involving the musculoskeletal system, all of the following are **true** EXCEPT?
(A) . MRI may reveal marrow and soft-tissue lesions that are radiographically occult.
(B) . Axial bone lesions may be distinguished from metastases using standard MRI techniques.
(C) . Soft-tissue features include muscle edema and nodular muscle lesions that enhance after gadolinium administration.
(D) . Joint involvement includes synovitis, tenosynovitis, effusions, and subarticular bone marrow edema.
??
**Ans: B**
「除外」的錯誤敘述為 B：中軸骨類肉瘤病灶與骨轉移在標準 MRI 訊號／強化完全重疊（T1 低、fluid-sensitive 高、會強化），STATdx 明載無可靠區分依據、常需切片。其餘皆真——MRI 可顯示 X 光隱匿之骨髓／軟組織病灶(A)、軟組織可見肌肉水腫與結節狀強化病灶(C)、關節可見 synovitis／tenosynovitis／積液與軟骨下骨髓水腫(D)。→ [[sarcoidosis-msk]]
