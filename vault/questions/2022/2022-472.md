#交換 #2022交換 #CH Patient with anomalous right coronary artery arising from the left coronary cusp is at risk for which of the following?
(A) Arrhythmia
(B) Myocardial infarction
(C) Sudden cardiac death
(D) All of the above (the correct answer)
(E) None of the above; this is a benign finding
??
**Ans: D**
右冠狀動脈起源於左冠狀竇（R-ACAOS）時，若近端走 interarterial course（於主動脈與肺動脈幹之間）且常合併 intramural segment，運動時受壓可致心肌缺血，進而引發心律不整、心肌梗塞與猝死——三者皆為其風險，故答案為 D（以上皆是）。此屬『惡性』冠狀動脈異常，非良性偶然發現，故 E 錯誤。走行路徑分類與高風險解剖特徵 → [[acaos]]。
