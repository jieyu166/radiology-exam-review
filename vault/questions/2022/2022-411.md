#交換 #2022交換 #ABD Renal vascular assessment is crucial in renal transplant evaluation. Which arterial condition is NOT the contraindication to donor nephrectomy?
(A) Fibromuscular disease involving renal artery.
(B) Triplicated renal arteries.
(C) Duplicated renal arteries with luminal diameter greater than 3 mm.
(D) Atherosclerosis with severe stenosis of renal artery.
??
**Ans: C**
活體腎捐贈術前 CTA 目的在找出傷害捐贈者或不利移植的血管病變。管徑足夠（>3 mm）的重複腎動脈可外科吻合重建，屬可接受、非絕對禁忌。相對地：腎動脈 FMD 提示捐贈者潛在血管病、進展與動脈瘤風險；嚴重動脈粥狀硬化狹窄使供腎灌流受損；三條（triplicated）腎動脈增加重建困難——皆屬不利／禁忌。故「不」是禁忌者為 C。→ [[renal-donor-vascular-evaluation]]
