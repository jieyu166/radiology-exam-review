#交換 #2022交換 #PED What is the main differentiating feature between type 1 and type 2 diastematomyelia?
(A) Presence or absence of scoliotic spine curvature.
(B) Presence or absence of tethered-cord syndrome.
(C) Individual versus single dural tube.
(D) Presence or absence of cutaneous findings.
??
**Ans: C**
Split cord malformation 依「硬膜囊分隔」分型：Type I＝兩條 hemicord 各在獨立硬膜囊內、被中線堅硬骨性/軟骨性 spur 隔開；Type II＝兩條 hemicord 共用單一硬膜囊、僅有柔軟纖維中隔。故主要鑑別＝individual vs single dural tube（C）。脊柱側彎、tethered cord、皮膚徵象兩型皆可出現，非分型依據。→ [[diastematomyelia]]。
