#交換 #2022交換 #CV Which of the following statements is incorrect regarding finding of bird beak configuration after TEVAR?
(A) Bird-beak configuration is defined as the lack of apposition of the proximal stent-graft to the aortic wall along the greater curvature.
(B) Bird-beak configuration is correlated with higher risk of type Ia endoleak.
(C) Bird-beak configuration is associated with higher percentage of type II endoleak from left subclavian artery.
(D) Bird-beak configuration is a potential risk factor for proximal stent graft infolding.
??
**Ans: A**
A 為不正確敘述。bird-beak 定義為近端支架與主動脈弓「內側/小彎（inner/lesser curvature）」貼合不全、形成楔形間隙，並非沿「greater curvature（大彎/外彎）」——方向寫反。依 Ueda 2010（Radiology），bird-beak 出現在弓的內彎（underside），因支架難以服貼弓內彎銳角而翹起。B（與 type Ia endoleak 風險升高相關）、C（type II endoleak 較多來自左鎖骨下動脈）、D（為近端支架內折的潛在危險因子）皆正確。→ [[tevar-bird-beak-configuration]]
