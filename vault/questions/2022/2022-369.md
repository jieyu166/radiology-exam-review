#交換 #2022交換 #ABD Which one is not **true** about gadoxetic acid-enhanced MRI?
(A) Organic anion–transporting polypeptide 1B3 (OATP1B3) expressed at the sinusoidal membrane in human hepatocytes is considered to be a major uptake transporter of gadoxetic acid.
(B) Multidrug resistance–associated protein (MRP) 1 and MRP3 expressed on the sinusoidal side and MRP2 expressed on the canalicular side are export transporters of gadoxetic acid.
(C) Liver mass without functioning hepatocytes commonly exhibit low or no OATP1B3 expression.
(D) HCC with hyperintensity during the hepatobiliary phase shows β-catenin and hepatocyte nuclear factor 4α activation.
(E) None of the above.
??
**Ans: E**
本題問「哪一項不正確」。A：OATP1B3 位於肝細胞 sinusoidal 膜、為 Gd-EOB-DTPA 主要攝取轉運子（正確）。B：MRP1/MRP3（sinusoidal 側）與 MRP2（canalicular 側）皆為 export 轉運子（正確）。C：無功能性肝細胞之病灶 OATP1B3 表現低/無，故肝膽期不攝取（正確）。D：肝膽期呈高訊號之 HCC 常見 β-catenin 與 HNF4α 活化（Kitao，正確）。A–D 皆為真，故不正確的選項是 E「None of the above」。→ [[hepatobiliary-mri-contrast]]
