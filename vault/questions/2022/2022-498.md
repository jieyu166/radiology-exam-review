#交換 #2022交換 #PED Neonatal small left colon syndrome (meconium plug syndrome) has the strongest association with which of the following?
(A) Down syndrome
(B) Cystic fibrosis
(C) Maternal diabetes mellitus
(D) Maternal alcohol use
??
**Ans: C**
新生兒小左結腸症（＝胎便栓塞症候群/結腸功能不成熟）最強關聯為母親糖尿病，占 MPS 約 40–50%（另有產前硫酸鎂、早產）。Cystic fibrosis 是 meconium ileus 的典型關聯，本病與 CF 的連結爭議且不確定；Down syndrome 較常關聯 Hirschsprung/十二指腸閉鎖。→ [[neonatal-small-left-colon-syndrome]]
