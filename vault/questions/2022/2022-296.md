#交換 #2022交換 #PED In terms of congenital CMV (Cytomegalovirus) infection, which one is **wrong**?
(A) Periventricular calcification is one of the typical imaging findings.
(B) Migrational abnormalities are associated with toxoplasmosis instead of congenital CMV infection.
(C) Microcephaly usually implies poorer outcome.
(D) White matter diseases may be present.
??
**Ans: B**
問哪一敘述『錯誤』。答案 B。神經元移行異常(migrational abnormalities：lissencephaly、pachygyria、polymicrogyria、schizencephaly)是先天 CMV 感染的特徵，可見於多達約 10% 個案，感染時機愈早皮質發育破壞愈重；toxoplasmosis 以實質及腦室周圍鈣化為主，並非以移行異常為特徵，故 B 敘述顛倒、為錯誤選項。A 腦室周圍/室管膜下鈣化(~50%)、C 小頭畸形代表較差神經預後、D 白質病變(膠質增生/髓鞘不足) 皆為 CMV 正確影像特徵。詳見 [[torch-infection-neuroimaging]]。
