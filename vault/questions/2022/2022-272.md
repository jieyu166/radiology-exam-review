#交換 #2022交換 #ABD Which of the following MR imaging finding allows differentiation of endometriomas from other differential diagnoses of an adnexal lesion?
(A) Endometriomas tend to have higher T1 and higher T2 signal intensities than hemorrhagic cysts.
(B) The loss of signal intensity within a T1-hyperintense adnexal mass at fat-suppressed imaging facilitates characterization of the mass as a mature cystic teratoma.
(C) High signal intensity on STIR MR images is specific for mature cystic teratoma and may exclude endometrioma.
(D) None of the above.
??
**Ans: B**
B 正確：T1 高訊號腫塊若在抑脂（fat-suppressed）序列上訊號「下降」，代表其成分為脂肪，可判為成熟囊性畸胎瘤（mature cystic teratoma）；相對地 endometrioma 為血性內容、抑脂後仍維持高訊號，故此表現可資鑑別。A 錯：endometrioma 典型 T2 因 shading 而「偏低」，並非比出血性囊腫更高的 T2。C 錯：STIR 高訊號並非畸胎瘤特異——脂肪在 STIR 反而被抑制，且此表現無法排除 endometrioma。故選 B。→ [[endometrioma]]
