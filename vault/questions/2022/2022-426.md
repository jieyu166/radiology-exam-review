#交換 #2022交換 #CV Which one is NOT **true** about Pectus excavatum?
(A) It is about the deformity of chest wall characterized by sternal depression
(B) Haller index is the ration of transverse diameter divided by sagittal diameter of chest
(C) Measurement of Haller index is starting from outer aspect of chest wall.
(D) Pectus excavatum is as known as funnel chest
??
**Ans: C**
本題問「何者為非」。Haller index 取自內側骨性標記（AP 徑為胸椎體前緣到胸骨後緣、橫徑為胸廓內側），並非自胸壁外側（outer aspect）測量，故 C 錯誤（即答案）。A（胸骨凹陷之胸壁畸形）、B（橫徑÷前後徑）、D（又稱 funnel chest 漏斗胸）皆正確。→ [[pectus-excavatum]]
