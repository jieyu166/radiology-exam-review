#交換 #2022交換 #ABD Of the features below, which may help discriminate pancreatic neuroendocrine tumor from adenocarcinoma?
(A) lower degree of main pancreatic duct obstruction
(B) heterogeneous and less enhancement of the tumor
(C) higher fibrotic component and infiltration into surrounding tissue
(D) marked distal pancreatic parenchymal atrophy
??
**Ans: A**
答案 A。可鑑別 PNET 與胰管腺癌（PDAC）者為「較低程度的主胰管阻塞」——PNET 多為富血管、界清、對胰管壓迫／阻塞較輕，而 PDAC 為乏血管、促結締組織增生，典型造成上游胰管阻塞與遠端胰實質萎縮。B（異質、較弱強化）、C（高纖維成分、浸潤周圍）、D（明顯遠端胰實質萎縮）皆為 PDAC 之特徵，非 PNET；PNET 反而是動脈期高強化。故僅 A 指向 PNET。詳見 → [[pancreatic-neuroendocrine-tumors]]
