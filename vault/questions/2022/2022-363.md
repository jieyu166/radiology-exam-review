#交換 #2022交換 #ABD Which of the following statement of Pancreatic Cysts about worrisome feature is **wrong**?
() Cyst > 3cm Thickened/enhancing cyst wall Enhancing mural nodule Main pancreatic duct caliber > 7 mm
??
**Ans: C**
依 2017 ACR White Paper，worrisome imaging features 為 cyst ≥3 cm、thick/enhancing wall、NON-enhancing mural nodule、main pancreatic duct ≥7 mm。選項『Enhancing mural nodule』為錯——enhancing solid component/mural nodule 屬 high-risk stigmata（非 worrisome，worrisome 為 non-enhancing mural nodule）。⚠️ 原始選項四項並排於單一字串，錯誤者為第三項 Enhancing mural nodule，對應官方答案 C。 → [[pancreatic-cystic-lesions]]
