#交換 #2022交換 #MSK The following descriptions about wrist, which one is **correct**?
(A) A. Ulnar impaction syndrome is almost always associated with a negative ulnar variance.
(B) B. The radial attachment of the TFC may appear bifurcated, with two bands of low signal intensity separated by a region of higher signal intensity.
(C) C. Osteonecrosis of the scaphoid bone is called Kienbock's disease.
(D) D. Lunotriquetral dissociation can lead to volar intercalated segmental instability.
??
**Ans: D**
本題問「何者正確」，正解為 D（原題庫答案 A 經查證判定為誤 ⚠️）。D 正確：VISI（volar intercalated segment instability，lunate 掌傾）主要源自 lunotriquetral 韌帶（及掌側 ulnar arcuate 韌帶尺側半）損傷，故 lunotriquetral dissociation 可導致 VISI。A 錯：ulnar impaction syndrome 大多伴隨 positive（正）ulnar variance，非 negative；尺骨相對過長撞擊 lunate 尺側與 triquetrum 橈側。B 錯：呈分叉/兩條低訊號帶夾一段高訊號的正常變異位於 TFC 的尺側附著（ligamentum subcruentum 區），而非題目所述之 radial（橈側）附著。C 錯：Kienböck disease 是 lunate 的骨壞死；scaphoid 骨壞死應稱 Preiser disease。詳見 [[wrist-ulnar-variance-carpal-instability]]。
