#交換 #2022交換 #ABD Which statement is **true** regarding testicular microlithiasis :
(A) Incidental finding during ultrasound of scrotum
(B) Link between testicular microlithiasis as well as male infertility
(C) Clinical consequence depend on co-occurrence of risk factors.
(D) Testicular microlithiasis alone in the absence of risk factor is not indication for further investigation.
(E) All of the above
??
**Ans: E**
答案 E。四項敘述皆正確：(A) 睪丸微石症多為陰囊超音波偶然發現；(B) 文獻報告與男性不孕有相關；(C) 臨床意義取決於是否共存風險因子（GCT 個人/家族史、隱睪、orchidopexy、睪丸萎縮、不孕）；(D) 單純 TM 且無風險因子並非進一步檢查適應症。故 All of the above。→ [[testicular-microlithiasis]]
