#交換 #2022交換 #CV Coronary computed tomographic angiography (CCTA) has been recommended as a frontline testing strategy in the evaluation of patients with stable and acute chest pain who have no known coronary artery disease (CAD). CCTA was given
(A) Class 1 recommendation with level of evidence of A for patients with stable and acute chest pain
(B) Class 2 recommendation with level of evidence of B for patients with stable and acute chest pain
(C) Class 2 recommendation with level of evidence of A for patients with stable and acute chest pain
(D) Class 1 recommendation with level of evidence of B for patients with stable and acute chest pain
??
**Ans: A**
⚠️ 本題考 2021 AHA/ACC 胸痛評估指引對 CCTA 的建議層級——對無已知 CAD、中-高風險之急性或穩定胸痛病人，CCTA 獲最高之 Class 1（強）建議、佐以 Level of Evidence A，故對映選項為 A。本概念（[[ccta]]）以 CCTA 之診斷效能（ACCURACY 試驗高 NPV、rule-out 定位）與 CAD-RADS 報告框架為主，未逐字收錄該指引之 class/LOE 條文，答案依原卷保留為 A 並標記待醫師對照 2021 指引原文。此高 NPV「守門員」角色與指引將其列為前線 rule-out 策略的方向一致。→ [[ccta]]
