#交換 #2022交換 #ABD Which one is not **true** about the disadvantages of gadoxetic acid-enhanced MRI?
(A) The arterial and venous phase enhancement seen with gadoxetic acid is as weak compared with that seen with conventional extracellular contrast agents.
(B) Transient dyspnea and transient severe motion are more.
(C) The uptake of gadoxetic acid in liver parenchyma decreases as liver function or fibrosis worsens.
(D) None of the above.
??
**Ans: D**
本題問關於 gadoxetic acid 缺點「哪一項不正確」。A：相較傳統細胞外對比劑，Gd-EOB-DTPA 動脈期/靜脈期強化較弱（血中濃度低，真實缺點）。B：短暫呼吸困難與短暫嚴重移動較常見（Davenport 2013、Motosugi 2016，真實缺點）。C：肝功能不良/纖維化惡化時肝實質攝取下降（Motosugi 2009、Nishie 2012，真實缺點）。A–C 皆為真實缺點，故不正確的選項是 D「None of the above」。→ [[hepatobiliary-mri-contrast]]
