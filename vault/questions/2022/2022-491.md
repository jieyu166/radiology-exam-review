#交換 #2022交換 #NR Which diagnosis is also known as “surfer's ear”?
(A) External auditory canal exostoses
(B) External auditory canal osteomas
(C) Keratosis obturans
(D) Cholesteatoma
??
**Ans: A**
外耳道 exostoses（外耳道骨疣）俗稱「surfer's ear／衝浪者耳」，肇因於冷水反覆暴露引起的反應性骨增生。顳骨 CT 上典型為兩側、寬基底、環繞骨性外耳道的骨性增生，可造成外耳道狹窄。B（osteoma）為單側、單發、有蒂的骨腫塊；C（keratosis obturans）與 D（cholesteatoma）為角化物/軟組織病灶，皆非「surfer's ear」。→ [[eac-exostoses]]
