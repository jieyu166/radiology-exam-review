#交換 #2022交換 #NR Which of the following diseases does NOT demonstrate bilateral multifocal subcortical DW hyperintensities?
(A) Alexander disease
(B) Canavan disease
(C) Fragile X-associated tremor/ataxia syndrome
(D) Neuronal intranuclear inclusion disease
??
**Ans: A**
神經元核內包涵體病（NIID）招牌徵象＝雙側皮質-髓質交界沿腦回呈帶狀 DWI 高訊號；Canavan 累及皮質下 U-fibers、FXTAS 亦可見皮質下白質／DWI 變化，皆可表現雙側皮質下 DWI 高訊號。Alexander disease 為額葉為主、累及深部白質與基底核、腦幹的白質失養症，非此皮質下多灶 DWI 型態，故為「不」符合者。→ [[bilateral-subcortical-dwi-hyperintensity-ddx]]
