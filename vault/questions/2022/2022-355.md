#交換 #2022交換 #NR Which is **wrong** about the MRI vessel wall images?
(A) Atherosclerotic plaque: usually concentric wall thickening and homogeneous enhancement
(B) Arterial dissection: intimal flap on T2-weighted images, intramural hematoma on T1-weighted images
(C) intracranial saccular aneurysms: lack of wall enhancement predicts aneurysm stability
(D) Extracranial carotid artery atherosclerotic plaque: detect features associated with high risk for ischemic events, such as thin/ruptured fibrous cap, large lipid-rich necrotic core, or intraplaque hemorrhage
??
**Ans: A**
A 為錯誤。動脈粥狀硬化斑塊在血管壁 MRI 上為偏心（eccentric）管壁增厚、不均勻（heterogeneous）強化；『同心圓＋均勻強化』是血管炎（vasculitis）的型態。選項 B（剝離：T2 內膜瓣、T1 壁內血腫）、C（囊狀動脈瘤：管壁無強化預測穩定）、D（顱外頸動脈斑塊高風險特徵）皆與 Radiopaedia、STATdx 相符、正確。→ [[mri-vessel-wall-imaging]]
