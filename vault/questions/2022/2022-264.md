#交換 #2022交換 #US Which of the following statements about the ultrasound appearance of hepatic cavernous hemangioma is **not true**?
(A) Typical hemangioma is a well-defined, uniformly hyperechoic mass
(B) Internal vascularity is often undetectable with color Doppler
(C) May see posterior acoustic enhancement
(D) “Typical atypical” hemangioma shows hypoechoic rim with hyperechoic center
??
**Ans: D**
錯誤處在於「typical atypical hemangioma shows hypoechoic rim with hyperechoic center（低回音暈＋高回音中心）」——此描述與典型不符：脂肪肝背景下的不典型血管瘤反而呈「高回音暈／低回音中心」（相對背景反轉），下方課本示意圖 panel ① 即為此樣態。![[Pasted image 20260711221853.png]]（課本示意 Figures 1–3，不典型肝血管瘤的三種超音波樣態：① 低回音型——邊緣扇貝狀高回音、內部以低回音為主，即「高回音暈＋低回音中心」，正與選項 D 所述相反；② 網狀型——病灶內線狀高回音帶形成 reticular pattern；③ 顆粒型——細點狀回音形成 granular 外觀。）敘述中其他項正確：典型血管瘤為界清、均質高回音腫塊（A）；color Doppler 中央常測不到血流（慢流，B）；可見後方穿透增強（acoustic enhancement，非 shadowing，C）。詳見 → [[hepatic-hemangioma]]
