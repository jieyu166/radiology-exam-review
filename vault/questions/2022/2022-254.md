#交換 #2022交換 #NR Which one of following descriptions is **WRONG** regarding vertebral artery dissection (VAD)?
(A) Spontaneous VAD occurs most commonly in middle-age females.
(B) Pain is a prominent symptom in approximate 70% patients, most localized at neck and occiput areas.
(C) Lateral medullary (Wallenberg) infarct is rarely seen in VAD.
(D) The vertebral artery is most vulnerable to dissection after it leaves the foramen transversarium of C2.
??
**Ans: C**
椎動脈剝離（VAD）常造成後循環梗塞，其中外側延髓（Wallenberg）症候群是 VAD 最典型的梗塞表現之一，並非罕見，故 C「lateral medullary (Wallenberg) infarct rarely seen」敘述錯誤。A（自發性 VAD 好發中年、女性略多）、B（約 70% 以頸部/枕部疼痛為顯著症狀）、D（椎動脈於 C2 離開橫突孔轉折後最易剝離，即 V3 活動段）皆為 VAD 之正確描述。本概念以剝離之壁內血腫影像與 ICAD 為主，VAD 後循環梗塞屬同源延伸判讀。→ [[arterial-dissection-mri]]
