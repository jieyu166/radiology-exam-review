#交換 #2022交換 #MSK Which description about plantar fibroma is **WRONG**?
(A) Plantar fibromas (Ledderhose disease) are the most common solid soft tissue tumor in the foot and ankle.
(B) Patients typically complain of a palpable mass along the plantar aspect of the foot and may experience mild pain after standing or walking for long periods.
(C) Due to their high collagen content, plantar fibromas are uniformly high signal on T1- and T2-weighted images.
(D) Plantar fibromas typically have internal enhancement and can have a fascial tail sign where linear foci of enhancement extend from the lesion along the aponeurosis.
??
**Ans: C**
本題問「哪一項描述錯誤」。C 為錯：足底纖維瘤因膠原含量高，在 T1、T2 上其實均呈等-低（低-中等）訊號，而非「均勻高訊號」（Radiopaedia：T1 iso-to-low、T2 low-to-intermediate；STATdx：T1 isointense、T2 hypo-to-isointense）。A（Ledderhose disease，足踝最常見實性軟組織腫瘤）、B（足底可觸及腫塊、久站/久走後輕微疼痛）、D（內部強化＋fascial tail sign）皆為正確描述，其中 D 的內部強化與 fascial tail sign 為真實特徵。⚠️ 匯入原始答案標為 D，經 Radiopaedia＋STATdx 查證更正為 C。→ [[plantar-fibroma]]
