#交換 #2022交換 #Breast Which of the following about Breast MR imaging is INCORRECT?
(A) An increased sensitivity for cancer detection in certain high-risk women, but a decrease in specificity and high false positive rate.
(B) Incremental cancer detection rate is 8-36 per 1000 for MR imaging and 3-6 per 1000 for mammography.
(C) MR is costly, requires IV gadolinium, and not tolerated by prone positioning.
(D) Combination of mammography and MR imaging have a lower cancer yield and high false positive rate than whole breast US combined with mammography.
??
**Ans: D**
本題問「何者為 INCORRECT」。乳房攝影＋MRI 的癌症偵測率高於（並非低於）超音波＋乳房攝影，故 D 錯誤（即答案）。A（高風險女性敏感度提高、特異度下降、偽陽性高）、B（增額偵測率 MR 8–36/1000、乳房攝影 3–6/1000）、C（昂貴、需靜脈釓、成本/耐受限制）皆為正確或可接受敘述。→ [[breast-mri-enhancement-patterns]]
