#交換 #2022交換 #MSK Which tendon sits medially to the os trigonum and in the sulcus between the medial tubercle and the larger lateral tubercle of talus?
(A) The flexor hallucis longus (FHL) tendon
(B) Peroneal Tendon
(C) Posterior tibial tendon
??
**Ans: A**
距骨後突有內、外側兩結節，屈拇長肌腱（FHL）走於兩結節之間的溝內、位於 os trigonum（未融合的外側結節）內側，此處 FHL 腱鞘炎/夾擠是後踝疼痛常見原因。腓骨肌腱走外踝後方、脛後肌腱走內踝後方，皆非此溝內結構。答案 A。→ [[accessory-ossicles-foot]]
