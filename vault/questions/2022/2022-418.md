#交換 #2022交換 #Breast 根據 BIRADS ,下列哪一種 乳房鈣化的Likelihood of malignancy 最高 ?
(A) Round circumscribed
(B) Oval circumscribed
(C) Oval microlobulated
(D) Irregular spiculated
??
**Ans: D**
答案 D。⚠️ 題幹寫「乳房鈣化」但四個選項實為 BI-RADS 腫塊（mass）之 shape/margin 描述詞（round/oval/irregular 之形＋circumscribed/microlobulated/spiculated 之緣），非鈣化型態。依 BI-RADS，腫塊惡性風險隨 shape（oval/round→irregular）與 margin（circumscribed→microlobulated→spiculated）遞增，故 Irregular spiculated 惡性可能性最高；round/oval circumscribed 為典型良性。→ [[breast-calcifications]]
