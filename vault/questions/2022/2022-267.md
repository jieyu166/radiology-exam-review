#交換 #2022交換 #CH Incorrect statement about “ crazy paving ” pattern?
(A) Crazy Paving is a combination of ground glass opacity with superimposed septal thickening. It was first thought to be specific for alveolar proteinosis.
(B) In pathology, linear appearance is due to air space process, which is more severe in peripheral of lobule and not due to interstitial involvement itself.
(C) In eosinophilic pneumonia, consolidation and ground glass is more common than crazy-paving pattern.
(D) Cryptogenic pneumonia may have crazy paving pattern and upper lung predominant
??
**Ans: B**
Crazy-paving 定義為毛玻璃（GGO）疊加小葉間隔（interlobular）＋小葉內間隔（intralobular）增厚所成的網狀線影，其線狀成分本質上「包含間隔（間質）增厚」，並非單純肺泡腔（air space）過程。選項 B 稱線狀外觀「純因肺泡腔過程、於小葉周邊較嚴重、而非間質受累本身」——否認間質成分，與 crazy-paving 的定義相矛盾，故為錯誤敘述，選 B。A（合併 GGO 與間隔增厚、最初被認為對 PAP 具特異性）、C（嗜酸性肺炎以實質化與 GGO 較常見、crazy-paving 較少）、D（COP/隱源性可呈 crazy-paving、可上肺為主）皆屬正確。→ [[crazy-paving]]
