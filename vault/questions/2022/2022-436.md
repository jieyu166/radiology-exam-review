#交換 #2022交換 #CV According to SCCT guidelines in coronary artery segmentation, segment 18 pertains to
(A) Ramus intermedius
(B) Left posterolateral branch
(C) Right posterolateral branch
(D) Right posterior descending artery.
??
**Ans: B**
SCCT 18 節冠狀動脈模型是在原始 1975 AHA 15 節模型上加入三支：第 16 節＝右冠狀動脈的後外側支(RPL)、第 17 節＝ramus intermedius(中間支)、第 18 節＝迴旋支的左後外側支(left posterolateral branch, LPL)。故 segment 18＝Left posterolateral branch（B）。Ramus intermedius 為第 17 節、Right posterolateral branch 為第 16 節。→ [[scct-coronary-segmentation]]
