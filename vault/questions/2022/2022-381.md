#交換 #2022交換 #NR Which is not the typical process in radiomic analysis?
(A) Feature extraction
(B) Feature selection
(C) Segmentation
(D) Cross validation
(E) Backpropagation (屬於machine learning)
??
**Ans: E**
Radiomics 標準工作流程為 segmentation → feature extraction → feature selection → 建模與 cross-validation；backpropagation 是神經網路（深度學習）之訓練演算法，非 radiomics 分析之典型步驟，故選 E。→ [[machine-learning-radiomics-basics]]
