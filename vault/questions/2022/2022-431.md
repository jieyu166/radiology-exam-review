#交換 #2022交換 #CH Which statement about CT feature of ''chronic pulmonary thromboembolism '' is not **true**
(A) abrupted narrowing of distal artery when complete obstruction
(B) post stenoic vessles narrowing was found when partial obstruction
(C) thrombus often present with obtused angle with related vessles
(D) sometimes may see thrombus calcification
??
**Ans: B**
B 為不正確敘述。慢性肺栓塞部分阻塞時，血流通過狹窄段後造成的是狹窄後擴張（post-stenotic dilatation）與管徑不一，而非選項所述的 post-stenotic vessel narrowing（狹窄後變窄）。A（完全阻塞時遠端動脈突然變細/pouch defect）、C（慢性血栓偏心貼壁、與管壁呈鈍角 obtuse angle，有別於急性栓塞的銳角/中央充盈缺損）、D（可見血栓鈣化）皆為慢性肺栓塞的正確 CT 徵象。→ [[chronic-pulmonary-thromboembolism]]
