#交換 #2022交換 #MSK Which of the following components of the ulnar collateral ligament is often intact even when the radial head and coronoid process are both fractured ? Anterior bundles. Transverse bundles Posterior bundles None of above
??
**Ans: A**
Anterior bundle 是 UCL 抗外翻主力，附著於 coronoid 的 sublime tubercle。肘關節骨折-脫位中 coronoid 常為小的 tip 型骨折（位於 sublime tubercle 遠端），故即使 radial head 與 coronoid 同時骨折，anterior bundle 常仍完整。posterior bundle 附著於內側鷹嘴、transverse（Cooper's）bundle 幾無穩定作用。→ [[ulnar-collateral-ligament-injury]]
