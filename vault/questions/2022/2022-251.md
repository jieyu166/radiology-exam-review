#交換 #2022交換 #CV Regarding MR imaging in the evaluation of myocardial infarction, which of the following is the MOST important technique?
(A) T2-weighted
(B) First pass perfusion
(C) Velocity-encoded phase-contrast
(D) Delayed-enhancement
??
**Ans: D**
評估心肌梗塞（疤痕/存活心肌）最重要的技術為延遲增強（delayed-enhancement，即 late gadolinium enhancement, LGE）：注射釓對比劑後延遲取像，正常心肌洗掉對比劑、梗塞疤痕因細胞間隙擴大而滯留對比劑呈高訊號，並可依透壁程度（>50% 壁厚為 transmural）預測血運重建後恢復機會，故選 D。A（T2WI）看的是水腫；B（first pass perfusion）看首過灌流缺損；C（velocity-encoded phase-contrast）看流速/流量，皆非評估梗塞疤痕的首選。→ [[cardiac-mri-lge]]
