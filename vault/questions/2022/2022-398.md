#交換 #2022交換 #CV Which of the following descriptions about annuloaortic ectasia is **wrong**?
(A) It refers to a proximal dilatation of the aortic root at the level of the sinotubal junction and sinus of Valsalva It usually occurs with connective tissue diseases and is most commonly associated with Marfan syndrome As the aortic root expands, poor aortic cusp coaptation and distortion of the cusps may eventually result in aortic insufficiency It puts the patients at increased risk for aortic dissection, which in turn may lead to rupture
??
**Ans: A**
⚠️ 選項於題庫匯入時合併為單一長字串（四個子敘述併入 A），請對照原卷。就內容判斷：把 annuloaortic ectasia 說成「主動脈根部在 sinotubular junction 與 sinus of Valsalva 層面擴張」為錯誤——擴張層面在 aortic annulus／sinus of Valsalva，且 sinotubular junction 是被抹平（effacement）而非擴張的部位（tulip bulb sign，見於 60–80% Marfan）。其餘敘述（常見於結締組織病、最常合併 Marfan；主動脈瓣 coaptation 不良致 aortic insufficiency；增加 dissection 與破裂風險）皆正確。→ [[annuloaortic-ectasia]]
