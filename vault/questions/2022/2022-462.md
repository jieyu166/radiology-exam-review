#交換 #2022交換 #MSK Which of the following characteristically causes a short segment kyphoscoliosis of the thoracic spine?
(A) Osteoid osteoma.
(B) Neuromuscular scoliosis.
(C) Neurofibromatosis.
(D) Chiari malformation with syrinx.
??
**Ans: C**
Neurofibromatosis（NF1）的營養不良型脊椎側彎典型為短節段、銳角、累及 < 6 個脊椎節的胸椎後側凸，常伴椎體扇形凹陷、椎弓根發育不良與肋骨帶狀變形。相對之下 Neuromuscular scoliosis(B) 為長節段平緩 C 形長弧、Chiari 合併脊髓空洞(D) 側彎多為長節段非銳角、Osteoid osteoma(A) 造成疼痛性、凹面朝向病灶的側彎而非短節段角狀後側凸。→ [[nf1-dystrophic-scoliosis]]
