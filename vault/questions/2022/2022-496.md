#交換 #2022交換 #NR Which of the following cannot have discrete multiple brain masses?
(A) Gliomatosis cerebri
(B) Metastases
(C) Lymphoma
(D) Multifocal glioblastomas
??
**Ans: A**
Gliomatosis cerebri 為瀰漫浸潤性生長型態（依定義累及 ≥3 腦葉、常雙側、可跨胼胝體、通常不強化且佔位效應輕），呈單一瀰漫浸潤而非離散多發腫塊。Metastases、lymphoma、multifocal glioblastoma 皆可呈離散多發腦內腫塊，故唯 gliomatosis cerebri 不會有 discrete multiple masses。→ [[multiple-hypodense-brain-lesions]]
