#交換 #2022交換 #MSK Which one of the following radiographic features of the thoracolumbar spine is NOT usually seen in patients with ankylosing spondylitis?
(A) Syndesmophytes
(B) Squaring of the vertebrae
(C) Shiny corner appearance
(D) Picture frame appearance
??
**Ans: D**
「Picture frame appearance（畫框椎體）」是 Paget 病的椎體徵象（皮質與粗大骨小樑增厚勾勒出方框），非 AS 特徵。AS 胸腰椎典型 X 光徵象為細直垂直 syndesmophytes、椎體 squaring（方形化）、shiny corner／Romanus lesion（椎體角骨炎）。故「非 AS」者為 D。 → [[ankylosing-spondylitis]]
