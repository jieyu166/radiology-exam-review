#交換 #2022交換 #ABD Which subtype of renal cell carcinoma (RCC) is the most common subtype of RCC (about 75%), showing hypervascularized and heterogeneous appearance, usually intense contrast uptake in the corticomedullary phase and typical washout in the nephrographic phase ?
(A) clear cell RCC
(B) papillary RCC
(C) chromophobe RCC
(D) medullary RCC
??
**Ans: A**
Clear cell RCC 為最常見亞型（約 70–75%），屬 hypervascular，皮質髓質期明顯強化並於腎實質期相對洗出，因壞死／出血／囊變常呈不均勻外觀。Papillary(B) 乏血管、chromophobe(C) 中等強化 spoke-wheel、medullary(D) 罕見具侵襲性，皆不符。→ [[renal-cell-carcinoma]]
