#交換 #2022交換 #MSK When necrotizing fasciitis is suspected, which of the following CT findings is important in making a definitive diagnosis?
(A) Gas tracking with fluid collection along fascial planes.
(B) Asymmetric fascial thickening with stranding.
(C) Thickening and enhancement of the superficial or deep fascial layers.
(D) Reactive lymphadenopathy.
??
**Ans: A**
氣體沿筋膜層追蹤合併液體聚積(gas tracking with fluid collection along fascial planes)是壞死性筋膜炎 CT 上最具特異性的定性徵象(無穿刺傷/近期介入時近乎 pathognomonic)。不對稱筋膜增厚合併脂肪絮狀、淺/深筋膜層增厚與強化皆為非特異性(NF 與非 NF 皆可見)，反應性淋巴結亦非定性徵象。惟軟組織氣體僅見於 <50% 病例，無氣體不能排除 NF——NF 為臨床診斷，影像陰性不應延誤手術。→ [[necrotizing-fasciitis-imaging]]
