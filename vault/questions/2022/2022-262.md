#交換 #2022交換 #CH Which of the followings concerning lymphangioleiomyomatosis is **wrong**?
(A) A progressive cystic lung disease that occurs almost exclusively in women with Tuberous Sclerosis Complex
(B) Associated with multiple macronodular pneumocystic hyperplasia
(C) Characterized and defined by the presence of greater than 10 rounded, thin-walled air filled cysts
(D) Occurrence of spontaneous pneumothoraces.
??
**Ans: B** B「Associated with multiple macronodular pneumocystic hyperplasia」為錯誤——與 LAM／結節性硬化症併存的肺部病灶，**正確用詞為 multifocal micronodular pneumocyte hyperplasia（MMPH）**；題敘「macronodular pneumocystic hyperplasia」在結節大小（應為 micronodular 微結節，非 macronodular）與細胞名稱（pneumocyte 肺泡細胞，非 pneumocystic）皆用詞失準。A（進展性囊狀肺病、幾乎僅見於女性、與 TSC 相關）、C（以 >10 個圓形薄壁含氣囊腫為特徵）、D（可發生自發性氣胸）皆正確。→ [[lam]]
