#交換 #2022交換 #CH Which of the followings concerning lymphangioleiomyomatosis is **wrong**?
() A progressive cystic lung disease that occurs almost exclusively in women with Tuberous Sclerosis Complex Associated with multiple macronodular pneumocystic hyperplasia Characterized and defined by the presence of greater than 10 rounded, thin-walled air filled cysts Occurrence of spontaneous pneumothoraces.
??
**Ans: B**
⚠️ 原卷四個子敘述於匯入時併為單一段落、無法對應題號答案字母（來源答 B），僅能就內容判讀。LAM 為進展性囊狀肺病、幾乎僅見於女性（正確），可反覆自發性氣胸（正確），影像上以多發（>10）圓形薄壁含氣囊腫為特徵（正確）。錯誤在「occurs almost exclusively in women with Tuberous Sclerosis Complex」——LAM 分為散發型（S-LAM）與 TSC 相關型（TSC-LAM），並非幾乎僅發生於 TSC 患者；且與其併存者為 multifocal micronodular pneumocyte hyperplasia（MMPH），題敘「macronodular pneumocystic hyperplasia」亦屬用詞失準。故 TSC 專屬性之敘述為錯。→ [[lam]]
