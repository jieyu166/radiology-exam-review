#交換 #2022交換 #CH Which of the followings concerning lymphangioleiomyomatosis is **wrong**?
(A) A progressive cystic lung disease that occurs almost exclusively in women with Tuberous Sclerosis Complex
(B) Associated with multiple macronodular pneumocystic hyperplasia
(C) Characterized and defined by the presence of greater than 10 rounded, thin-walled air filled cysts
(D) Occurrence of spontaneous pneumothoraces.
??
**Ans: B** B「Associated with multiple macronodular pneumocystic hyperplasia」為錯誤——與 LAM／結節性硬化症併存的肺部病灶，**正確用詞為 multifocal micronodular pneumocyte hyperplasia（MMPH）**；題敘「macronodular pneumocystic hyperplasia」在結節大小（應為 micronodular 微結節，非 macronodular）與細胞名稱（pneumocyte 肺泡細胞，非 pneumocystic）皆用詞失準。C（以 >10 個圓形薄壁含氣囊腫為特徵）、D（可發生自發性氣胸）皆正確。⚠️ 附註：A「occurs almost exclusively in women **with Tuberous Sclerosis Complex**」措辭亦有瑕疵——LAM 幾乎僅見於女性無誤，但並非幾乎僅見於 TSC 患者：LAM 分為**散發型（S-LAM，無 TSC）**與 **TSC-LAM** 兩型，故 A 若嚴格解讀亦不夠精確；惟本題定調之錯誤選項（keyed answer）為用詞明確有誤的 B。→ [[lam]]
