#交換 #2022交換 #ABD Which choice best localizes the calcification of Medullary nephrocalcinosis?
(A) Renal cortex
(B) Renal pyramids and medulla
(C) Renal calyces
(D) Renal pelvis
(E) Renal vasculature
??
**Ans: B**
顧名思義，髓質腎鈣質沉著之鈣化位於腎髓質／腎椎體（renal pyramids and medulla）；髓質型佔腎鈣質沉著約 95%、皮質型僅約 5%。並非位於皮質、腎盞、腎盂或腎血管。→ [[medullary-nephrocalcinosis]]
