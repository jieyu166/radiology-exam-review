#交換 #2022交換 #PED Hindfoot valgus can be present in all the following conditions EXCEPT which of the following?
(A) Cerebral palsy.
(B) Skewfoot.
(C) Congenital vertical talus.
(D) Congenital talipes equinovarus.
??
**Ans: D**
D Congenital talipes equinovarus（clubfoot）為例外。CTEV 是後足內翻（varus），不會有後足外翻。A cerebral palsy（痙攣性平足外翻）、B skewfoot（後足外翻＋前足內收）、C congenital vertical talus（equinovalgus、rocker-bottom）皆屬後足外翻（valgus）疾病譜。→ [[pediatric-hindfoot-alignment]]
