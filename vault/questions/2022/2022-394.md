#交換 #2022交換 #MSK Which of the following ligaments is the strongest ligament in the lateral ligaments of ankle? Anterior talofibular ligament Calcaneofibular ligament Posterior talofibular ligament Spring ligament
(A) Anterior talofibular ligament
(B) Calcaneofibular ligament
(C) Posterior talofibular ligament
(D) Spring ligament
??
**Ans: C**
踝外側三條副韌帶中 Posterior talofibular ligament（PTFL，後距腓韌帶）最強、最厚、最少受傷；ATFL 最弱且最常在內翻扭傷中撕裂、CFL 居中。故選 Posterior talofibular ligament（C）。選項 D『Spring ligament（彈簧／跟舟足底韌帶）』屬內側／足底支撐結構，為干擾項。詳見 → [[lateral-ankle-ligaments]]
