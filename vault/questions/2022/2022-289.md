#交換 #2022交換 #CV What is **true** about spontaneous coronary artery dissection (SCAD)?
(A) SCAD can result in myocardial infarction
(B) Patient with SCAD do not benefit from MR angiography of abdomen and pelvis.
(C) Most prevalent coronary segment involved in SCAD was proximal coronary arteries.
(D) The disadvantage of coronary CT angiography (CCTA) over catheter coronary angiography in diagnosing SCAD is that CCTA cannot evaluate vascular wall and cannot detect intramural hematoma.
??
**Ans: A**
A 正確：SCAD 表現由急性冠心症/心肌梗塞至猝死皆可見，確實可造成心肌梗塞。B 錯：SCAD 與 fibromuscular dysplasia（可達 86%）、動脈瘤等血管病高度共病，指引建議所有病患做「腦到骨盆」血管影像篩檢，故 SCAD 病患確實受益於腹部/骨盆 MR 血管攝影。C 錯：SCAD 最常侵犯冠狀動脈的中段至遠段，僅 <10% 侵犯近段 LAD/迴旋支/右冠/左主幹（CCTA 較適用『近段』是模態解析度考量，勿與病灶好發部位混淆）。D 錯：CCTA 的優勢正是能顯示血管壁並直接偵測壁內血腫（新月狀/環狀管壁增厚、管腔外高衰減），此為加分而非缺點。詳解與影像判讀見 [[spontaneous-coronary-artery-dissection]]
