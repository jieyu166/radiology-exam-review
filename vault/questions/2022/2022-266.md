#交換 #2022交換 #Breast Which of the following about Mammographic Breast Density is CORRECT?
(A) Breast cancers most commonly arise in epithelial cells and fatty elements.
(B) A large breast volume indicates a greater chance that cancer arise.
(C) Mammographic sensitivity decreased with overlapping radiopaque dense breast known as masking effect.
(D) An increased percentage of interval cancers might benefit from screening mammography combined with Ultrasound.
??
**Ans: C**
C 正確：緻密（radiopaque）纖維腺體重疊會遮蔽腫塊、降低乳房攝影敏感度，即「遮蔽效應（masking effect）」，是 c/d 級緻密乳房的核心意義之一。A 錯：乳癌主要源自上皮細胞（epithelial），而非脂肪成分。B 錯：乳房體積大並不代表罹癌機率較高；提高風險的是緻密度（緻密組織比例）而非單純體積。D 敘述不精確：緻密乳房因敏感度下降間隔癌較多，補充篩檢（超音波/MRI）可提升癌偵測，但正確表述應為「緻密乳房者」受益，而非把間隔癌比率當受益主體，故非最佳選項。綜合以最直接正確者為 C。→ [[breast-composition]]
