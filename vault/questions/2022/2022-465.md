#交換 #2022交換 #PED Which is incorrect about germ cell tumor?
(A) Primary intracranial choriocarcinoma is most malignant
(B) Germinoma is most common
(C) Yolk sac tumor is leading perinatal tumor
(D) More common in Asians
??
**Ans: C**
C 為錯誤敘述。胎兒/圍產（perinatal）期最主要的先天性腦瘤是 teratoma（畸胎瘤，占胎兒腦瘤約 26–50%），並非 yolk sac tumor（卵黃囊瘤）。其餘正確：A 原發顱內 choriocarcinoma 最惡性；B germinoma 為最常見顱內生殖細胞瘤（占 60–80%）；D 亞洲（遠東/日本）發生率高數倍。→ [[intracranial-germ-cell-tumors]]
