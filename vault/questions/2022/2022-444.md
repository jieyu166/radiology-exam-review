#交換 #2022交換 #NR Which of the followings is the LEAST reliable factor to predict the size of infarct core at acute stage of ischemic stroke?
(A) Early infarct signs on brain CT
(B) NIH stroke scale (NIHSS)
(C) ASPECT score on Brain CT
(D) The presence of large artery occlusion
(E) Rapidly improving symptoms
??
**Ans: D**
急性期梗塞核心大小可由早期 CT 缺血徵象、ASPECTS、NIHSS 及症狀快速改善等因子估計。選項 D「大血管阻塞的存在」最不可靠：LVO 只表示有近端阻塞，不反映核心體積——同為 LVO 者核心可小可大，取決於側枝循環與時間窗（大 penumbra 者適合取栓）。故 D 為最不可靠的預測因子。→ [[ischemic-stroke-imaging-timeline]]
