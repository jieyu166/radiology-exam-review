#交換 #2022交換 #PED All of the following describe characteristic features of a dermal sinus EXCEPT:
(A) May be associated with a hairy nevus, hyperpigmented patch, or capillary hemangioma.
(B) Most frequently occurs in the cervicothoracic region.
(C) Often associated with a spinal dermoid or epidermoid.
(D) Complications may include abscess formation or meningitis
??
**Ans: B**
EXCEPT 題求「錯誤」敘述。B 為錯誤：dorsal dermal sinus 最常見於腰薦部（lumbosacral，STATdx ~70%），其次枕部，而非頸胸段。其餘皆為特徵：A 常伴多毛/色素斑/毛細血管瘤等皮膚徵象；C 常合併脊髓 dermoid 或 epidermoid（尤 paramedian 型）；D 併發症含膿瘍與復發性腦膜炎。→ [[dorsal-dermal-sinus]]。
