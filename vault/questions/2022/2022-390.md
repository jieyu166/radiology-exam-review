#交換 #2022交換 #PED Which description about features of coarctation is incorrect?
(A) berry aneurysms are a recognized complication
(B) the isthmus of the aorta is the usual site
(C) a lateral chest X-ray may show dilated internal mammary arteries
(D) the post-ductal type presents in infancy
??
**Ans: D**
『the post-ductal type presents in infancy（D）』為錯誤——導管後（adult／postductal）型多為 simple coarctation、常晚期才被發現；於嬰兒期表現者為 preductal（infantile）／complex coarctation（遠端血流依賴 PDA）。其餘正確：(A) berry aneurysm 為公認合併症（cerebral aneurysm 2.5–10%、可致 SAH）、(B) isthmus（峽部，左鎖骨下遠端）為最常見部位、(C) 側面 CXR 可見擴張之內乳（internal mammary）動脈側支（併 inferior rib notching／Roesler 徵）。詳見 → [[aortic-coarctation]]
