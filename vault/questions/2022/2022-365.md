#交換 #2022交換 #PED Which statement of Tetralogy of Fallot is **wrong**?
() Including: Obstructed RV outflow tract, right ventricular hypertrophy(RVH), VSD and Aorta overriding the IVS. Clinical finding including cyanosis by 3–4 months; time of presentation depends on degree of right ventricular hypertrophy. Plain radiograph shows Boot-shaped heart. Anomalies of coronary arteries about 5% (LAD from RCA, single RCA)
??
**Ans: B**
第二項『time of presentation depends on degree of right ventricular hypertrophy』為錯誤：TOF 的紫紺與臨床表現時機取決於 RVOT（肺動脈）阻塞的嚴重度，而非 RVH 程度；RVH 只是壓力過載的結果。其餘皆正確：四聯（RVOT 阻塞/RVH/VSD/主動脈跨騎）、CXR boot-shaped heart、冠狀動脈異常約 5%（LAD 自 RCA）。⚠️ 原始選項四項並排於單一字串，錯誤者為第二項，對應官方答案 B。 → [[tetralogy-of-fallot]]
