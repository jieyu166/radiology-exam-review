#交換 #2022交換 #ABD CT signs of pancreatic malignancy
() Focal mass (low density) Ductal dilatation Diffused enlargement All of the above
??
**Ans: D**
胰臟惡性（PDAC）之 CT 徵象包含焦點低密度腫塊（乏血管腫瘤故相對低衰減）、胰管／膽管擴張（含 double duct sign）與腺體局部或瀰漫性隆起／增大，三者皆為可見徵象，故選 All of the above。→ [[pancreatic-adenocarcinoma-mdct-phases]]
