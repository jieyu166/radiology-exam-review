#交換 #2022交換 #PED The following characteristic features of Sturge-Weber syndrome is correct?
(A) there is a “strawberry” nevus on the face
(B) the lesion is usually bilateral
(C) the intracerebral vascular lesion is usually well shown on arteriography
(D) calcification in the occipital cortex is visible on plain skull films
??
**Ans: D**
『calcification in the occipital cortex is visible on plain skull films（D）』為正確——SWS gyriform／tram-track 皮質下鈣化多在頂枕（parieto-occipital）區，歷史上可於顱骨平片顯示。其餘為錯：(A) 臉部為 port-wine nevus（capillary malformation），非 strawberry nevus；(B) 約 85% 為單側／單半球，非雙側；(C) 軟腦膜血管瘤為低流量病灶，動脈攝影常無法清楚顯示（DSA 反見淺層皮質靜脈缺如與異常深部引流）。詳見 → [[sturge-weber-syndrome]]
