#交換 #2022交換 #ABD Which of the following renal calculi would not be seen on plain radiography?
(A) Uric acid (the correct answer)
(B) Calcium oxalate
(C) Struvite
(D) Pure calcium phopshate
??
**Ans: A**
純尿酸（uric acid）結石透 X 光（radiolucent），平片看不到，須靠 CT 或超音波偵測；含鈣結石（calcium oxalate、pure calcium phosphate）與 struvite 含高原子序成分、於平片不透 X 光（radiopaque）而可見。故答案為 A。→ [[ureteral-calculi-imaging]]
