#交換 #2022交換 #ABD Which of the following description about retrocaval ureter is **wrong**?
(A) Retrocaval ureter is an anomalous segmental development of ureter rather than a vena cava anomaly.
(B) Symptoms usually present in the 3rd to 4th decades of life as a result of progressive obstructive uropathy.
(C) Intravenous urogram showing the pathognomonic “fish-hook” or the “S shaped” contrast illumination of the proximal ureter
(D) Obstruction is usually at the retrocaval segment of the ureter as it lies compressed between the IVC and the body of the third lumbar vertebra.
??
**Ans: A**
選項 A 為錯誤敘述：retrocaval (circumcaval) ureter 是「下腔靜脈(IVC)的發育異常」（right posterior cardinal vein 殘留），而非輸尿管本身的節段發育異常。其餘 B（3–4 十歲漸進阻塞）、C（IVU 魚鉤／S 形近端輸尿管）、D（阻塞位於 IVC 與 L3 椎體間之 retrocaval 段）皆正確，故選 A。→ [[retrocaval-ureter]]
