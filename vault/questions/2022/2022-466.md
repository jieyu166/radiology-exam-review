#交換 #2022交換 #PED Hindfoot varus is a component of which congenital deformity?
(A) Vertical talus.
(B) Oblique talus.
(C) Clubfoot.
(D) Pes planus.
??
**Ans: C**
C Clubfoot。後足內翻（hindfoot varus）是 clubfoot（congenital talipes equinovarus）的核心構成之一（助記 CAVE＝Cavus, forefoot Adductus, hindfoot Varus, Equinus）。A vertical talus 與 B oblique talus 屬後足外翻/中間型、D pes planus 為扁平（外翻）足，皆非內翻。→ [[pediatric-hindfoot-alignment]]
