#交換 #2022交換 #MSK Which description about rheumatic arthritis is **WRONG**?
(A) A. The three main findings of an inflammatory arthritis on MRI are synovitis, osteitis, and erosions.
(B) B. Synovitis, or synovial proliferation, is indicative of active disease and will appear as high signal on T2-weighted images without enhancement on post-contrast images.
(C) C. Osteitis is the presence of bone marrow edema in a subarticular location and seen in the early stage of the disease process.
(D) D. Most erosions in inflammatory arthritidies are at the periphery of the bone and joint capsule where there is absence of articular cartilage.
??
**Ans: B**
本題問「何者為非」。發炎性關節炎 MRI 三大徵象為 synovitis、osteitis、erosions（A 正確）。B 為錯：活動性滑膜炎（synovial proliferation）在 T2/fluid-sensitive 影像呈高訊號，且打對比劑後 T1 C+ FS 會「明顯強化（avidly enhancing）」以勾勒低訊號積液與侵蝕輪廓——B 稱「without enhancement」與活動性疾病特徵矛盾，故為非。C 正確（osteitis＝軟骨下骨髓水腫、疾病早期即現，且為侵蝕進展的強力預測因子）。D 正確（侵蝕多始於關節囊內、軟骨未覆蓋之『bare area』，如掌骨頭邊緣、指骨基部、尺/橈骨莖突）。⚠️ 原輸入標記答案為 D，經 STATdx（RA of Wrist and Hand）與 Radiopaedia 查證後更正為 B。→ [[ra-cervical-spine]]
