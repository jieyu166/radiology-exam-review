#交換 #2022交換 #Breast 3. About the axillary adenopathy after COVID-19 vaccination identified on mammography and breast ultrasound, which is **wrong**?
(A) The Moderna vaccine was more associated with axillary adenopathy than the other brands of COVID-19 vaccines.
(B) The axillary adenopathy more often occurred at the vaccine inoculated side.
(C) Mammography can detect more axillary adenopathy than ultrasound.
(D) The axillary adenopathy was more likely to be seen <=14 days after vaccination.
(E) Persistent axillary adenopathy can be seen up to 43 weeks after vaccination.
??
**Ans: C**
答案為 C（錯誤敘述）。COVID-19 疫苗接種後腋下淋巴腫大為良性、同側（接種側）、自限性反應。逐選項：A 正確——Moderna 較其他品牌更常見淋巴腫大（Moderna 46% vs Pfizer 38%，P=.007）。B 正確——多發生於接種側（同側）腋窩，符合淋巴引流。C 錯誤（本題答案）——超音波遠比乳房攝影敏感，同日檢查中 US 單獨偵測 61%、MG 單獨僅 9%，故「乳房攝影可比超音波偵測更多」為錯。D 正確——最常見於接種後 ≤2 週（14 天）內。E 正確——個案可持續至接種後 43 週。臨床處置：不因近期接種延遲篩檢乳房攝影，懷疑疫苗相關時採 ≥12 週追蹤間隔。詳見 [[covid-vaccine-axillary-adenopathy]]
