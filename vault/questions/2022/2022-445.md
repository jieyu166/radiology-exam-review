#交換 #2022交換 #NR Which one of the followings contraindications of r-tPA injection in acute ischemic stroke is **FALSE**?
(A) Patient has received heparin within 48 hours and has an elevated aPTT
(B) Platelet count < 100,000 /mm3
(C) Perform CTA study for further endovascular thrombectomy evaluation
(D) Abnormal blood glucose (400 mg/dL)
(E) Major surgery or serious non-head trauma in the previous 10 days
??
**Ans: C**
r-tPA 溶栓的真正禁忌包括：48 小時內用 heparin 且 aPTT 上升（A）、血小板 <100,000/mm³（B）、血糖異常如 400 mg/dL（D）、近 10–14 天內重大手術或嚴重非頭部外傷（E）。選項 C「進行 CTA 評估取栓」並非禁忌，而是評估大血管阻塞、規劃取栓的必要影像流程，故 C 為 FALSE（非禁忌）。→ [[acute-stroke-management]]
