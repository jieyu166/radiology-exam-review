#交換 #2022交換 #NR Elevated 2-hydroxyglutarate peak (2-HG) resonating at 2.25 ppm can be found in (可複選)
(A) Diffuse Astrocytoma, IDH-Mutant
(B) Anaplastic Astrocytoma, IDH-Mutant
(C) Diffuse Astrocytoma, IDH-Wild-Type
(D) Glioblastoma, IDH-Wild-Type
??
**Ans: A**
複選題，正解為 A 與 B。2-HG 僅在 IDH-mutant 神經膠質瘤堆積（突變型 IDH 把 α-ketoglutarate 還原成 D-2-HG），MRS 上共振於 2.25 ppm。A（Diffuse Astrocytoma, IDH-Mutant）與 B（Anaplastic Astrocytoma, IDH-Mutant）皆為 IDH-mutant，可見 2-HG 峰；C 與 D 為 IDH-wild-type，不產生 2-HG。→ [[2-hydroxyglutarate-idh-mutant-glioma]]
