#交換 #2022交換 #NR Which is **wrong** while considering endovascular treatment of the dural arteriovenous fistula?
(A) Benign vs aggressive: determine whether the AVF is angiographically benign or aggressive on the basis of the absence or presence of cortical venous reflux/drainage.
(B) Arterial supply and venous drainage: determine all visible arterial supplies to the AVF, the location of the fistulous point, and the primary venous drainage points. Cure of the AVF via embolization will be dependent on successful occlusion of the fistulous point and the foot of the draining vein.
(C) Risk from a transarterial (TA) embolization approach to embolize all the feeders proximally
(D) Risk from a transvenous (TV) embolization approach: Based on the venous drainage pattern, we determine the risk of intracranial hemorrhage, venous infarction, or long-term intracranial venous hypertension that would be associated with a TV embolization approach
??
**Ans: C**
dAVF 血管內治療評估要件：治癒取決於「成功閉塞瘻管點（fistulous point）與引流靜脈的根部（foot of draining vein）」，如 B 所述。選項 C「經動脈途徑把所有 feeder 近端栓塞」是錯誤觀念——近端 feeder 栓塞無法閉塞真正的瘻管點，只會促成側枝血管再徵召、造成日後更難治療，並非正確策略。A（依有無皮質靜脈逆流判良性/侵襲性）、B（辨明供血與引流、以閉塞瘻管點及引流靜脈根部為治癒關鍵）、D（依靜脈引流型態評估經靜脈途徑之出血/梗塞/靜脈高壓風險）皆為正確評估原則。故錯誤者為 C。→ [[dural-avf]]
