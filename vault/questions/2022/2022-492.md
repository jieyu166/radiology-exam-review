#交換 #2022交換 #NR Which is the best descriptor of the sonographic appearance of parathyroid adenomas?
(A) Uniformly hypoechoic, central vascularity
(B) Uniformly hypoechoic, peripheral vascularity
(C) Heterogeneously hypoechoic, central vascularity
(D) Heterogeneously hypoechoic, peripheral vascularity
??
**Ans: B**
副甲狀腺腺瘤超音波的最佳描述為均質（uniformly/homogeneously）低回音腫塊，Doppler 顯示由極性供血血管（polar vessel，多源自 inferior thyroid artery）造成的周邊／病灶周圍（perilesional/peripheral）血流。故選 B。「Heterogeneously（不均質）」較符合甲狀腺結節或退變/出血病灶；中央血流雖亦可見，但辨識性最高的判讀特徵是均質低回音＋周邊極性血流。→ [[parathyroid-adenoma]]
