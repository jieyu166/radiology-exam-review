#交換 #2022交換 #ABD Which subtype of renal cell carcinoma (RCC) may have a central sacr, which is indistinghishable from benign oncocytoma of kidney?
(A) clear cell RCC
(B) papillary RCC
(C) chromophobe RCC
(D) medullary RCC
??
**Ans: C**
Chromophobe RCC 可呈中央星狀疤痕與輻輪狀（spoke-wheel）強化，與良性 oncocytoma 影像重疊、不可僅憑此徵象區分（可能源自共同前驅細胞）。故中央疤痕非 oncocytoma 專屬。→ [[renal-cell-carcinoma]]
