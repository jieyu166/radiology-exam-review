#交換 #2022交換 #Breast Which description about male breast cancer is correct?
(A) A mass can be challenging in those men with moderate-to-marked gynecomastia.
(B) Tomosynthesis can better differentiate the fibroglandular tissue of gynecomastia from an underlying mass than 2D mammography can.
(C) Tomosynthesis images depict the irregular shape and indistinct or speculated borders, better differentiating a mass from the surrounding tissue.
(D) All of the above
??
**Ans: D**
A、B、C 皆為正確描述，故答 D「以上皆是」。男性乳癌典型為乳暈下略偏心之不規則腫塊，常被同時存在的男性女乳症（gynecomastia）纖維腺體遮蔽，故在中至重度 gynecomastia 中偵測腫塊確有困難（A 對）；斷層合成（tomosynthesis）藉逐層去除重疊組織，較 2D 乳攝更能把 gynecomastia 的纖維腺體與其下腫塊區分（B 對），並更清楚顯示癌之不規則形狀與界不清／毛刺緣（C 對）——與概念「癌偏離乳頭、界不清、可被 gynecomastia 遮蔽，US／進階攝影為必要」一致。→ [[male-breast-cancer]]
