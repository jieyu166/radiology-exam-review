#交換 #2022交換 #MSK Using ultrasound to diagnose necrotizing fasciitis, all of the following statements are **true** EXCEPT:
(A) Ultrasound is not useful in the pediatric age group.
(B) Sonographic findings include turbid fluid collection in the fascial layers.
(C) The examination may be limited by soft-tissue gas.
(D) Ultrasound may be used to guide fluid aspiration.
??
**Ans: A**
本題為 EXCEPT 題，找非真敘述。「Ultrasound is not useful in the pediatric age group」為錯誤——超音波在兒童族群反而更有用(Radiopaedia: 'Ultrasound may be more useful in children'，尤其原發性水痘感染後發生率上升)。其餘敘述皆正確：US 徵象含筋膜層混濁液體(turbid fluid)聚積(B)、軟組織氣體造成聲影而限制檢查(C)、US 可導引抽吸取樣(D)。→ [[necrotizing-fasciitis-imaging]]
