#交換 #2022交換 #NR Regarding to Fabry disease, which one is **wrong**
(A) An X-linked lysosomal storage disorder of glycosphingolipid metabolism
(B) NECT: bilateral, often symmetric calcification in basal ganglia and thalami
(C) Never dolichoectasia
(D) Abnormal accumulation of glycosphingolipids in various tissues
??
**Ans: C**
答案 C。C「Never dolichoectasia」錯誤：法布瑞氏症的血管病變典型即包含「基底動脈／椎基底動脈 dolichoectasia（擴張迂曲）」，並非從不發生。A 正確：為 X 性聯溶酶體儲積症、醣鞘脂代謝障礙（α-半乳糖苷酶 A 缺乏）。B 正確：NECT 可見雙側、常對稱之基底節與視丘鈣化。D 正確：醣鞘脂（globotriaosylceramide）異常堆積於多種組織。詳見 → [[fabry-disease-pulvinar]]
