#交換 #2022交換 #CV Which one of the following situations is not indicated for immediate stenting?
(A) Leriche syndrome May-Thurner syndrome and related venous thrombosis Acute femoral artery thrombosis due to arterial fibrillation Segmental chronic total occlusion of superficial femoral artery
??
**Ans: C**
⚠️ 選項於題庫匯入時合併為單一長字串，請對照原卷。就內容判斷：Acute femoral artery thrombosis due to atrial fibrillation（心房顫動引起的急性股動脈栓塞）不適合即時支架——急性栓子阻塞第一線為 embolectomy／catheter-directed thrombolysis 清除血栓，而非直接放支架。其餘（Leriche syndrome 主動脈-髂動脈阻塞、May-Thurner 及相關靜脈血栓、SFA 節段性慢性完全閉塞）皆為結構性狹窄/閉塞病灶，適合血管成形±支架。→ [[peripheral-vascular-immediate-stenting]]
