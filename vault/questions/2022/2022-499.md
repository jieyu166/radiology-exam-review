#交換 #2022交換 #PED Which choice likely represents the cause of hindfoot valgus?
(A) Amniotic band syndrome
(B) Osteogenesis imperfecta
(C) Cerebral palsy
(D) Nonaccidental trauma
(E) Muscular dystrophy
??
**Ans: C**
痙攣型腦性麻痺（cerebral palsy）屬神經肌肉疾病，是兒童後足外翻／痙攣性平足外翻（pes planovalgus）的公認成因。Amniotic band syndrome 多致 equinovarus/束帶截肢、osteogenesis imperfecta 致骨脆弱與彎曲、nonaccidental trauma 致骨折、muscular dystrophy 多為 equinovarus/cavovarus 攣縮，皆非後足外翻的典型成因。→ [[pediatric-hindfoot-alignment]]
