#交換 #2022交換 #NR According to the American Joint Committee on Cancer (AJCC) TNM classification, what stage is a tumor with extrathyroidal extension into the sternothyroid muscle?
(A) T1
(B) T2
(C) T3
(D) T4
??
**Ans: C**
sternothyroid（胸骨甲狀肌）為 strap 帶狀肌之一；AJCC 第 8 版中 gross extrathyroidal extension 只侵犯帶狀肌（sternohyoid/sternothyroid/thyrohyoid/omohyoid）＝T3b，仍歸 T3。超過帶狀肌侵入喉/氣管/食道/皮下/喉返神經才升為 T4a。→ [[thyroid-cancer-tnm-staging]]
