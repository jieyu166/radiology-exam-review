#交換 #2022交換 #Breast According to BI-RADS fifth edition, which of the following statement is **wrong**?
(A) In the “typically benign” category, eggshell and lucent-centered calcifications have been combined into a new term, rim, while round and punctate calcifications are combined into the term round
(B) Amorphous, dystrophic, coarse heterogeneous, fine pleomorphic, and fine linear or fine linear branching calcifications are now placed in the “suspicious morphology” category.
(C) The five remaining distributions of calcification are termed diffuse, segmental, regional, grouped, and linear.
(D) The number of calcifications in the “grouped” distribution has been revised to include (a) a minimum of five calcifications within 1 cm of each other and (b) a larger number of calcifications within 2 cm of each other.
??
**Ans: D**
⚠️ 本題選項文字為 OCR 擷取後嚴重不完整（僅存 A、B 兩段關於鈣化群聚範圍「至少 5 顆於彼此 1 cm 內／較多顆於 2 cm 內」的描述），無法自 [[breast-composition]] 概念完整逐項核對，暫依來源標記答案 D。就概念可確認者：BI-RADS 2013 最大改版為把緻密度「百分比估計」改為 a–d 四級主觀描述、強調緻密組織的遮蔽效應而非比例數字；「需報告纖維腺體約略比例（如 0–25%）」屬第 4 版做法、第 5 版已廢除，為此類題的高頻陷阱。建議醫師覆核原始完整題幹與選項。→ [[breast-composition]]
