#交換 #2022交換 #ABD What is the typical origin of uterine leiomyosarcoma? A: Drop metastasis from other organ B: Complication of molar pregnancy C: Arise from cesarean delivery scar D: May arise either de novo or in a preexisting leiomyoma
(A) A only
(B) A and C
(C) A,B,C
(D) D
??
**Ans: D**
子宮平滑肌肉瘤（leiomyosarcoma）多為 de novo 起源於子宮肌層或子宮血管結締組織，少數源自既有平滑肌瘤（leiomyoma）之惡性轉化（肉瘤化率僅 0.1–0.8%）；並非其他器官 drop metastasis、非葡萄胎併發、非剖腹產疤痕。故 A、B、C 皆非，唯 D 正確。→ [[uterine-position-and-sarcoma]]
