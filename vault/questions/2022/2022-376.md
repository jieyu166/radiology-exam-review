#交換 #2022交換 #Breast Which description about mammography in breast malignancy is correct?
(A) Malignant spiculations may be secondary to tumor cells or fibrosis.
(B) Including the full extent of the radiating fine spicules in the mammographic tumor measurement usually result in significant size overestimation of the tumor extent.
(C) If there is an absence of architectural distortion and a lack of sufficient contrast to define the mass margins, a malignant lesion may be occult to tomosynthesis imaging.
(D) All of the above
??
**Ans: D**
三敘述皆正確：毛刺可源於腫瘤細胞或纖維化（desmoplastic reaction，A）；把放射狀細毛刺完整範圍算入測量常顯著高估腫瘤範圍（B）；若缺乏結構扭曲且與周邊對比不足以界定邊緣，惡性病灶仍可能對斷層合成隱匿（C）。故選 All of the above。→ [[mammographic-signs-malignancy]]
