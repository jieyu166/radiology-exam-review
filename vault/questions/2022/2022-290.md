#交換 #2022交換 #CV Which of the following is **true** about noninvasive imaging for evaluating ischemic heart disease?
(A) A moderate stenosis of coronary artery (50-69% stenosis) are usually flow limiting.
(B) The best noninvasive study to function all assess coronary stenosis is coronary CT angiography followed by PET myocardial perfusion imaging.
(C) T1 mapping of cardiovascular MR is the best technique to detect left ventricular thrombus.
(D) A left ventricular pseudoaneurysm differ from left ventricular aneurysm by having narrow neck in CT and MR images.
??
**Ans: D**
正解 D。假性動脈瘤與真性動脈瘤的判讀分水嶺在「頸部寬窄」：假性動脈瘤是心室游離壁破裂被沾黏心包／血栓侷限，瘤壁不含心肌與心內膜，具特徵性窄頸（頸/瘤體最大徑比 <0.5，多為 0.25–0.5），破裂風險高（約 30–45%）；真性動脈瘤瘤壁保有心內膜＋心肌＋心外膜三層（僅纖維化變薄），呈寬頸，破裂風險低。CT/MR 上以頸部寬窄與瘤壁是否含心肌鑑別二者，故 D 正確。其餘皆錯：(A) 中度狹窄（50–69%）不一定造成血流限制，常需 FFR/功能性檢查確認；(B) CCTA 屬解剖性評估而非功能性，且敘述語意不成立；(C) 偵測左心室血栓最佳為延遲增強（LGE）／早期增強或超音波，非 native T1 mapping。詳見 [[left-ventricular-aneurysm-vs-pseudoaneurysm]]
