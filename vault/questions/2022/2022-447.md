#交換 #2022交換 #NR A 32-year-old person presents with acute-onset blindness. MRI shows subarachnoid hemorrhage, T2 hyperintensity of the putamina, and T2* blooming of the putamina. What is the most likely cause?
(A) Cyanide poisoning.
(B) Methanol poisoning.
(C) MDMA abuse.
(D) Hypoglycemia.
??
**Ans: B**
急性失明 + 雙側殼核（putamen）T2 高訊號 + T2*（GRE/SWI）blooming（出血性壞死）+ 蜘蛛膜下腔出血，是甲醇中毒（formic acid 毒性）的招牌組合，故答案為 B。甲醇代謝為 formic acid 造成視網膜與殼核毒性壞死，臨床先中樞抑制、後視覺障礙可進展至失明。氰化物（A）較典型侵犯蒼白球且非以殼核出血為主；MDMA（C）與低血糖（D，偏皮質/海馬）影像型態不符。→ [[methanol-toxicity]]
