#交換 #2022交換 #NR Which of the following statement about Moyamoya disease is **wrong**?
(A) “Puff of smoke” on angiography: numerous collaterals supplying ACA and MCA.
(B) Stenosis or occlusion of supraclinoid ICA.
(C) MRI: multiple tiny flow voids on T2W images, which are collaterals; engorged collaterals may produce FLAIR dark sulci(ivy sign).
(D) Occasionally, the posterior circulation is involved.
??
**Ans: C**敘述中錯誤之處為「engorged collaterals may produce FLAIR dark sulci (ivy sign)」——ivy sign 為 FLAIR 上沿腦溝腦回的「高訊號（bright）」，代表軟腦膜側支內緩慢逆行血流，並非「dark sulci」。其餘敘述皆正確：血管攝影 puff of smoke、supraclinoid ICA 狹窄／閉塞、T2 多發細小 flow void 為擴張側支、偶爾侵犯後循環。詳見 → [[moyamoya]]
