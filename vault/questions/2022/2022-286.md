#交換 #2022交換 #Breast 2. About the MRI appearance of breast DCIS (ductal carcinoma in situ), which is **wrong**?
(A) The most common MRI features of breast DCIS is non-mass enhancement (NME).
(B) If the MRI finding is not identified on initial ultrasound, a second-look ultrasound can be performed.
(C) (承上述), the MRI pattern of mass is more likely to be detected on second-look ultrasound than the non-mass enhancement
(D) On the dynamic contrast-enhanced MRI, DCIS most likely shows washout kinetics on delayed phase.
??
**Ans: D**
**D 為錯**——DCIS 於 MRI 最常見 non-mass enhancement（NME，clumped/linear/segmental），型態比 kinetics 可靠；延遲期動力學多變，**最常見為 plateau（非 washout）**，故「典型呈 washout」敘述有誤。A/B/C 皆為文獻支持之真敘述。
