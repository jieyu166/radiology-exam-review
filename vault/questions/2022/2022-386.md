#交換 #2022交換 #Breast Regarding the different molecular subtype of breast cancer, which of the following statements is incorrect?
(A) Luminal A tumors have the best prognosis of all subtypes.
(B) Luminal B tumors have significantly shorter overall and disease-free survival to luminal A tumors, require more aggressive treatment.
(C) HER2-enriched disease carries a poor prognosis, but greatly improved outcomes with the anti-HER2 therapies.
(D) Basal-like cancers are frequently high grade, high recurrent rate, usually presented as spiculated mass with pleomorphic calcifications or calcifications alone.
??
**Ans: D**
選項 D 為錯誤敘述：basal-like／三陰性乳癌雖為高惡性度、高復發率，但其典型影像實為邊界清楚(circumscribed)、圓／橢圓形、單發、且『不』伴鈣化，甚至可酷似良性病灶；而非毛刺狀腫塊＋多形性鈣化（那屬 luminal／HER2 型）。A/B/C 皆正確，故選 D。⚠️ 原始匯入選項退化為單一大段字串，已依 JBI 與 Radiopaedia 重建為 A–D 四選項。→ [[breast-cancer-molecular-subtypes]]
