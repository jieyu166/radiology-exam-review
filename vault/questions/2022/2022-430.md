#交換 #2022交換 #CH Which one is correct about the thoracic sequele of tuberculosis?
(A) Tuberculomas may be a manifestation of either primary or postprimary tuberculosis. After antituberculous chemotherapy, the tuberculous cavity may disappear and become solid lesion.
(B) Cicatrization atelectasis is a common finding after primary tuberculosis.
(C) Rasmussen aneurysm is a rare phenomenon caused by weakening of the pulmonary artery wall from adjacent cavitary tuberculosis
(D) Fibrothorax with diffuse pleural thickening but without effusion on CT scans suggests activity
??
**Ans: C**
C 為正確敘述：Rasmussen aneurysm 為結核空洞旁肺動脈壁受破壞、變弱所形成的（假性）動脈瘤，是罕見但可致命的咳血成因。B 錯：cicatrization atelectasis 是後原發性（reactivation）結核的後遺症，而非原發性結核的常見表現；D 錯：fibrothorax 合併瀰漫肋膜增厚但無積液提示不活動性/慢性病變，而非活動性；A 為干擾項（與標準教材陳述不符）。→ [[pulmonary-tuberculosis-imaging]]
