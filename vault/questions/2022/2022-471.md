#交換 #2022交換 #US Failure of the mesentery to fuse is a congenital anomaly associated with development of a(n):
(A) omental cyst
(B) mesentery cyst
(C) umbilical hernia
(D) Meckel diverticulum
??
**Ans: A** 
原文：「Congenital failure of the mesentery to fuse is a congenital anomaly associated with development of an omental cyst；mesenteric cysts are related to the Wolffian or lymph ducts」——腸繫膜未融合對應網膜囊腫，而腸繫膜囊腫源自 Wolffian 管／淋巴管。
但根據 Radiographics. 2021 Sep-Oct;41(5):1368-1386. 最適合的答案應該是 Peritoneal Simple Mesothelial Cyst (PSMC)，未出現在選項中。
