#交換 #2022交換 #US Failure of the mesentery to fuse is a congenital anomaly associated with development of a(n):
(A) omental cyst
(B) mesentery cyst
(C) umbilical hernia
(D) d. Meckel diverticulum
??
**Ans: A**
依 Radiopaedia，『腸繫膜兩葉未能正常融合（failure of the leaves of the mesentery to fuse）』正是腸繫膜囊腫（mesenteric cyst）的公認胚胎成因之一，故最佳答案為 B。
⚠️ 醫師覆核（2026-07-10）：改回原卷答案 **A（omental cyst）**。醫師依原文：「Congenital failure of the mesentery to fuse is a congenital anomaly associated with development of an omental cyst；mesenteric cysts are related to the Wolffian or lymph ducts」——腸繫膜未融合對應網膜囊腫，而腸繫膜囊腫源自 Wolffian 管／淋巴管。先前改為 B 之更正已撤回。
