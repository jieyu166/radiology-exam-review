#交換 #2022交換 #NR Which of the following is a characteristic of spinal cord hemangioblastoma?
(A) Classified as a neuroepithelial tumor.
(B) Appears at imaging with minimal contrast enhancement.
(C) Originates at the pial surface of the spinal cord.
(D) Familial cases with neurofibromatosis
??
**Ans: C**
脊髓 hemangioblastoma 為富血管的間質性腫瘤，典型起源於脊髓背側「軟膜／軟膜下（pial/subpial surface）」，故 C 正確——影像上表現為軟膜面強烈強化的小壁結節＋大囊/大範圍 syrinx＋T2 蛇形血管流空。A 錯：它並非神經上皮性（neuroepithelial）腫瘤（ependymoma/astrocytoma 才是膠質性）。B 錯：血管母細胞瘤屬「強烈強化」而非 minimal enhancement。D 錯：多發時的家族性關聯是 von Hippel-Lindau（VHL，約 30%），並非 neurofibromatosis。→ [[intramedullary-spinal-tumors]]
