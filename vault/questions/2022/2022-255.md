#交換 #2022交換 #CH Concerning tracheobronchial Injuries Imaging, which one of the following is not correct?
(A) Bronchoscopic procedures most commonly affect the cervical and proximal intrathoracic trachea, especially the less protected posterior membrane.
(B) The precise site of injury is easy, quick and confident to identify at MPR, MIP, and virtual bronchoscopic views.
(C) High-impact blunt force injuries tend to involve the intrathoracic distal trachea near the carina, with a slight preponderance of right-sided involvement.
(D) Endotracheal intubation usually causes cuff-related injuries rather than direct lacerations.
??
**Ans: B**
⚠️（原始匯入選項殘缺、僅顯示 A，答案沿用來源標記 B）就本題所示敘述而言，錯誤處在於「損傷處於 MPR／MIP／virtual bronchoscopy 上容易、快速且有把握地精確定位」——實際上 CT 僅能於約 70% 病例定位損傷處，並非「easy, quick and confident」。敘述中其他方向性正確：鈍性高衝擊力好發於近隆凸之遠端胸腔內氣管、右側略多；氣管內插管多為 cuff 相關傷而非直接撕裂。鈍傷主導遠端氣管、穿刺傷好發頸部氣管為本概念核心考點。詳見 → [[chest-trauma-imaging]]
