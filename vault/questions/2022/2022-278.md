#交換 #2022交換 #Physics Which one about GBCA (Gadolinium based contrast agents) is correct?
(A) The macrocyclic chelates are more stable than the linear type
(B) Evidence showing more deposition for macrocyclic chelates than linear type
(C) Gadolinium deposition in brain is harmful
(D) Linear chelates be banned in Taiwan by FDA (2017)
??
**Ans: A**
答案 A。macrocyclic（環狀）螯合較 linear（線狀）穩定，去螯合少、NSF 與腦沉積風險較低，為正確敘述。B 錯誤：沉積量 linear ＞ macrocyclic，非 macrocyclic 較多。C 錯誤：腦部釓沉積目前尚無證據造成失智／小腦症候群等臨床危害，臨床意義未明，不能斷言「有害」。D 錯誤：2017 為 EMA（歐洲）暫停／限制多數 linear 製劑，非台灣 FDA 全面禁用 linear。詳見 → [[gadolinium-contrast]]
