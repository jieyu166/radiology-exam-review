#交換 #2022交換 #ABD Given a patient with anterior pelvic arch fracture and soft tissue abnormalities indicative of a perivesical effusion demonstrated by the radiography. Which of the following procedure should be performed next?
(A) Insertion of the Foley catheter
(B) Retrograde urethrocystography
(C) Cystoscopy
(D) Pelvic angiography
??
**Ans: B**
前骨盆環骨折＋膀胱周圍積液高度懷疑尿道／膀胱損傷；此情境首選逆行尿道（膀胱）攝影(retrograde urethrocystography)評估尿道完整性，且必須在置入 Foley 導尿管之前進行——貿然插管可能將部分尿道斷裂惡化為完全斷裂。故選 B。→ [[bladder-urethral-trauma-imaging]]
