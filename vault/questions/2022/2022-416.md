#交換 #2022交換 #US Which of the following statements about the advantages of color and power Doppler is not **true**?
(A) Color Doppler determines the flow direction
(B) Color Doppler determines relative flow velocity
(C) Power Doppler is more affected by Doppler angle
(D) Power Doppler relates to blood-flow volume
??
**Ans: C**
「Power Doppler is more affected by Doppler angle（能量都卜勒較受都卜勒角度影響）」為錯誤——能量都卜勒以訊號振幅（power/amplitude）編碼，不依賴都卜勒角度（angle-independent）、無 aliasing，這正是其對慢/低流敏感的優勢。其餘為真：彩色都卜勒可判斷血流方向、可反映相對流速（依平均頻移編碼）；能量都卜勒與血流量（血球密度）相關。→ [[color-vs-power-doppler]]
