#交換 #2022交換 #CV Which of the following statements is NOT **true**?
(A) Cor triatriatum sinister is congenital anomaly with fibromuscular diaphragm or membrane dividing left atrium into posterior and anterior chambers
(B) Cor triatriatum dexter is the analogous anomality involving right atrium
(C) Posterior (or proximal) chamber receives all or part of pulmonary veins
(D) Anterior (or distal ) chamber receives all or part of pulmonary veins
??
**Ans: D**
本題問「何者為非」。依 AJR 2009（Dillman）全文與 Radiopaedia：cor triatriatum sinister 左心房被膜分為後（近端/proximal）與前（遠端/distal）兩腔，『後腔接受肺靜脈回流、前腔含二尖瓣供血至左心室』。故 D（前/遠端腔接受肺靜脈）錯誤（即答案）。A（左型定義）、B（dexter 為右心房類似畸形）、C（後/近端腔接受肺靜脈）皆正確。→ [[cor-triatriatum]]
