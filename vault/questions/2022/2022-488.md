#交換 #2022交換 #MSK Isolated soft-tissue swelling around the proximal interphalangeal joints of all fingers, the differential diagnosis should include which of the following?
(A) Juvenile idiopathic arthritis
(B) Pachydermodactyly
(C) Rheumatoid arthritis
(D) All of the above (correct!)
??
**Ans: D**
所有手指 PIP 周圍孤立性軟組織腫脹的鑑別須含三者：JIA 與 RA 早期以關節周軟組織腫脹起始（續有骨旁疏鬆、關節間隙變窄、侵蝕）；pachydermodactyly 則為良性、好發年輕男性、無骨侵蝕的軟組織纖維增生，是不可漏的非發炎性鑑別。故答案 D（以上皆是）。→ [[pip-soft-tissue-swelling-ddx]]
