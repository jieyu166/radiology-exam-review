#交換 #2022交換 #MSK Which of the following is NOT a common location for Chondroblastoma?
(A) Proximal humerus
(B) Proximal femur
(C) Distal femur
(D) Proximal tibia
(E) Scapula (correct!)
??
**Ans: E**
Chondroblastoma 絕大多數起源於長骨骨骺/骨突（epiphysis 85%、apophysis 12%；長骨 75–80%），近端肱骨、近端與遠端股骨、近端脛骨為最常見部位。肩胛骨為扁平骨，起源罕見且多見於成人，故非常見部位，答案 E。→ [[chondroblastoma]]
