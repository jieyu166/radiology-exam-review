#交換 #2022交換 #ABD Benign prostatic hyperplasia classically develops from which of the following regions of the prostate?
(A) Central zone
(B) Transition zone
(C) Peripheral zone
(D) None of the above
??
**Ans: B**
良性攝護腺增生（BPH）典型起源於移行區（transition zone）與尿道周圍腺體，並壓迫周邊區與中央區；與前列腺癌好發之周邊區（約 70–75%）不同，故答案為移行區。→ [[prostate-zonal-anatomy]]
