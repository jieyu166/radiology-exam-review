#交換 #2022交換 #Breast What is the following about Medial Audit is INCORRECT?
(A) Regulation Mammography quality assurance program by Department of Health (DOH) in Taiwan
(B) PPV1 indicates positive cases at screening examination, includes recommendation of BI-RADS Category 0,4,5.
(C) PPV2 indicates recommendation for tissue diagnosis in Category 4,5.
(D) PPV3 indicates recommendation for biopsied tissue diagnosis (positive biopsy rate) in Category 4,5.
??
**Ans: B**
B 為不正確敘述。依 ACR BI-RADS 2013 醫學稽核定義，PPV1（abnormal findings at screening）的分母為所有篩檢陽性判讀，即 BI-RADS category 0、3、4、5——選項 B 只列「0、4、5」而漏掉 category 3（篩檢時給定的 category 3 亦屬陽性，其偽陽性計入 FP1），故敘述不完整而錯誤。A（台灣衛福部規範乳攝品保）、C（PPV2＝建議切片之 category 4、5）、D（PPV3＝實際做切片之 category 4、5，即陽性切片率/PBR）皆正確。→ [[mammography-medical-audit-ppv]]
