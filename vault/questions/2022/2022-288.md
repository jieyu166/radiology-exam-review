#交換 #2022交換 #CH Which of the following is **true** about lymphatic systems?
(A) In the lungs, lymphatics are usually present in the centrilobular and perihilar region.
(B) Both the peripheral and central lymphatics ducts cannot be imaged by using MRI.
(C) The thoracic duct is the largest lymphatics channel in the body, collecting lymph from everywhere in the body except the right upper hemithorax, right upper extremity, and right side of the neck
(D) Percutaneous lymphatic ducts intervention is not feasible.
??
**Ans: C**
正解 C。胸管（thoracic duct）為全身最大淋巴通道，起自 L1 附近的 cisterna chyli，收集全身淋巴，唯獨右上胸、右上肢、右側頭頸由 right lymphatic duct 引流，此為 RadioGraphics 2022 明列之 teaching point。A 錯：肺內淋巴管位於小葉間隔（interlobular septa）、胸膜下（subpleura）與支氣管血管束旁（bronchovascular），並非 centrilobular/perihilar，故淋巴病變表現為間隔與支氣管血管周圍間質增厚。B 錯：中央淋巴管可用 MR lymphangiography（肝內或結內注射）顯影，周邊淋巴管用 conventional lymphangiography，MRI 可影像化淋巴管。D 錯：thoracic duct embolization 等經皮淋巴管介入不僅可行，且為治療 chyle leak/chylothorax 的成熟術式。詳見 [[lymphatic-system-imaging]]
