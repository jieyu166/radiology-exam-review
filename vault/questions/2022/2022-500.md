#交換 #2022交換 #PED An umbilical venous catheter generally doses not passes through which vessels?
(A) umbilical vein
(B) internal iliac vein
(C) left portal vein
(D) middle or left hepatic vein
(E) inferior vena cava.
??
**Ans: B**
UVC 自臍靜脈直接上行，正常路徑 umbilical vein(A) → umbilical recess → left portal vein(C) → ductus venosus → middle/left hepatic vein(D) → IVC(E) → RA，tip 最佳在 IVC-RA junction。全程不經過 internal iliac vein——髂靜脈屬臍動脈導管(UAC)端的動脈側解剖，UAC 才向尾側下行入 internal iliac artery → common iliac artery → aorta。故「不會經過」者為 B。→ [[uvc-catheter-route]]
