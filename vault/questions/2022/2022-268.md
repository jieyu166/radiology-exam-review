#交換 #2022交換 #CH Which statement about septic emboli is not **true**:
(A) Chest radiograph is usually normal
(B) Chest CT may have tree in bud appearance.
(C) CTA may show filling defect at lobar , segmented pulmonary artery.
(D) Angiography is gold standard
??
**Ans: D**
答 D，「Angiography is gold standard」為錯誤敘述——敗血性肺栓塞的診斷主力是胸部 CT（結合多發周邊結節、空洞、feeding vessel 徵象與臨床菌血症），並非以（導管）血管攝影為黃金標準。其餘正確：胸部平片早期常可正常或僅見非特異結節（A 對）；CT 可因細支氣管內填充呈 tree-in-bud（B 對）；CTA 於栓子造成阻塞時可在肺葉／肺節動脈見充填缺損（C 對）。→ [[septic-pulmonary-emboli]]
