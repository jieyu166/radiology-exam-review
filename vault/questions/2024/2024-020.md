#交換 #2024交換 #MSK Regarding liposarcomas, which of the following is **NOT** true?
(A) Liposarcoma and leiomyosarcoma are the most common intra-abdominal soft-tissue sarcomas, and the extremity is their most common location.
(B) The WHO defines three distinct histologic patterns in well-differentiated liposarcoma: lipoma-like, sclerosing, and inflammatory
(C) Myxoid liposarcoma is extremely rare in the retroperitoneum and is usually metastatic in this location.
(D) When distant metastases occur, liposarcoma metastases most commonly arise in the lungs and liver
??
**Ans: A**
官方答案 A。官方詳解引 Levy 等人（RadioGraphics 2017）原文：liposarcoma 與 leiomyosarcoma 是腹內最常見的軟組織肉瘤，而它們最常見的位置是**後腹腔（retroperitoneum）**，不是四肢，故 A 錯。
(B) 正確（依官方答案）：WHO 將 well-differentiated liposarcoma 分為 lipoma-like、sclerosing、inflammatory 三種組織型態。
(C) 正確（依官方答案）：myxoid liposarcoma 極少原發於後腹腔，在此處出現通常是轉移。官方詳解另提醒：well-differentiated liposarcoma 可有黏液樣（myxoid）特徵，不要與 myxoid liposarcoma 混淆。
(D) 正確（依官方答案）：發生遠處轉移時，liposarcoma 最常轉移到肺與肝。
